The Honesty Ledger

A retraction here is a success of the rigour machinery, not an embarrassment: it is the record of a wrong claim being caught before anything was built on it.

This page is the complete list of claims this project made and then took back. Each entry is dated and says four things: what was claimed, why it was wrong, who or what caught it — with the error-catching layer it belongs to — and how it was resolved. No af-validated result was left resting on a retracted af certificate — each retraction records its cascade as closed; where a retraction removed an af-validated result, the campaign replay shows the corresponding fall in the T0 count. The layers themselves, and their measured catch record, are on the defense page.

Dead routes

A retraction removes a claim; a death certificate closes an approach. When a proof strategy is shown not to work — usually by an explicit counterexample — the reason is written down and dated so it is not quietly re-walked six weeks later. Those certificates live in FINDINGS.md, which is the working record: dozens of dated entries, each naming the mechanism that failed and the point of death. They are deliberately not parsed into this site's data layer — a death certificate is an argument, not a row, and it should be read where it was written.

The seven families below are the inherited ones, barred by name in the project's working rules (CLAUDE.md Rule 13). Attempting one again requires reading its certificate first and escalating — "but this time…" is exactly the failure mode the rule exists to stop.

barred — Rule 13; certificate in FINDINGS.md

Raw-index path-product floors

Refuted outright by the cloning obstruction for any δ0 ≥ 0.233: duplicating a state leaves the map invariant but breaks any raw-index floor. Only clone-invariant (quotient) quantities may appear in a proof.

barred — Rule 13; certificate in FINDINGS.md

Coefficient-only LP support-cleanup

The "pushed-witness death certificate" — cleaning up the support of an LP witness by coefficients alone does not survive.

barred — Rule 13; certificate in FINDINGS.md

Universal C ≤ 2

Refuted. (The companion refutation in the same certificate: C0 < 1 in (EX), where a transverse pair at a = ¼ gives Φ/δ = 5/4.)

barred — Rule 13; certificate in FINDINGS.md

Exists-exact-max-volume selectors

Exact maximum-volume selectors, and arbitrary max-volume tie-selection, fail.

barred — Rule 13; certificate in FINDINGS.md

Jensen / convexity

Recorded refuted upstream, alongside pointwise, σ-only and single-swap selectors.

barred — Rule 13; certificate in FINDINGS.md

The canonical-g energy method

Recorded refuted upstream; the energy bookkeeping does not close in the canonical gauge.

barred — Rule 13; certificate in FINDINGS.md

Finite-corner-as-asymptotic

Treating the finite corner as if it were the asymptotic regime — recorded refuted upstream, together with the literal ψ-gap.
