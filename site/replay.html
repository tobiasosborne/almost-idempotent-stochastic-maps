Campaign Replay

Every research turn of this project is logged, once, append-only, by the exploration controller: which direction was pulled, what came back, and what was decided next. Nothing is edited afterwards, so the record contains the reversals as well as the wins — the line below falls several times, and each fall is a result that had been banked as af-validated and was then taken back off the shelf. Those dips are the point: a curve that only goes up is a curve nobody was allowed to correct. The dated retractions behind them are written up in the honesty ledger and in docs/LEARNINGS.md.
