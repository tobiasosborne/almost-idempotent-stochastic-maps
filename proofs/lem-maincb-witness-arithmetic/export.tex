\step{1.2} Therefore the finite minimum r\_reset of the eleven reset candidates displayed in the contract is positive, finite, universal, independent of dimension, amplification, block data, class count, and stage index, and it has exactly the asserted formula. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]

\step{1.5} Define W to be the def-maincb-witness-ledger tuple (c0\_cb,L,K\_1,K\_2,K\_3,K\_call,e\_env,e\_1,e\_s2,e\_cross,r\_reset,epsilon\_MAIN).
