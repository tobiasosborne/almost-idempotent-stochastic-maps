## Node 1

**Statement:** Cohomological Kunneth isomorphism over R: for CW complexes X and Y with each H^k(Y;R) a finitely generated free R-module, the cross product H*(X;R) (x)_R H*(Y;R) -> H*(X x Y;R) is an isomorphism of rings; in particular this holds over the field R = reals for finite-CW spaces with finite-dimensional cohomology.

**Type:** claim

**Inference:** assumption

**Status:** validated

**Taint:** clean

### Node 1.1

**Statement:** By the allowed external GT-hatcher-AT-thm-3.15 (Hatcher, Theorem 3.15, refs/hatcher-algebraic-topology/AT.txt:13505-13506), for an arbitrary commutative coefficient ring R, if X and Y are CW complexes and H^k(Y;R) is a finitely generated free R-module for every k, then the cross product H*(X;R) (x)_R H*(Y;R) -> H*(X x Y;R) is an isomorphism of rings.

**Type:** claim

**Inference:** direct application of external GT-hatcher-AT-thm-3.15

**Status:** validated

**Taint:** clean

#### Node 1.1.1

**Statement:** Scope correction and application: in node 1.1 and in the general clause of root node 1, R ranges only over commutative coefficient rings, exactly the ambient class introduced in the source immediately before GT-hatcher-AT-thm-3.15; no assertion is made for noncommutative rings. With this restriction, the hypotheses that X and Y are CW complexes and every H^k(Y;R) is finitely generated free are precisely those of GT-hatcher-AT-thm-3.15, whose conclusion is the displayed cross-product ring isomorphism. The later specialization remains valid because the real numbers form a commutative field.

**Type:** claim

**Inference:** assumption

**Status:** validated

**Taint:** clean

### Node 1.2

**Statement:** In the stated special case R = real numbers, with X and Y finite-CW spaces and H^k(Y;R) finite-dimensional for every k, X and Y are CW complexes and each H^k(Y;R) is a finitely generated free R-module.

**Type:** claim

**Inference:** elementary unpacking of finite-CW and finite-dimensional vector space

**Status:** validated

**Taint:** clean

#### Node 1.2.1

**Statement:** By definition, a finite-CW space is a CW complex having finitely many cells; hence the assumed finite-CW spaces X and Y satisfy the CW-complex hypothesis of GT-hatcher-AT-thm-3.15.

**Type:** claim

**Inference:** by_definition

**Status:** validated

**Taint:** clean

#### Node 1.2.2

**Statement:** For every k, finite-dimensionality of the real vector space H^k(Y;real numbers) means it has a finite basis. The same basis makes H^k(Y;real numbers), viewed as a module over the field of real numbers, free and generated by finitely many elements. Thus it is a finitely generated free real module for every k.

**Type:** claim

**Inference:** by_definition

**Status:** validated
