With the fixed positive finite e\_cross\textasciicircum{}0 and e\_env\textasciicircum{}0, K\_3\textasciicircum{}0=max\{1,e\_cross\textasciicircum{}0/e\_env\textasciicircum{}0\} is finite and universal, is at least 1, and satisfies e\_cross\textasciicircum{}0/K\_3\textasciicircum{}0<=e\_env\textasciicircum{}0. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
