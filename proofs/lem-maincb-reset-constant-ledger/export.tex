\step{1.2} Set D\_*:=max\{1,D\_0,D\_1,D\_2,D\_3\}. Apply lem-maincb-witness-arithmetic to the fixed witnesses and the receiving choices of the preceding step. \step{1.3} The exact field identities furnished by lem-maincb-witness-arithmetic imply W.L>=L\textasciicircum{}0, W.K1>=K\_1\textasciicircum{}0, W.K2>=max\{K\_2\textasciicircum{}0,1,W.L,W.c0\_cb*W.L\}, W.K3>=max\{K\_3\textasciicircum{}0,1,W.L,W.c0\_cb*W.L\}, W.e\_env<=e\_env\textasciicircum{}0, W.e1<=e\_1, W.e\_s2<=min\{e\_s2\textasciicircum{}0,e\_2\}, and W.e\_cross<=min\{e\_cross\textasciicircum{}0,e\_3\}.
