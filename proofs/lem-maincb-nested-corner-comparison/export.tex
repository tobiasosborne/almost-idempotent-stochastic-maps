\begin{proofbox}{There are universal C\_nest < infinity and e\_nest > 0 such...}
\step{1} There are universal C\_nest < infinity and e\_nest > 0 such that, whenever R,P,Q are t-projections in a finite-dimensional extended t-C*-algebra, R is nonvanishing, P,Q are subordinate to R with all four left/right subordination errors at most t <= e\_nest, A\_R = S\textasciicircum{}A\_R, P\textasciicircum{}R = Co\textasciicircum{}A\_R(P), and Q\textasciicircum{}R = Co\textasciicircum{}A\_R(Q), then P\textasciicircum{}R,Q\textasciicircum{}R are C\_nest*t-projections in A\_R and, at every amplification, ||F\textasciicircum{}R\_\{P,Q\}(Co\textasciicircum{}A\_R X) - X|| <= C\_nest*t*||X|| for X in S\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P,Q\}, while ||Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P,Q\} Y - Y|| <= C\_nest*t*||Y|| for Y in S\textasciicircum{}\{A\_R\}\_\{P\textasciicircum{}R,Q\textasciicircum{}R\}. \steptag{validated} \\[6pt]
\step{1.1} Fix universal constants K\_def,e\_def witnessing the O(delta+epsilon) compression-to-raw-product bounds in def-compressed-corner, and the constants and thresholds in the five named externals. After shrinking a universal e\_nest, every t-projection used below, at every amplification, has uniformly bounded norm; moreover P\textasciicircum{}R=Co\textasciicircum{}A\_R(P) and Q\textasciicircum{}R=Co\textasciicircum{}A\_R(Q) differ from P,Q by O(t), with universal dimension-free coefficients. \steptag{validated} \\[6pt]
\step{1.1.1} Uniform norm bound: if T is any t-projection in the extended t-C*-algebra and t<=1/4, the C*-lower bound and product-norm axiom give (1-t)||T||\textasciicircum{}2 <= ||T\textasciicircum{}2|| <= ||T||+t, hence ||T||<=2. By the block-diagonal operator-space axiom, ||I\_n tensor T||=||T|| for every n. Thus R,P,Q and their amplifications have norm at most 2, and every factor occurring in the fixed telescopes is uniformly bounded after the O(t) perturbations below. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.1.2} Outer-compression bound: let K\_def,e\_def be universal witnesses for the compression-to-each-raw-map O(delta+epsilon) estimate in def-compressed-corner. Since ||PR-P||,||RP-P||<=t, product-norm and triangle inequalities give ||R(PR)-P||<=t+(1+t)||R||t and ||(RP)R-P||<=t+(1+t)||R||t; hence, for 2t<=e\_def, ||P\textasciicircum{}R-P||<=K\_P*t, universally. The identical argument gives ||Q\textasciicircum{}R-Q||<=K\_P*t. By lem-compcb-amplified-compression these same bounds hold for I\_n tensor P\textasciicircum{}R versus I\_n tensor P and for Q at every n, with no n-dependent coefficient. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.2} The external lem-compcb-corner-algebra makes A\_R=S\textasciicircum{}A\_R an extended O(t)-C*-algebra. In that algebra P\textasciicircum{}R and Q\textasciicircum{}R are Hermitian and have compressed-product idempotence defects O(t), hence are C\_proj*t-projections for a universal C\_proj. \steptag{validated} \\[6pt]
\step{1.2.1} Apply lem-compcb-corner-algebra to the nonvanishing t-projection R in the extended t-C*-algebra (so delta+epsilon=2t): for 2t<=e\_ca, A\_R=S\textasciicircum{}A\_R with compressed product a dot\_R b=Co\textasciicircum{}A\_R(ab), inherited dagger, and compressed unit is an extended 2*C\_ca*t-C*-algebra. By lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities, Co\_R is idempotent and dagger-preserving on the diagonal; since P,Q are Hermitian, P\textasciicircum{}R=Co\_R(P) and Q\textasciicircum{}R=Co\_R(Q) are Hermitian elements of A\_R. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.2.2} Let a=P\textasciicircum{}R and p=P. Bilinearity gives a\textasciicircum{}2-p\textasciicircum{}2=(a-p)a+p(a-p), so node 1.1's uniform bounds imply ||a\textasciicircum{}2-a||<=((1+t)(||a||+||p||)K\_P+1+K\_P)t=K\_0*t. Because a lies in range(Co\_R), Co\_R(a)=a; and def-compressed-corner plus the uniform bound on R makes ||Co\_R|| universally bounded. Therefore ||a dot\_R a-a||=||Co\_R(a\textasciicircum{}2-a)||<=K\_1*t. The same proof applies to Q\textasciicircum{}R. Together with the preceding exact Hermitian identities, P\textasciicircum{}R,Q\textasciicircum{}R are C\_proj*t-projections in A\_R for a universal C\_proj. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.3} For every n and X in M\_n tensor S\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P,Q\}, amplified almost-containment gives Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{R\_n\}(X)=X+O(t)||X||; expanding F\textasciicircum{}R\_\{P,Q\}=Co\textasciicircum{}\{A\_R\}\_\{P\textasciicircum{}R,Q\textasciicircum{}R\}, replacing its two internal products by ambient products, and replacing P\textasciicircum{}R,Q\textasciicircum{}R,Co\_R X by P,Q,X gives ||F\textasciicircum{}R\_\{P,Q\}(Co\textasciicircum{}A\_R X)-X|| <= C\_fwd*t*||X|| with a universal coefficient independent of n. \steptag{validated} \\[6pt]
\step{1.3.1} Fix n, put R\_n=I\_n tensor R, P\_n=I\_n tensor P, Q\_n=I\_n tensor Q, and let X lie in M\_n tensor S\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P,Q\}. In lem-compcb-amplified-almost-containment take the projection-defect and algebra parameters both equal to t and instantiate (P\_1,P,Q\_1,Q)=(P,R,Q,R). The hypotheses are exactly ||PR-P||<=t and ||QR-Q||<=t; hence, whenever 2t<=e\_ac, Z:=Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{R\_n\}(X) satisfies ||Z-X||<=2*C\_ac*t||X|| and therefore ||Z||<=(1+2*C\_ac*t)||X||. Lem-compcb-amplified-compression gives Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{R\_n\}=I\_n tensor Co\textasciicircum{}A\_R and M\_n tensor S\textasciicircum{}A\_R=S\textasciicircum{}A\_\{R\_n\}, so Z=(I\_n tensor Co\textasciicircum{}A\_R)X lies in M\_n tensor A\_R. No estimate for P\textasciicircum{}R-P or Q\textasciicircum{}R-Q is asserted or used in this node. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.3.2} In the extended 2*C\_ca*t-C*-algebra A\_R, node 1.2 makes P\textasciicircum{}R,Q\textasciicircum{}R C\_proj*t-projections. Thus def-compressed-corner, at level n using lem-compcb-amplified-compression and lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities, makes F\_n=Co\textasciicircum{}\{A\_R\}\_\{P\textasciicircum{}R\_n,Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n\} differ by at most K\_def*(C\_proj+2*C\_ca)t from each raw internal map Z maps to P\textasciicircum{}R\_n dot\_R (Z dot\_R Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n) and Z maps to (P\textasciicircum{}R\_n dot\_R Z) dot\_R Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n. Twice applying lem-compcb-rectangular-product to elements of the amplified R-corner replaces the two dot\_R products by the correspondingly parenthesized ambient A-products with O(t)||X|| error. Bilinearity, the product-norm bound, node 1.1's ||P\textasciicircum{}R\_n-P\_n||,||Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n-Q\_n||=O(t), and ||Z-X||=O(t)||X|| then give ||F\_n(Z)-P\_n(XQ\_n)||<=K\_tel*t||X||. Finally X=Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P\_n,Q\_n\}X by the amplified range/idempotence identities, and def-compressed-corner gives ||P\_n(XQ\_n)-Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P\_n,Q\_n\}X||<=2*K\_def*t||X||. Hence ||F\_n(Z)-X||<=C\_fwd*t||X||, uniformly in n. \steptag{validated} \\[6pt]
\step{1.3.2.1} Validated inputs and the missing Z estimate: by validated nodes 1.1.1 and 1.1.2, for every n one has ||P\_n||,||Q\_n||<=2 and ||P\textasciicircum{}R\_n-P\_n||,||Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n-Q\_n||<=K\_P*t; hence ||P\textasciicircum{}R\_n||,||Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n||<=2+K\_P*t. By validated nodes 1.2.1 and 1.2.2, A\_R is an extended 2*C\_ca*t-C*-algebra and P\textasciicircum{}R,Q\textasciicircum{}R are C\_proj*t-projections in it. Independently of pending sibling 1.3.1, apply lem-compcb-amplified-almost-containment in A with (P\_1,P,Q\_1,Q)=(P,R,Q,R): the hypotheses ||PR-P||,||QR-Q||<=t are among the assumed subordination errors, so when 2t<=e\_ac, Z=Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{R\_n\}(X) satisfies ||Z-X||<=2*C\_ac*t||X|| and therefore ||Z||<=(1+2*C\_ac*t)||X||. Lem-compcb-amplified-compression identifies Z as an element of M\_n tensor A\_R. These are dimension-free estimates and use only validated nodes plus an allowed external. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.3.2.2} Use only the preceding validated-input package and the allowed externals. Write d=C\_proj and alpha=2*C\_ca. In the extended alpha*t-C*-algebra A\_R, def-compressed-corner at level n (with lem-compcb-amplified-compression for the amplification) gives ||F\_n(Z)-P\textasciicircum{}R\_n dot\_R (Z dot\_R Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n)||<=K\_def*(d+alpha)*t*||Z|| once (d+alpha)t is below its universal threshold. Put W=Z dot\_R Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n. Applying lem-compcb-rectangular-product in the original amplified R-corner first to (Z,Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n) and then to (P\textasciicircum{}R\_n,W) gives ||W-ZQ\textasciicircum{}R\_n||<=2*C\_co*t||Z||||Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n|| and ||P\textasciicircum{}R\_n dot\_R W-P\textasciicircum{}R\_n W||<=2*C\_co*t||P\textasciicircum{}R\_n||||W||. The product-norm axiom also gives ||P\textasciicircum{}R\_n W-P\textasciicircum{}R\_n(ZQ\textasciicircum{}R\_n)||<=(1+t)||P\textasciicircum{}R\_n||||W-ZQ\textasciicircum{}R\_n||, while the A\_R product-norm bound gives ||W||<=(1+alpha*t)||Z||||Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n||. The bounds in the preceding child therefore yield ||F\_n(Z)-P\textasciicircum{}R\_n(ZQ\textasciicircum{}R\_n)||<=K\_dot*t||X|| with universal K\_dot. Exact bilinearity, with the displayed parenthesization fixed, gives P\textasciicircum{}R\_n(ZQ\textasciicircum{}R\_n)-P\_n(XQ\_n)=(P\textasciicircum{}R\_n-P\_n)(ZQ\textasciicircum{}R\_n)+P\_n((Z-X)Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n)+P\_n(X(Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n-Q\_n)); two uses of ||UV||<=(1+t)||U||||V|| and the preceding bounds give norm at most K\_pert*t||X||. Finally X lies in M\_n tensor S\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P,Q\}=S\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P\_n,Q\_n\}; lem-compcb-amplified-compression and lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities imply Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P\_n,Q\_n\}(X)=X. The ambient def-compressed-corner comparison gives ||P\_n(XQ\_n)-X||<=2*K\_def*t||X||. Hence ||F\_n(Z)-X||<=(K\_dot+K\_pert+2*K\_def)t||X||, uniformly in n, after taking the universal threshold minimum. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.4} For every n and Y in M\_n tensor S\textasciicircum{}\{A\_R\}\_\{P\textasciicircum{}R,Q\textasciicircum{}R\}, the exact range identity Y=F\textasciicircum{}R\_\{P,Q\}(Y), followed by the same fixed internal-product telescope in reverse and the ambient compression-to-raw-product estimate, gives ||Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P,Q\}(Y)-Y|| <= C\_rev*t*||Y|| with a universal coefficient independent of n. Taking C\_nest=max(C\_proj,C\_fwd,C\_rev) and e\_nest no larger than all thresholds proves node 1. \steptag{validated} \\[6pt]
\step{1.4.1} Fix n and Y in M\_n tensor S\textasciicircum{}\{A\_R\}\_\{P\textasciicircum{}R,Q\textasciicircum{}R\}. By lem-compcb-amplified-compression and lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities inside A\_R, this space is the range of the idempotent F\_n=Co\textasciicircum{}\{A\_R\}\_\{P\textasciicircum{}R\_n,Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n\}, so F\_n(Y)=Y. The def-compressed-corner comparison in A\_R therefore gives ||Y-P\textasciicircum{}R\_n dot\_R (Y dot\_R Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n)||<=K\_def*(C\_proj+2*C\_ca)t||Y|| (and likewise for the other association), after the universal threshold is imposed. \steptag{validated} \\[6pt]
\step{1.4.1.1} Set a=P\textasciicircum{}R=Co\textasciicircum{}A\_R(P), b=Q\textasciicircum{}R=Co\textasciicircum{}A\_R(Q), and let K\_def,e\_def witness the universal def-compressed-corner bound ||Co\_\{U,V\}-L\_U R\_V||,||Co\_\{U,V\}-R\_V L\_U|| <= K\_def*(delta+epsilon). This branch derives its own projection parameter. For t<=1/4, the epsilon-C*-lower and product-norm axioms give ||R||,||P||,||Q||<=2. Subordination gives ||R(PR)-P||<=||R(PR-P)||+||RP-P||<=4t, and similarly for the other raw association and for Q. Hence the compression comparison for the t-projection R in the ambient t-C*-algebra gives ||a-P||,||b-Q||<=K\_P*t with K\_P=4*K\_def+4. Shrink t so K\_P*t<=1 and 2*K\_def*t<=1. The same comparison with the raw map X↦R(XR) then gives a universal operator bound ||Co\_R||<=8. The external lem-compcb-corner-algebra makes A\_R an extended 2*C\_ca*t-C*-algebra. The amplified adjoint compression identity at n=1 makes a,b Hermitian. Moreover, with K\_0=8*K\_P+1, bilinearity and the product-norm axiom give ||a\textasciicircum{}2-a||<=K\_0*t and ||b\textasciicircum{}2-b||<=K\_0*t; since Co\_R(a)=a and Co\_R(b)=b, one has ||a dot\_R a-a||=||Co\_R(a\textasciicircum{}2-a)||<=8*K\_0*t and likewise for b. Thus, with C\_proj=8*K\_0, a,b are C\_proj*t-projections in A\_R, derived here without node 1.2. At level n, a\_n=I\_n tensor a and b\_n=I\_n tensor b have the same defects because the extended product is amplified entrywise and the operator-space block-diagonal axiom gives ||I\_n tensor T||=||T||. Impose also (C\_proj+2*C\_ca)*t<=min(e\_def,e\_cmp). Then lem-compcb-amplified-compression identifies M\_n tensor S\textasciicircum{}\{A\_R\}\_\{a,b\} with S\textasciicircum{}\{A\_R\}\_\{a\_n,b\_n\}, and lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities makes F\_n=Co\textasciicircum{}\{A\_R\}\_\{a\_n,b\_n\} idempotent. Therefore Y in that space satisfies F\_n(Y)=Y, while def-compressed-corner inside A\_R yields ||Y-a\_n dot\_R (Y dot\_R b\_n)||<=K\_def*(C\_proj+2*C\_ca)*t*||Y|| and the analogous bound for (a\_n dot\_R Y) dot\_R b\_n. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.4.2} All factors in the preceding internal products lie in M\_n tensor A\_R=M\_n tensor S\_R. Two applications of lem-compcb-rectangular-product with the compatible R\_n-corners replace dot\_R by the ambient A product with O(t)||Y|| error. Node 1.1 then replaces P\textasciicircum{}R\_n,Q\textasciicircum{}R\_n by P\_n,Q\_n, giving ||Y-P\_n(YQ\_n)||<=K'\_tel*t||Y||. In the original ambient algebra, def-compressed-corner gives ||Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P\_n,Q\_n\}(Y)-P\_n(YQ\_n)||<=2*K\_def*t||Y||; hence ||Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P,Q\}Y-Y||<=C\_rev*t||Y|| under the amplified identification, uniformly in n. The constants are universal and taking the stated maximum and threshold minimum completes node 1.4. \steptag{validated} \\[6pt]
\step{1.4.2.1} Write a=P\textasciicircum{}R=Co\_R(P), b=Q\textasciicircum{}R=Co\_R(Q), and a\_n=I\_n tensor a, b\_n=I\_n tensor b. This step derives, without using nodes 1.1, 1.2, or 1.4.1, universal K\_pert,K\_raw and a universal positive threshold such that for every n and Y in M\_n tensor S\textasciicircum{}\{A\_R\}\_\{a,b\}, ||a\_n-P\_n||+||b\_n-Q\_n||<=K\_pert*t and ||Y-a\_n dot\_R (Y dot\_R b\_n)||<=K\_raw*t*||Y||. Indeed, for t<=1/4, the C*-lower bound and t-projection defect give (1-t)||T||\textasciicircum{}2<=||T\textasciicircum{}2||<=||T||+t, hence ||R||,||P||,||Q||<=2. If K\_def witnesses the compression-to-raw-map estimate in def-compressed-corner, then ||R(PR)-P||<=||R(PR-P)||+||RP-P||<=2(1+t)t+t and ||Co\_R(P)-R(PR)||<=2*K\_def*t||P||; thus ||a-P||<=K\_0*t, and identically ||b-Q||<=K\_0*t. The block-diagonal operator-space axiom gives the same estimates for a\_n-P\_n and b\_n-Q\_n. By lem-compcb-corner-algebra, A\_R is an extended 2*C\_ca*t-C*-algebra. By lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities, a and b are Hermitian. Also Co\_R has universally bounded operator norm because it is within 2*K\_def*t of X maps to R(XR), while ||R||<=2. Bilinearity and the product-norm axiom give ||a\textasciicircum{}2-a||<= (1+t)||a-P||(||a||+||P||)+||P\textasciicircum{}2-P||+||P-a||<=K\_1*t. Since Co\_R(a)=a by idempotence, ||a dot\_R a-a||=||Co\_R(a\textasciicircum{}2-a)||<=K\_2*t; the same holds for b. Hence a,b are K\_2*t-projections in A\_R. After shrinking t so (K\_2+2*C\_ca)t meets the amplified compression thresholds, lem-compcb-amplified-compression and lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities identify M\_n tensor S\textasciicircum{}\{A\_R\}\_\{a,b\} with the range of the idempotent F\_n=Co\textasciicircum{}\{A\_R\}\_\{a\_n,b\_n\}; therefore F\_n(Y)=Y. Applying the compression-to-first-raw-map estimate in the extended algebra A\_R gives ||Y-a\_n dot\_R(Y dot\_R b\_n)||<=K\_def*(K\_2+2*C\_ca)*t||Y||. All displayed constants are universal and independent of n. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.4.2.2} Use the two estimates just established to complete the reverse telescope, with no appeal to nodes 1.1 or 1.4.1. Both a\_n,b\_n,Y and Y dot\_R b\_n lie in compatible amplified R\_n-corners. Lem-compcb-rectangular-product, applied first to (Y,b\_n) and then to (a\_n,Y dot\_R b\_n), gives ||Y dot\_R b\_n-Yb\_n||<=2*C\_co*t||Y||||b\_n|| and ||a\_n dot\_R(Y dot\_R b\_n)-a\_n(Y dot\_R b\_n)||<=2*C\_co*t||a\_n||||Y dot\_R b\_n||. The preceding perturbation bounds and ||P\_n||,||Q\_n||<=2 make ||a\_n||,||b\_n|| universal; the first displayed estimate then also makes ||Y dot\_R b\_n||<=B||Y|| universally. Consequently ||a\_n dot\_R(Y dot\_R b\_n)-a\_n(Yb\_n)||<=K\_3*t||Y||. Exact bilinearity yields a\_n(Yb\_n)-P\_n(YQ\_n)=(a\_n-P\_n)(Yb\_n)+P\_n(Y(b\_n-Q\_n)), so the product-norm axiom and the perturbation bounds give ||a\_n(Yb\_n)-P\_n(YQ\_n)||<=K\_4*t||Y||. Combining with ||Y-a\_n dot\_R(Y dot\_R b\_n)||<=K\_raw*t||Y|| gives ||Y-P\_n(YQ\_n)||<=(K\_raw+K\_3+K\_4)t||Y||. Finally def-compressed-corner in M\_n tensor A gives ||Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P\_n,Q\_n\}(Y)-P\_n(YQ\_n)||<=2*K\_def*t||Y||. By lem-compcb-amplified-compression, Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P\_n,Q\_n\}=I\_n tensor Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P,Q\}; hence ||Co\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P,Q\}(Y)-Y||<=C\_rev*t||Y|| at every amplification, where C\_rev=K\_raw+K\_3+K\_4+2*K\_def is universal. Taking the threshold minimum and the maximum with the other universal coefficients is exactly the conclusion of nodes 1.4.2 and 1.4. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\end{proofbox}
