\step{1} After first choosing a universal c0 witness for which lem-maincb-error-improvement remains valid, fixing the corresponding L\textasciicircum{}0,e\_env\textasciicircum{}0 witnesses of lem-maincb-direct-corner-envelope, and fixing the C\_s2\textasciicircum{}0,e\_s2\textasciicircum{}0 witnesses of lem-maincb-stage2-extcb-datum, there is a universal K\_2\textasciicircum{}0 >= 1 with every Stage-2 prerequisite absorbed such that for every def-maincb-witness-ledger datum W with W.c0\_cb = c0, W.L >= L\textasciicircum{}0, W.K2 >= max\{K\_2\textasciicircum{}0,1,W.L,W.c0\_cb*W.L\}, and W.e\_s2 <= e\_s2\textasciicircum{}0, if A is a finite-dimensional extended epsilon-C*-algebra, w:C\textasciicircum{}m->A is an extended W.c0\_cb*epsilon-inclusion satisfying ||w(I\_\{C\textasciicircum{}m\})-I\_A|| <= W.c0\_cb*epsilon with one-dimensional atomic images, a supplied MAIN partition state for A,w has nonempty U contained in one equivalence class, j notin U in that same class, and R=U union \{j\}, 0 <= epsilon <= W.e\_s2/W.K2, and a supplied current reset isomorphism v\_U:M\_\{|U$|\}-\rangle$A\_U has recorded ambient field epsilon\_U <= W.L*epsilon and satisfies d\_U <= W.c0\_cb*epsilon\_U and ||v\_U(I\_\{M\_\{|U|\}\})-u\_\{A\_U\}|| <= W.c0\_cb*epsilon\_U, then lem-maincb-direct-corner-envelope certifies A\_R with the Stage-2 raw-call target ambient record epsilon\_R := W.L*epsilon, and t\_2=W.K2*epsilon dominates epsilon\_U,d\_U,epsilon\_R, the reset unit error, and every other datum error, so lem-maincb-stage2-extcb-datum furnishes the explicit Stage-2 EXT raw-call datum with total defect at most C\_s2\textasciicircum{}0*t\_2. \steptag{validated} \\[6pt]
\step{1.1} Choose the universal c0 furnished by lem-maincb-error-improvement, then fix the corresponding witnesses L\textasciicircum{}0>=1 and e\_env\textasciicircum{}0>0 from lem-maincb-direct-corner-envelope and C\_s2\textasciicircum{}0>=1 and e\_s2\textasciicircum{}0>0 from lem-maincb-stage2-extcb-datum. Define K\_2\textasciicircum{}0:=max\{1,e\_s2\textasciicircum{}0/e\_env\textasciicircum{}0\}; this is universal, finite, at least 1, and satisfies e\_s2\textasciicircum{}0/K\_2\textasciicircum{}0<=e\_env\textasciicircum{}0. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.2} For arbitrary W and data satisfying the root hypotheses, put t\_2:=W.K2*epsilon. Then 0<=t\_2<=W.e\_s2<=e\_s2\textasciicircum{}0, epsilon<=e\_env\textasciicircum{}0, and each of epsilon, W.L*epsilon, and W.c0\_cb*W.L*epsilon is at most t\_2. \steptag{validated} \\[6pt]
\step{1.2.1} Because W.K2>=1 and epsilon>=0, t\_2=W.K2*epsilon is nonnegative; multiplying epsilon<=W.e\_s2/W.K2 by the positive W.K2 gives t\_2<=W.e\_s2<=e\_s2\textasciicircum{}0. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.2.2} Using W.K2>=K\_2\textasciicircum{}0, W.e\_s2<=e\_s2\textasciicircum{}0, and K\_2\textasciicircum{}0=max\{1,e\_s2\textasciicircum{}0/e\_env\textasciicircum{}0\}, the same upper bound gives epsilon<=W.e\_s2/W.K2<=e\_s2\textasciicircum{}0/K\_2\textasciicircum{}0<=e\_env\textasciicircum{}0. Multiplying W.K2>=1, W.K2>=W.L, and W.K2>=W.c0\_cb*W.L by epsilon>=0 gives respectively epsilon<=t\_2, W.L*epsilon<=t\_2, and W.c0\_cb*W.L*epsilon<=t\_2. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.3} The hypotheses of lem-maincb-direct-corner-envelope hold for the displayed A,w and the nonempty sets U and R. Applying it gives A\_R as an extended L\textasciicircum{}0*epsilon-C*-algebra; P\_U, P\_\{R minus U\}=P\_j, and P\_R as c0*epsilon-projections; and every stated subordination and complementarity error among them at most L\textasciicircum{}0*epsilon. Since W.L>=L\textasciicircum{}0, record the monotone upper ambient parameter epsilon\_R:=W.L*epsilon: A\_R is an extended epsilon\_R-C*-algebra and the subordination and complementarity errors are at most epsilon\_R<=t\_2. The projection defects are not asserted to be at most epsilon\_R; instead they are bounded separately by c0*epsilon<=W.c0\_cb*W.L*epsilon<=t\_2. Thus every direct-corner datum error required for the Stage-2 call is at most t\_2. \steptag{validated} \\[6pt]
\step{1.3.1} For the separate projection bound, c0=W.c0\_cb and the projection-defect parameter c0*epsilon is nonnegative. Because W.L>=L\textasciicircum{}0>=1 and epsilon>=0, c0*epsilon<=W.c0\_cb*W.L*epsilon; node 1.2 gives W.c0\_cb*W.L*epsilon<=t\_2. In contrast, the L\textasciicircum{}0*epsilon envelope applies only to the ambient, subordination, and complementarity errors, for which L\textasciicircum{}0*epsilon<=W.L*epsilon=epsilon\_R<=t\_2. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.4} The supplied reset and incoming map meet all analytic input bounds for lem-maincb-stage2-extcb-datum at t=t\_2: epsilon\_U<=W.L*epsilon<=t\_2; d\_U<=W.c0\_cb*epsilon\_U<=W.c0\_cb*W.L*epsilon<=t\_2; the reset unit error obeys the same bound and is carried as an invariant (not used to manufacture any EXT clause); A has epsilon<=t\_2; and w, originally an extended W.c0\_cb*epsilon-inclusion, is also a non-unital extended t\_2-inclusion because W.c0\_cb*epsilon<=t\_2. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.5} The partition geometry meets the remaining hypotheses of lem-maincb-stage2-extcb-datum: U is nonempty, j is not in U, R=U union \{j\}, all atomic images P\_k are one-dimensional, and because U and j lie in the same equivalence class, def-maincb-partition-state gives dim S\textasciicircum{}A\_\{P\_k,P\_j\}=1 for every k in U, hence in particular the required cross-corner is nonzero. \steptag{validated} \\[4pt]
\step{1.6} Apply lem-maincb-stage2-extcb-datum to the original ambient A, the partition state, w, U,j,R, and reset v\_U with t=t\_2. The preceding bounds give epsilon\_U,d\_U<=t\_2<=e\_s2\textasciicircum{}0 and d\_U<=c0*epsilon\_U, while the geometry gives every remaining premise. \step{1.6.1} By nodes 1.1-1.5, every hypothesis of lem-maincb-stage2-extcb-datum holds with its ambient variable equal to the original A and t=t\_2: smallness and domination are supplied by 1.2 and 1.4, the required partition and corner dimensions by 1.5, and the selected A\_R ambient record plus all direct-corner errors by 1.3. \step{1.6.1.1} Keep the two ambient-defect records distinct.
