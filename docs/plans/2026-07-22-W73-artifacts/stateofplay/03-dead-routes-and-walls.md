## 3. Recurring structural obstructions

**Cloning obstruction.** State-duplication invariance breaks any raw-index quantity. Only clone-invariant quotient quantities admissible.

**One-sided ledger.** Financing/budget arguments supply a bound in one direction only; apparent dodges (CAP-1/2, (SI)→(RSI), orphan-financing) transfer the burden into a new residual rather than eliminating it.

**Anti-splitting / quotient-packing (class-count wall, "dead route c10").** Composed caps summing surviving mass over separating directions need a dimension-free bound on geometrically distinct genuine-outside quotient classes — exactly the killed conjecture. Aggregate/global (dual-certificate, clone-quotient measure-compression) proofs dodge it; per-class/per-wedge/per-block proofs reimport it.

**Exposedness absorption (σ_g wall).** Pushing mass toward the LP-relaxation optimum makes the exact-geometry optimizer's recipients become visible (`W` grows, `H→0`) before the cap is threatened. The true content of most caps is cluster-to-EXPOSEDNESS, not cluster-to-proximity (W35).

**ρ-halo exemption.** Absorption is driven by a ratio of affine-circuit coefficients (negative anchors vs far positive support) crossing a threshold when a heavy circuit partner enters the ρ-ball. The "bridge" gap (shipped row mass vs affine-circuit/witness mass) recurs W37–W43 as the single terminal difficulty of Route A.

**Dual-direction wall.** Every feasible circuit only *upper*-bounds hiddenness (`t*`); hiddenness cannot be run backward through upper bounds. A primal (not dual) exposer-construction mechanism is required.

**Tightness-promotion wall / whole-optimal-face membership.** Near-zero LP-dual values certify nothing; membership requires always-tightness on the *entire* optimal face (W42/W43 hard stop). Every other Route-A thread provably reduces to this one unresolved statement.

**Exposer value-normalization ≠ Lipschitz control.** `def-exposed` normalizes by value; 4τ-close rows need not have comparable exposedness LPs.
