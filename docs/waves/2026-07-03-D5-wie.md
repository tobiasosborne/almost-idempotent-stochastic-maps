## T1-PROVED-INLINE. Exact Stationarity Ledger

Beta stationarity is the pivot row of `P^2=P`:

```text
beta_l = sum_j beta_j P_jl.                              (S)
```

Split (S) into the four source groups `D`, `L`, `N`, and the complement
`K={classes/T rows}`. Define

```text
N^D_l = sum_{j in D} beta_j P_jl^-,
L_l   = sum_{j in L} beta_j P_jl,
N_l   = sum_{j in N} beta_j P_jl,
C_l   = sum_{h in K} beta_h P_hl.
```

Since `sum_{j in D} beta_j P_jl = A_l - N^D_l`, (S) gives the exact identity

```text
A_l - beta_l^+
  = N^D_l - beta_l^- - L_l - N_l - C_l.                  (1)
```

This is the requested financing ledger. The class term is not abstract:
if `C_r` is an H-M clone class and `Gamma_r=sum_{h in C_r} beta_h`, then
class rows have identical row `P_{u_r,*}`, so

```text
C_l = sum_r Gamma_r P_{u_r,l}       plus zero-column/T rows, if any.        (2)
```

Thus class rows can finance B-import excess through their B columns. The D4
refuter is exactly this: the pivot-class aggregate is negative and the pivot
row has positive mass on the two B columns.

Taking positive parts in (1) gives the one-sided estimate

```text
(A_l - beta_l^+)_+
 <= N^D_l + (-L_l)_+ + (-N_l)_+ + (-C_l)_+.               (3)
```

Term meanings:

- `N^D_l`: own negative entries of D rows flowing into column `l`; unweighted,
  this is part of `R_D^nu`, but after multiplying by `mu_l` it still needs an
  import-target bound.
- `(-C_l)_+`: signed class aggregate financing. Negative `Gamma_r` times
  positive `P_{u_r,l}`, or positive `Gamma_r` times negative `P_{u_r,l}`, can
  create demand. The raw budget `G_class^-` sees `Gamma_r^-`, not the imported
  target mass `mu_l`.
- `(-N_l)_+`: beta-negative B rows. Positive entries of a beta-negative row
  create demand; negative entries are credit. The available budget `S_-^mu`
  controls `|beta_j| mu_j`, not automatically
  `|beta_j| sum_l P_jl^+ mu_l`.
- `(-L_l)_+`: legal positive B rows. Positive legal mass over-finances, but
  negative legal entries create demand. This term has no home in
  `G_class^- + S_-^mu + R_D^nu` unless a legal-horn estimate is supplied.

## T1. Why WIE Still Does Not Close RSI

D4's import decomposition gives, for `j in D`,

```text
mu_j <= sum_{l in B} P_jl^+ mu_l + own-source_j.
```

Multiplying by `beta_j` and summing over `D`,

```text
M_D <= sum_{l in B} A_l mu_l + Own_D.                    (4)
```

WIE controls only the positive excess of `A_l` over `beta_l^+`. It does not
remove the baseline positive-beta mass on legal B rows. Indeed

```text
sum_l A_l mu_l
 <= sum_l beta_l^+ mu_l + W
 = M_D + L_mu + W,                                      (5)

L_mu := sum_{l in B\D, beta_l>0} beta_l mu_l.
```

Substituting (5) into (4) gives

```text
M_D <= M_D + L_mu + W + Own_D,
```

which is not an upper bound for `M_D`. If `L_mu>0`, the legal horn has leaked
into the source inequality. Even if `L_mu=0`, throwing away the negative part
of `A_l-beta_l^+` loses the contraction present in the centered fan; WIE is a
budget certificate for import excess, not by itself a transport inequality.

Therefore beta stationarity supplies the right ledger, but not the missing
source estimate. A proof must retain enough signed deficit/contraction, or
prove an additional legal-aware import-control statement.

## T2. Single Resisting Statement

No exact realized argmin refuter of WIE or RSI was found in this wave. The
stationarity calculation reduces the open step to the following single
legal-aware financing statement:

```text
(FIN) Under the D/RSI hypotheses, with the notation above,

L_mu
+ sum_{l in B} mu_l *
    ( N^D_l - L_l - N_l - C_l )_+
  <= C_fin * (G_class^- + S_-^mu + R_D^nu).
```

This is deliberately stronger than WIE but weaker than a monolithic TT proof:
it contains no `A_l`; all terms are stationarity-financing terms. It also
names the legal leakage `L_mu` explicitly. If a later argument proves `L_mu=0`
in the all-degenerate horn, `(FIN)` reduces to a pure signed-financier import
bound. If legal rows can coexist with the D tax, `(FIN)` must be proved
together with the legal-collateral horn, not hidden inside RSI.

The currently tempting smaller subclaims are not enough:

- `G_class^-` alone does not control `sum_l (-C_l)_+ mu_l` without an import
  target bound for representative rows.
- `S_-^mu` alone does not control beta-negative positive imports
  `sum_l |beta_j| P_jl^+ mu_l`.
- `R_D^nu` alone does not control `sum_l N^D_l mu_l` unless negative entries
  of D rows cannot land on large-`mu` targets.
- Legal positive rows are absent from `M_D`; their negative B entries require
  either a separate legal-horn estimate or an explicit term in `(FIN)`.
