‖Q² − Q‖∞→∞ ≤ η ≤ η0   ⇒   ∃ E = E² stochastic with ‖Q − E‖∞→∞ ≤ C√η

There are universal constants η0, C > 0, independent of the dimension, such that every row-stochastic matrix Q whose idempotence defect ‖Q²−Q‖∞→∞ is at most η ≤ η0 admits a genuine stochastic idempotent E within distance C·√η. The exponent 1/2 cannot be improved (cor-classical-sharpness) — the live witness below is that family.

The rung reached is af-validated, not Lean. Both halves of the theorem were driven to validated in an adversarial proof tree: a fresh prover, a separate fresh verifier per node, and challenge/amendment rounds, with the orchestrator forbidden from judging any step. That is strictly weaker, per inference, than a kernel-checked proof. No sorry-free Lean/mathlib proof exists for anything here, nothing on this site is peer-reviewed or published, and the constants are not explicit. What the record does support is measured defense-in-depth — see the Defense page.

Why the exponent is ½ and not better

The sharpness half is carried by an explicit 4×4 family. For each λ ∈ (0, ½) the matrix Qλ below is row-stochastic with defect η = 2λ², yet every stochastic idempotent F on ℓ∞(4) stays at distance at least λ = √(η/2) from it. Drag the slider: the defect collapses quadratically while the distance only collapses linearly, so no exponent β > ½ can survive.

The constants are universal — and not explicit

The upper-bound half closes through Route F. Its assembly lemma (lem-routef-f0-assembly) does not produce a number; it produces a recipe. From the universal scalars K ≥ 1 and ηK > 0 supplied by lem-routef-k-ledger, one may take

C := K + 4√(2K),    η0 := ηK = min{ρfac, (24K)−1, 1}.

K is explicit only up to the unnamed big-O constants of the source factorisation, so no numerical value is claimed. Two consequences are pinned, because they follow from K ≥ 1 alone:
