\section{Dimension selection in an EXT-CB datum}\label{sec:extcb1-dimension-selection}

A closed EXT-CB datum (\texttt{def-extcb-datum}) carries $\dproj$-projections $P,Q$ with
$\nrm{P+Q-I}\le\dproj$, an extended $\dproj$-isomorphism $v:\Mat{r}\to\Cnrs{P}$,
$\dim\Cnrs{Q}=1$ and $\Cnr{P}{Q}\ne0$. Nothing in the datum says how large the cross
corner is. The two lemmas of this section settle it: the corner has dimension exactly
$r$, and the diagonal $Q$-corner exactly $1$ --- the shape needed to build one map on
$\Mat{r+1}$. The first lemma is the technical enabler; the second is the count.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-extcb1-close-corner-dimension}]\label{lem:extcb1-close-corner-dimension}
There is a universal $\comb_{\mathrm{close}}>0$ such that, in an EXT-CB datum with
$\comb\le \comb_{\mathrm{close}}$, the compression ranges $\Cnr{v(I_r)}{Q}$ and
$\Cnr{P}{Q}$ have the same dimension.
\emph{Transcription note.} $v(I_r)$ is the image of the \emph{source} unit; it is an
approximate projection close to $P$, not equal to it, and the two corners it and $P$ cut
out are literally different subspaces. Only their dimensions are claimed equal.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Close-compression range invariance: there is a universal e_close > 0 such that, in an EXT-CB datum with e <= e_close, the compression ranges S_{v(I_r),Q} and S_{P,Q} have the same dimension.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
Write $R=v(I_r)$, $E=\Co{R}{Q}$ and $F=\Co{P}{Q}$.

\emph{$R$ is close to $P$, and is itself an approximate projection.} Bijectivity of $v$
onto $\Cnrs{P}$ makes that corner nonzero, so $P$ is nonvanishing; $\dim\Cnrs{Q}=1$ makes
$Q$ nonvanishing; the nonvanishing alternative of \texttt{def-delta-projection} then
bounds $\nrm{P},\nrm{Q}$ by a universal $M_1$. By
\texttt{lem-compcb-corner-algebra} the corner $\Cnrs{P}$ is an extended
$C_{\mathrm{ca}}\comb$-$C^*$-algebra with unit $\cunit{P}=\Cos{P}(P)$, and the unit clause
for the extended $\dproj$-isomorphism $v$ gives $\nrm{R-\cunit{P}}\le\dproj$; combining
with $\nrm{\cunit{P}-P(PP)}=O(\comb)\nrm{P}$ and
$\nrm{P(PP)-P}\le\bigl((1+\ecs)\nrm{P}+1\bigr)\dproj$ yields
\begin{equation}\label{eq:close-RP}
  \nrm{R-P}\;\le\;C_1\comb .
\end{equation}
Star-preservation gives $R\dg=R$, $\dproj$-multiplicativity gives $\nrm{R\cpr R-R}\le\dproj$
inside $\Cnrs{P}$, and the compressed-versus-ambient comparison converts this into
$\nrm{R^{2}-R}\le C_2\comb$ in $\mathcal A$: $R$ is a $(C_R\comb)$-projection there.

\emph{Two close bounded idempotents.} With the common adjusted defect
$\dproj'=C_R\comb$, the pairs $(R,Q)$ and $(P,Q)$ both satisfy the hypotheses of
\texttt{lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities} at $n=1$, so $E^{2}=E$ and
$F^{2}=F$. Each compression is $O(\comb)$-close to the corresponding
left-times-right multiplication, and
$\bigl(L_RR_Q-L_PR_Q\bigr)(X)=(R-P)(XQ)$ has norm at most
$(1+\ecs)^{2}\nrm{R-P}\nrm{Q}\nrm{X}$; with \eqref{eq:close-RP} this gives universal
$C_0,M_0$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:close-EF}
  \nrm{E-F}\;\le\;C_0\comb,\qquad \nrm{E}\;\le\;M_0 .
\end{equation}

\emph{Range transport.} If $E,F$ are bounded idempotents on a Banach space with
$\nrm{E}\le M$ and $\nrm{E-F}(2M+1)<1$, put $W=FE+(I-F)(I-E)$. Idempotence gives the two
exact identities
\begin{equation}\label{eq:close-W}
  W-I=(F-E)(2E-I),\qquad WE=FW=FE ,
\end{equation}
so $\nrm{W-I}\le\nrm{F-E}(2\nrm{E}+1)<1$ and $W$ is invertible by its Neumann series.
The relation $WE=FW$ sends $\Img E$ into $\Img F$ and, read as $EW^{-1}=W^{-1}F$,
sends $\Img F$ back; hence $W$ restricts to a linear bijection $\Img E\to\Img F$ and the
two ranges have equal dimension. Taking
$\comb_{\mathrm{close}}$ below the thresholds of the two preceding steps and below
$1/[2C_0(2M_0+1)]$ makes \eqref{eq:close-EF} satisfy that hypothesis, and
$\Img\Co{R}{Q}=\Cnr{R}{Q}$, $\Img\Co{P}{Q}=\Cnr{P}{Q}$ finish it.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-extcb1-cross-corner-dimension}]\label{lem:extcb1-cross-corner-dimension}
There is a universal $\comb_{\mathrm{sel}}>0$ such that every EXT-CB datum with
$\comb\le \comb_{\mathrm{sel}}$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sel-main}
  \bigl(\dim\Cnr{P}{Q},\ \dim\Cnr{Q}{Q}\bigr)=(r,1).
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{EXT-CB cross-corner dimension: there is a universal e_sel > 0 such that every EXT-CB datum with e <= e_sel satisfies (dim S_{P,Q},dim S_{Q,Q})=(r,1).}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The count is produced by transporting the diagonal matrix units through $v$, showing that
each transported unit is a one-dimensional projection equivalent to all the others, and
then summing.

\emph{Two inclusions.} Let $D_r\subseteq\Mat{r}$ be the diagonal subalgebra with
projection basis $\Pi_a=E_{aa}$, let $v_D$ be the restriction of $v$ to $D_r$, put
$R_a=v(E_{aa})$, and let $w:\mathbb C\to\mathcal A$ be $w(\lambda)=\lambda Q$, whose
source has the one-element projection basis $\{1\}$. Restriction preserves linearity,
star-preservation, $\dproj$-multiplicativity and the two-sided norm bounds, and the
compressed product on $\Cnrs{P}$ differs from the ambient one by $O(\comb)$ on bounded
elements, so $v_D$ is a non-unital $O(\comb)$-inclusion into $\mathcal A$ with
$v_D(I)=v(I_r)$, uniformly in $r$. For $w$: nonvanishing of $Q$ gives
$\lvert\nrm{Q}-1\rvert\le C_{\mathrm{nv}}\comb$, the operator-space axioms give the exact
cross-norm identity $\nrm{X\otimes Q}=\nrm{X}\nrm{Q}$ at every level, and
$\nrm{(XY)\otimes Q-(X\otimes Q)(Y\otimes Q)}=\nrm{(XY)\otimes(Q-Q^{2})}\le\dproj\nrm{X}\nrm{Y}$;
so every amplification of $w$ is an $O(\comb)$-inclusion as well.

\emph{The transported matrix units.} For $a,b\le r$ let $c_{ab}(X)=E_{aa}XE_{bb}$ on
$\Mat{r}$ and put $T_{ab}=v\,c_{ab}\,v^{-1}\Cos{P}$. Since $\Cos{P}$ is an idempotent with
range $\Cnrs{P}=\Img v$, one has $\Cos{P}v=v$ and $v^{-1}\Cos{P}v=\mathrm{id}$; as
$c_{ab}^{2}=c_{ab}$ with one-dimensional range, $T_{ab}$ is an \emph{exact} bounded
idempotent of rank one, with range $\mathbb C\,v(E_{ab})$. The tree then compares it with
the corresponding compression: uniformly in $a,b$ and $r$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sel-Tcomp}
  \nrm{\Co{R_a}{R_b}-T_{ab}}\;\le\;C_m\comb .
\end{equation}
Three fixed-length estimates give this --- each $R_a$ is a Hermitian $C_1\comb$-projection
(from $\nrm{E_{aa}}=1$, $\dproj$-multiplicativity and the compressed-versus-ambient
comparison); the subordination bound
$\nrm{R_a(XR_b)-R_a\bigl((\Cos{P}X)R_b\bigr)}\le C_s\comb\nrm{X}$, a finite telescope using
$R_i=\Cos{P}(R_i)$ and $\Cos{P}(Y)=P(YP)+O(\comb)\nrm{Y}$; and the transported-source
comparison $\nrm{T_{ab}X-R_a\bigl((\Cos{P}X)R_b\bigr)}\le C_3\comb\nrm{X}$, from two
applications of $\dproj$-multiplicativity in $\Mat{r}$ and the corner-algebra bounds ---
together with $\nrm{\Co{R_a}{R_b}X-R_a(XR_b)}\le C_4\comb\nrm{X}$. Since all source
matrix factors have norm one, no constant depends on $r$.

\emph{Rank invariance.} If $E,F$ are bounded idempotents with $\nrm{E-F}<1$ then
$x\in\Img E$ with $Fx=0$ gives $x=Ex=(E-F)x$, hence $x=0$; interchanging $E$ and $F$ gives
the reverse, so the two ranges have equal dimension when either is finite-dimensional.
Shrinking $\comb$ so that $C_m\comb<1$ and applying this to \eqref{eq:sel-Tcomp} gives
$\dim\Cnr{R_a}{R_b}=\rank T_{ab}=1$ for all $a,b$: taking $a=b$, each $R_a$ is a
one-dimensional projection; taking $a\ne b$, the $R_a$ are pairwise equivalent in the
sense of \texttt{def-one-dimensional-delta-projection}.

\emph{Dichotomy, additivity, selection.} Put $d_a=\dim\Cnr{R_a}{Q}$. By
\texttt{lem-extcb-one-dimensional-corner-dimension}
(\S\ref{sec:extcb-one-dimensional-corners}), applied to the one-dimensional projections
$R_a$ and $Q$ with the enlarged common accuracy, $d_a\le1$, so $d_a\in\{0,1\}$. The values
coincide: if some $d_b=1$ then $R_b$ is equivalent to $Q$, every $R_a$ is equivalent to
$R_b$, and transitivity of equivalence --- the registered property whose source proof is
the one-dimensional product lemma of \S\ref{sec:extcb-one-dimensional-corners} --- gives
$d_a=1$ for all $a$; otherwise all $d_a=0$. Write $d$ for the common value. Now
\texttt{lem-extcb-corner-dimension-additivity}
(\S\ref{sec:extcb-corner-dimension-additivity}), applied to $D_r$ and $\mathbb C$ with the
inclusions $v_D,w$, gives a linear bijection
$\Cnr{v(I_r)}{Q}\to\bigoplus_{a=1}^{r}\Cnr{R_a}{Q}$ and hence
$\dim\Cnr{v(I_r)}{Q}=\sum_a d_a=r\,d$; Lemma~\ref{lem:extcb1-close-corner-dimension} turns
this into $\dim\Cnr{P}{Q}=r\,d$. The datum assumes $\Cnr{P}{Q}\ne0$ and $r\ge1$, so
$d\ne0$, i.e.\ $d=1$ and $\dim\Cnr{P}{Q}=r$. Finally $\Cnr{Q}{Q}=\Cnrs{Q}$ has dimension
$1$ by hypothesis, and $\comb_{\mathrm{sel}}$ is the minimum of the finitely many
universal thresholds used, giving \eqref{eq:sel-main}.

\noindent\textbf{Role in the chain.} The close-corner lemma imports
\texttt{lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities},
\texttt{lem-compcb-corner-algebra} and \texttt{lem-hcb3-uniform-square-lower}; the
cross-corner lemma imports both of the preceding sections and the close-corner lemma. The
pair is the \emph{selection} step of the EXT tier: it is what licenses treating
$\Cnr{P}{Q}$ as an $r$-dimensional column and $\Cnrs{Q}$ as a line, i.e.\ the block shape
that the four-corner merge (\S\ref{sec:extcb-four-corner-merge}) assembles into a single
map on $\Mat{r+1}$. Its consumer is \texttt{conj-extcb} (\S\ref{sec:extcb}), now
\texttt{proved} with \texttt{af: validated}; the close-corner lemma is there because a
hostile verdict on that parent demanded it.
