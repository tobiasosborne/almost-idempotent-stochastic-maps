\section{The bounded-slab starvation completion obstruction}\label{sec:starvation-obstruction}

This standalone obstruction rules out a rank-three completion of the exchange-starvation configuration
without any auxiliary constant $K$. Fix a finite index set $I$, a real $A\in[4,6]$, a real
$\tsq\in(0,\tfrac1{256}]$, and set
\[
  t=\tsq^{2},\qquad a=\frac{\tsq}{1+\tsq}.
\]

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-starvation-completion-obstruction}]\label{lem:starvation-completion-obstruction}
There is no rank-three exact signed idempotent $P$ (so $P^2=P$, $P\mathbf1=\mathbf1$, $\rank P=3$) with
row negative mass $\nu_i\le t$ for every $i\in I$ that carries five distinct full row-point fibers,
represented by $v,w,f,z,o$, such that, writing $D=p_z-p_v$ and $E=p_o-p_v$ (linearly independent),
\[
  \lone{D}=\tsq,\quad p_f-p_v=-A\,D+t\,E,\quad p_w-p_v=a\,(p_f-p_v),
\]
with top-row fiber masses $c_v=1-\tsq$, $c_w=\tsq+t$, $c_f=-t$, and $c_Q=0$ for every other fiber $Q$,
where every full row-point fiber has unique coordinates $p_Q=p_v+x_Q D+y_Q E$ and every nonactor
support fiber satisfies $p_Q\in\conv\{p_v,p_w,p_f,p_z,p_o\}$ or $0\le y_Q\le1$.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Bounded-slab starvation completion obstruction (K-free): for every finite I, every real A in [4,6], every real tau in (0,1/256], and t := tau^2, a := tau/(1+tau), there is no rank-three exact signed idempotent P (P^2 = P, P*1 = 1, rank P = 3) with row negative mass nu_i <= t for every i in I having five distinct full row-point fibers represented by v,w,f,z,o such that, with D := p_z - p_v and E := p_o - p_v linearly independent, ||D||_1 = tau, p_f - p_v = -A*D + t*E, p_w - p_v = a*(p_f - p_v), top-row fiber masses c_v = 1 - tau, c_w = tau + t, c_f = -t, and c_Q = 0 for every other full row-point fiber Q, every full row-point fiber Q has unique reals x_Q, y_Q with p_Q = p_v + x_Q*D + y_Q*E, and every nonactor support fiber Q satisfies either p_Q in conv{p_v,p_w,p_f,p_z,p_o} or 0 <= y_Q <= 1.}

\noindent\textbf{Proof sketch (by contradiction).} Suppose such a $P$ exists. Group the coordinate
indices into fibers $Q$ (classes of equal rows) and set $c_Q=\sum_{j\in Q}P_{vj}$,
$d_Q=\sum_{j\in Q}(P_{zj}-P_{vj})$, $e_Q=\sum_{j\in Q}(P_{oj}-P_{vj})$. The negative-mass hypothesis
$\nu_i\le t$ gives the subset ledger $-t\le P_i(S)\le1+t$ and $\lone{p_i}\le1+2t$ for every row $i$ and
subset $S$. Subtracting the $v$-row from the $z$-row of $P^2=P$ gives $DP=D$ with $\sum_j D_j=0$;
expanding $D=\sum_j D_j p_j$ in the coordinates $p_j=p_v+x_jD+y_jE$ and using linear independence of
$D,E$ produces the \emph{exact unit moment}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:so-moment}
  \sum_Q x_Q\,d_Q=1.
\end{equation}
Every actor and interior fiber has bounded $x$-coordinate, $|x_Q|\le A$, so the hull part contributes at
most $A\tsq$ (since $\sum_{Q\in\mathrm{Hin}}|d_Q|\le\lone{D}=\tsq$). For an exterior fiber $Q$ the
disjunction forces $0\le y_Q\le1$, and the coordinate identity with the row-norm bounds gives the lever
estimate $|x_Q|\tsq\le2+4t$; a single global budget from the row-$z$, row-$f$, and row-$o$ subset
bounds gives $\sum_{Q\in\mathrm{Ext}}|d_Q|\le t\bigl(1+(2+t)/A\bigr)$, with \emph{no} dependence on the
number of fibers. Combining,
\[
  1=\sum_Q x_Q d_Q\;\le\;\tsq\Bigl[A+(2+4t)\bigl(1+\tfrac{2+t}{A}\bigr)\Bigr].
\]
But $0<\tsq\le\tfrac1{256}$ gives $t<\tfrac14$, $2+4t<3$, $(2+t)/A\le3/4$, and $A\le6$, so the right
side is strictly below $\tfrac1{256}\bigl[6+3(1+\tfrac34)\bigr]=\tfrac{45}{1024}<1$ --- contradicting
\eqref{eq:so-moment}. \hfill$\square$
