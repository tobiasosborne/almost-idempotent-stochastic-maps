bound. Nothing in this document establishes either hypothesis for any particular datum.
Their conditional shape is itself a correction of record --- the corresponding clause of
the parent contract was unconditional until an exact $\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C$
counterexample refuted it.

\subsection*{What is next, and what it is not}

The immediate queue continues the H-CB tier bottom-up:
\texttt{lem-hcb3-offdiagonal-inverse}, validated (16/16, taint clean) as this revision
closed --- its prose write-up follows in the next wave --- then the canonical tier \texttt{lem-hcb4-canonical-gram}, \texttt{lem-hcb4-canonical-inverse}
and \texttt{lem-hcb4-canonical-closeness}. Only when all of these are validated can the
parent contract \texttt{conj-hcb} be attempted; \texttt{conj-hcb} is at present
\texttt{proved-mod-audit} with \texttt{af: none} --- a hostile-verified paper proof, one
rung below rigorous here. The off-diagonal inverse inherits the same conditional shape as
its diagonal counterpart, and that is why \S\ref{sec:hcb3-diagonal-lower-modulus} and
\S\ref{sec:hcb3-diagonal-inverse} keep their hypotheses in the statements rather than
absorbing them into a threshold.

Beyond \texttt{conj-hcb} lies the EXT tier (\texttt{conj-extcb} and the
\texttt{lem-extcb*} family), and beyond that the assembly. Two items are explicitly
\emph{not} in the pipeline and must not be treated as pending detail: the MAIN-CB
assembly rows and the ledger rows are \textbf{quarantined} pending a focused repair and a
fresh hostile review, along with five named gap interfaces (exact-target approximation,
the Stage-1 polar contract, the MAIN structure, the ledger domains, and the F2/F3
bridge); and several Stage-1 external topology sources are not yet in \texttt{refs/},
which is a provisioning risk, not a proof step.

The honest summary is therefore: the base of one route has been made rigorous, the middle
is in progress, and the top is quarantined. \texttt{op-classical} is \textbf{open}.
