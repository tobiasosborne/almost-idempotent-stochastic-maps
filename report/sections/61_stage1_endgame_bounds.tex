\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-stage1-bound-quotient-left-inversion}]\label{lem:stage1-bound-quotient-left-inversion}
For every universal witness tuple $W$ satisfying the ranges and scale identities displayed
in the contract below, there are universal $e^{r}_{\mathrm{quot}}>0$ and
$\epsilon^{r}_{B}=\min\{\epsilon^{r}_{*},e^{r}_{\mathrm{quot}}\}>0$ such that every
finite-dimensional exact-unit $\epsilon_r$-$C^{*}$-algebra with
$0\le\epsilon_r\le\epsilon^{r}_{B}$ and $1<\dim_{\mathbb C}\calX<\infty$, equipped with
the displayed data satisfying $(A_2),(A_4),(A_5),(A_6)$ and $(R)$, has
\[
 \breve{\mathcal U}=\mathcal U_e/\mathrm U(1),\quad \breve e=[J],\quad
 \breve\mu([U],[V])=[\mu(U,V)],\quad \breve\sigma([U])=[\sigma(U)].
\]
Here $(\breve{\mathcal U},\breve\mu,\breve e)$ is a connected H-space,
$\breve\sigma$ is a smooth left inversion,
$\sigma(cU)=\overline c\,\sigma(U)$ and
$\nrm{\sigma(U)-U\dg}\le C_{\mathrm{grp}}\epsilon_r$; moreover
$\breve{\mathcal U}$ is a connected compact orientable smooth manifold without boundary
of real dimension $\dim_{\mathbb C}\calX-1$.
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
Fix $W$ and the displayed algebra.  Positivity of the entries in the defining minimum gives
$\epsilon_*^r>0$.  Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1-quotient-manifold-package} supplies the universal
$e_{mathrm{quot}}^r$; taking $\epsilon_B^r=\min\{\epsilon_*^r,e_{mathrm{quot}}^r\}$
provides the connected compact orientable quotient manifold with the asserted dimension.
The graph derivative in $(A_2)$ differs from the identity by less than one, so the smooth
atlas upgrade applies to the same $g_V$ and $\chi_V$, without changing values or first
derivatives.  For the polar map, the tree supplies the ambient-openness argument explicitly:
the involution splits $\calX_{\mathbb R}=\Herm\oplus\aHerm$, the graph charts give
$\dim_{\mathbb R}\Unit=\dim_{\mathbb R}\aHerm$, and therefore the domain of
$\Pi_{\delta_*}$ and $\calX_{\mathbb R}$ have equal dimension.  Its injective ambient
differential is thus an isomorphism, so the inverse-function theorem makes
$S_{\delta_*}$ ambient-open and the same inverse $(u_\delta,h_\delta)$ smooth.
The smooth-operations row then upgrades the same scalar action, $\mu$, and $\sigma$ and
retains their equivariance and the $(A_5)$ error estimate.

On the identity component, the scalar action is free and proper.  The quotient theorem
therefore gives a smooth surjective submersion; the tree deliberately chooses this quotient
structure and transports compactness, dimension, boundarylessness, orientation, and
connectedness topologically, rather than identifying it with the package atlas by a
misapplied uniqueness clause.  Equivariance makes $\breve\mu$ and $\breve\sigma$
representative-independent, while local quotient sections prove smoothness.  Finally set
$q_*=C_{\mathrm{grp}}\epsilon_r$ and use $(A_6)$ on
$U_0=\mu(\sigma(U),U)$ and $U_1=J$.  The path
$[H(t,\mu(\sigma(U),U),J)]$ is representative-independent, continuous, and basepoint
preserving, hence is the required left-inversion homotopy.  The live tree has thirteen
nodes; three archived nodes record the repaired atlas-identification route.  Fresh
verifiers also forced the ambient-openness proof and an explicit dependency for the
homotopy node; all three challenges were repaired without changing the contract.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-stage1-bound-quotient-local-index}]\label{lem:stage1-bound-quotient-local-index}
Under the stronger parameterized hypotheses $(A_2),(A_4)$--$(A_7)$ and $(R)$ quoted below,
at the same universal bound $\epsilon_B^r$, the quotient package of
Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1-bound-quotient-left-inversion} has charts
$\chi_s:B^{\aHerm}_{r_{\mathrm{iso}}}(0)\to\Unit$, $s\in\{\pm1\}$, with $\chi_s(0)=sJ$ and inverse $\psi_s=\phi_{sJ}^{\mathrm{par}}$ on the image, and each chart image is retained by $\sigma$, and
\[
 F_s=\psi_s\circ\sigma\circ\chi_s,qquad
 \nrm{D(F_s-\mathrm{id})(A)+2I}<1,qquad
 \nrm{A-B}\le\nrm{\chi_s(A)-\chi_s(B)}.
\]
The chart contains $\Unit\cap B_{r_{\mathrm{iso}}}(sJ)$.  Moreover $\breve e=[J]$ is an
isolated fixed point of $\breve\sigma$, the line $i\mathbb R J$ is
$D\sigma_J$-invariant,
\[
 \nrm{D\breve\sigma_{\breve e}+I}_{\mathrm{quot}}<1,qquad
 \det(I-D\breve\sigma_{\breve e})>0,qquad
 \operatorname{ind}(\breve\sigma,\breve e)=+1.
\]
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The first branch imports Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1-bound-quotient-left-inversion} with the same
$u_\delta,h_\delta,\mu,\sigma,H$ and repeats the smooth upgrades.  Its repaired
ambient-openness step uses the real splitting $\calX=\Herm\oplus\aHerm$, equality of source
and target dimensions, and the quantitative inverse-function lemma to show that every
point of $S_{\delta_*}$ has an ambient-open image neighborhood.
For $s=\pm1$, exact unitality gives $f_{sJ}(0)=0$; uniqueness in $(A_2)$ gives
$g_{sJ}(0)=0$, hence $\chi_s(0)=sJ$.  The inverse is the anti-Hermitian part
$\psi_s=\phi_{sJ}^{\mathrm{par}}$.  If $U\in\Unit$ lies within $r_{\mathrm{iso}}$ of
$sJ$, then $\phi_{sJ}(U)=s(U-sJ)$ has Hermitian and anti-Hermitian parts inside the graph
domain; uniqueness forces the Hermitian part to equal $g_{sJ}$, placing $U$ in the chart.
Contractivity of the anti-Hermitian projection gives the stated lower Lipschitz estimate.

Scalar equivariance differentiates to $D\sigma_J(iJ)=-iJ$.  Thus, for
$V=i\mathbb RJ$ and $R=DF_+(0)+I$, one has $R|_V=0$, so $R$ induces
$\overline R=D\breve\sigma_{\breve e}+I$ on $\aHerm/V$.  The repaired quotient estimate is
\[
 \nrm{\overline R[x]}_{\mathrm{quot}}
 \le \nrm{R(x+v)}\le\nrm R\,\nrm{x+v}\quad(v\in V),
\]
followed by the infimum over $v$; no false equality with an ambient norm is used.  Hence
$\nrm{D\breve\sigma_{\breve e}+I}_{\mathrm{quot}}<1$ by the fully non-strict chain in
$(A_7)$ and $(R)$.  Therefore $I-D\breve\sigma_{\breve e}$ is invertible.  In an arbitrary
smooth chart the tree transports this invertibility by conjugacy, rather than transporting
the norm bound, and applies the quantitative inverse-function lemma to
$x\mapsto x-\breve\sigma(x)$; this isolates $\breve e$.
Writing $E=D\breve\sigma_{\breve e}+I$, the path $2I-tE$ stays invertible for
$0\le t\le1$, so its determinant retains the positive sign of $2I$.  The local-index sign
formula, Lemma~\ref{lem:topology-local-index-sign}, gives index $+1$.  The nineteen-node
tree records five repaired verifier challenges: ambient openness, the quotient-norm step,
the coordinate-norm inference, a strict-inequality edge, and a missing dependency.  None
changed the registry contract; the hard cap alone was ratified upward from fifteen to
twenty to retain these visible repairs.
