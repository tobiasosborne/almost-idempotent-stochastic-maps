\section{The strengthened K-ledger package: scalar header positivity, the factor-map and factor-estimate packets, and the strengthened K-ledger}\label{sec:routef-kledger-package}

This section reproduces the four \texttt{af}-validated rows that package the Route-F
factorization.  The scalar header and setting datum are those of
Definition~\ref{def:routef-raw-factor-setting}; the first row fixes their universal
quantifier order, the next two preserve one serial packet, and the last performs the
positive-retract finish without reselecting any witness.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-routef-scalar-header-positivity}]\label{lem:routef-scalar-header-positivity}
There is one global witness package $W_{\mathrm{RF}}$ supplied by
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-raw-factor-setting-formation} such that, for its scalars
$K$ in (1.6), $\rho_{\mathrm{fac}}$ in (1.7), and
$\eta_K=\min\{\rho_{\mathrm{fac}},(24K)^{-1},1\}$ in (1.8),
$K<\infty$, $K\ge1$, $\rho_{\mathrm{fac}}>0$, and $\eta_K>0$.  These scalars are
universal and independent of $\mathcal H,\Phi,\eta,n$, the amplification level, the
simple-block count, and the block dimensions, and
\[
 \eta_K\le\rho_{\mathrm{fac}}\le\rho_2\le\rho_T\le\rho_{\mathrm{id}}^{\mathrm{corr}},
 \quad \rho_2\le\rho_{\Delta'},\rho_\Delta,
 \quad \rho_{\mathrm{fac}}\le\rho_{\Delta\Upsilon}\le\rho_\Upsilon\le\rho_{\Upsilon'},
 \quad \rho_{\mathrm{fac}}\le\rho_{\mathrm{mult}},\rho_{\Upsilon\Delta}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\contractquote{Route F scalar-header positivity: there exists one global witness package W_RF supplied by lem-routef-raw-factor-setting-formation such that, writing K for its scalar (1.6), rho_fac for its scalar (1.7), and eta_K := min{rho_fac, (24*K)^(-1), 1} for its scalar (1.8), K is finite with K >= 1, rho_fac > 0, and eta_K > 0, these scalars are universal and independent of H, Phi, eta, n, amplification level, simple-block count, and block dimensions, and eta_K <= rho_fac <= rho_2 <= rho_T <= rho_id^corr, rho_2 <= rho_Delta', rho_2 <= rho_Delta, rho_fac <= rho_DeltaUpsilon <= rho_Upsilon <= rho_Upsilon', rho_fac <= rho_mult, and rho_fac <= rho_UpsilonDelta.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The tree first selects the scalar header supplied, before every input quantifier, by
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-raw-factor-setting-formation}.  Its primitive fields give
$C_\theta=12(\sqrt2-1)>0$, the positive finite $C_A$, and
$\bar C_E=\max\{1,C_E\}$; hence $C_V,C_T$ are positive and finite.  Every entry in the
minimum defining $\rho_T$ is then positive, including the two reciprocal entries.
Proceeding through (1.2), each new coefficient is a finite sum of earlier coefficients
and each new radius is a finite minimum of positive terms.  The same induction through
(1.3)--(1.5) gives positive finite $C_N,C_R,C_L,C_{\Upsilon'},C_\Upsilon$ and positive
$\rho_{\Upsilon'},\rho_\Upsilon,\rho_{\Delta\Upsilon},\rho_{\mathrm{mult}}$ and
$\rho_{\Upsilon\Delta}$.  Thus (1.6) makes $K$ the maximum of $1$ and three finite
expressions, so $K<\infty$ and $K\ge1$; (1.7) makes $\rho_{\mathrm{fac}}$ the minimum
of the four positive radii $\rho_2,\rho_{\Delta\Upsilon},\rho_{\mathrm{mult}}$ and
$\rho_{\Upsilon\Delta}$; and (1.8) gives $\eta_K>0$.  The displayed inequalities are
exactly the coordinate inequalities of these successive minima.  Finally, all three
scalars depend only on the pre-input header, proving their asserted universality.
Two verifier challenges were repaired inside this proof tree: one removed a pending-sibling
dependency by rebuilding the positivity step with validated support, and one corrected
the count in (1.6) from five purported entries to its actual four.  Two superseded helper
nodes were archived; neither repair altered the registry contract.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-routef-factor-map-packet}]\label{lem:routef-factor-map-packet}
Fix the preceding $W_{\mathrm{RF}}$ and its $K,\rho_{\mathrm{fac}},\eta_K$.  For every
$n\ge1$, row-stochastic $Q:\linfd n\to\linfd n$, and $0\le\eta\le\eta_K$ with
$\nrm{Q^2-Q}_{\infty\to\infty}\le\eta$, let $D:M_n\to\mathbb C^n$ be diagonal
extraction, $J:\mathbb C^n\to M_n$ diagonal inclusion, $Q_{\mathbb C}$ the canonical
complex-linear extension of $Q$, and $\Phi=JQ_{\mathbb C}D$.  Then $\Phi$ is UCP and
$\nrm{\Phi^2-\Phi}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le\eta$, and there are a finite-dimensional unital
$C^*$-algebra $B$, one setting datum $S$ over this same $W_{\mathrm{RF}}$ for
$(\mathcal H,\Phi,\eta)=(\mathbb C^n,\Phi,\eta)$, CP maps
$\Delta':B\to M_n$, $\Upsilon':M_n\to B$, and UCP maps
$\Delta:B\to M_n$, $\Upsilon:M_n\to B$.  Here $\Delta'$ is supplied by
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-delta-prime-closeness}, $\Delta$ from that same $\Delta'$ by
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-delta-normalization-closeness}, $\Upsilon'$ from that same
$(\Delta',\Delta)$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-upsilon-prime-closeness}, and $\Upsilon$
from that same triple by Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-upsilon-normalization-closeness}.
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
Fix the header from Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-scalar-header-positivity}.  Its radius chain gives
$\eta_K\le\rho_{\mathrm{id}}^{\mathrm{corr}},\rho_{\Delta'},\rho_\Delta,
\rho_{\Upsilon'},\rho_\Upsilon$.  For arbitrary $n,Q,eta$, the diagonal-lift row
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-f0-ucp-lift} makes $\Phi=JQ_{\mathbb C}D$ UCP, while
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-f0-defect-identity} identifies its completely bounded defect with
$\nrm{Q^2-Q}_{\infty\to\infty}$.  Since $\mathbb C^n$ is nonzero, the formation row
applies at this exact triple and is invoked once, producing one algebra $B$ and one datum
$S$ over the already fixed header.  The proof then preserves that datum rigidly.  First
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-delta-prime-closeness} supplies the CP map $\Delta'$; then
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-delta-normalization-closeness} consumes that particular map and
supplies the UCP map $\Delta$.  Next Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-upsilon-prime-closeness}
consumes the same $(S,\Delta',\Delta)$ and supplies the CP map $\Upsilon'$, after which
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-upsilon-normalization-closeness} consumes that exact triple and
supplies the UCP map $\Upsilon$.  Thus the witnesses have the serial provenance claimed
at the root; no component packet is opened and no witness is reselected.  The exported
tree is first-pass and clean, with no recorded challenge or amendment.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-routef-factor-estimate-packet}]\label{lem:routef-factor-estimate-packet}
Fix the preceding $W_{\mathrm{RF}}$ and its $K,\rho_{\mathrm{fac}},\eta_K$.  For every
$n\ge1$, row-stochastic $Q:\linfd n\to\linfd n$, and $0\le\eta\le\eta_K$ with
$\nrm{Q^2-Q}_{\infty\to\infty}\le\eta$, form $D,J,Q_{\mathbb C}$ and
$\Phi=JQ_{\mathbb C}D$ as in Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-factor-map-packet}.  For every
same-datum packet $(B,S,\Delta',\Delta,\Upsilon',\Upsilon)$ supplied there,
\[
 \nrm{\Delta\Upsilon-\Phi}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le K\eta,
 \qquad \nrm{\Upsilon\Delta-I_B}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le K\eta,
\]
and, for every $r\ge1$ and $X,Y\in M_r(B)$,
\[
 \nrm{\Upsilon_r(\Delta_rX\,\Delta_rY)-XY}
 \le K\eta\nrm X\nrm Y.
\]
Moreover $0\le\eta\le\min\{(24K)^{-1},1\}$ and
\[
 3K\eta\le\tfrac18<1,
 \qquad \frac{3K\eta}{1-3K\eta}\le4K\eta\le\tfrac16<\tfrac12.
\]
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
Fix the header and an arbitrary packet supplied by Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-factor-map-packet}.
The serial provenance of that packet is precisely the input required by all three telescope
rows, while Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-scalar-header-positivity} gives
$0\le\eta\le\eta_K\le\rho_{\mathrm{fac}}$ and places $\rho_{\mathrm{fac}}$ below their
three domain radii.  Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-delta-upsilon-telescope} first yields
$\nrm{\Delta\Upsilon-\Phi}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le
(C_\theta+C_\Delta+2C_\Upsilon)\eta$; its coefficient is an entry in the maximum (1.6),
so it is at most $K$.  Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-upsilon-delta-telescope} similarly gives
coefficient $C_\Upsilon+2C_\Delta\le K$.  At every amplification,
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-multiplicative-telescope} gives coefficient
$C_\Upsilon+2(C_2+C_\theta+C_\Delta)\le K$, and multiplication by the nonnegative
$\eta\nrm X\nrm Y$ preserves the bound.  Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-k-finiteness} records the
common domain and coefficient ledger used in those three applications.  Finally,
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-threshold-minimum} applies the formula
$\eta_K=\min\{\rho_{\mathrm{fac}},(24K)^{-1},1\}$ to give the displayed smallness and
rational estimates, including positivity of $1-3K\eta$.  All assertions concern the same
arbitrary packet; the exported tree is first-pass and clean, with no recorded challenge.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-routef-k-ledger}]\label{lem:routef-k-ledger}
There is one global $W_{\mathrm{RF}}$ supplied by
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-raw-factor-setting-formation} such that, writing $K$ for (1.6),
$\rho_{\mathrm{fac}}$ for (1.7), and
$\eta_K=\min\{\rho_{\mathrm{fac}},(24K)^{-1},1\}$ for (1.8), $K\ge1$ and $\eta_K>0$
are universal and independent of $n$, amplification level, simple-block count and block
dimensions.  For every $n\ge1$, row-stochastic $Q:\linfd n\to\linfd n$, and
$0\le\eta\le\eta_K$ with $\nrm{Q^2-Q}_{\infty\to\infty}\le\eta$, let
$D:M_n\to\mathbb C^n$ be diagonal extraction, $J:\mathbb C^n\to M_n$ diagonal inclusion,
$Q_{\mathbb C}$ the canonical complex-linear extension, and $\Phi=JQ_{\mathbb C}D$.
Then there are a finite-dimensional unital $C^*$-algebra $B$ and UCP maps
$\Delta:B\to M_n$, $\Upsilon:M_n\to B$ satisfying
\[
 \nrm{\Delta\Upsilon-\Phi}_{\mathrm{cb}},\ \nrm{\Upsilon\Delta-I_B}_{\mathrm{cb}}
 \le K\eta,
 \qquad
 \nrm{\Upsilon_r(\Delta_rX\,\Delta_rY)-XY}\le K\eta\nrm X\nrm Y
\]
for every $r\ge1$ and $X,Y\in M_r(B)$.  The same $Q$ admits a stochastic idempotent $E$
such that
\[
 \nrm{Q-E}_{\infty\to\infty}\le
 \bigl(K+4\sqrt{2K}\bigr)\sqrt\eta.
\]
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
Choose the single global header by Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-scalar-header-positivity}; this
supplies the pre-input, universal $K\ge1$ and $\eta_K>0$.  For arbitrary admissible
$n,Q,\eta$, Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-factor-map-packet} supplies one algebra $B$, its setting
datum, and the serial maps $\Delta',\Delta,\Upsilon',\Upsilon$.  On that very packet,
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-factor-estimate-packet} supplies the two completely bounded factor
estimates, the amplified multiplicative estimate, and all threshold inequalities.
The $r=1$ specialization of the latter is the level-one hypothesis required by
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-f2-positive-unital-compression}.  That lemma proves that $B$ is
commutative, selects $k\ge1$ and a unital $*$-isomorphism $\mathbb C^k\to B$, and produces
positive unital maps $A:\linfd k\to\linfd n$ and $M:\linfd n\to\linfd k$ with
$\nrm{Q-AM}_{\infty\to\infty}\le K\eta$,
$\nrm{QA-A}_{\infty\to\infty}\le2K\eta$, and
$\nrm{Ax}_\infty\ge(1-3K\eta)\nrm{x}_\infty$.  Since $3K\eta\le1/8<1$,
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-f3-retract-defect} turns these into
$\nrm{MA-I_k}_{\infty\to\infty}\le3K\eta/(1-3K\eta)$.  The bounds
$\eta\le\min\{(24K)^{-1},1\}$ now meet the hypotheses of
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-prh-finish}, which returns the stated stochastic idempotent for the
same $Q$ with constant $K+4\sqrt{2K}$.  This seven-node tree validated on its first pass.
It proves the strengthened statement as a new obligation: no status was inherited from
the narrower \texttt{proved-mod-audit} W74F paper ledger that this registry result supersedes.
