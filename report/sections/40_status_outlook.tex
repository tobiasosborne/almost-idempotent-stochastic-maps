\subsection*{What is next, and what it is not}

With \S\ref{sec:hcb} the H-CB tier is closed, and with \S\ref{sec:extcb} so is the EXT
tier: \texttt{conj-extcb} is \texttt{proved} with \texttt{af: validated} and is reproduced
above as Lemma~\ref{conj:extcb}. What it delivers is one extension step --- from
$\Mat{r}$ to $\Mat{r+1}$, at one universal accuracy, with one unitary and four fixed
corner maps serving every amplification level. It is not an induction, and nothing here
iterates it.

Beyond the EXT tier lies the assembly, and that is where the live route now stops.

Its registry carrier \texttt{lem-thmainext-conditional} is \texttt{proved-mod-audit} with
\texttt{af: none} --- \textbf{open} at this document's rigour bar, and \textbf{not}
reproduced here. The MAIN-CB assembly rows and the ledger rows are
explicitly \emph{not} in the pipeline and must not be treated as pending detail. They
are \textbf{quarantined} pending a focused repair and a fresh hostile review, along with
the named gap interfaces (exact-target approximation, the polar contract, the MAIN
structure, the ledger domains, and the final bridge); and several external
topology sources are not yet in \texttt{refs/}, which is a provisioning risk, not a proof
step. The unconditional end-to-end constant ledger for Route F remains separate open work.

The honest summary is therefore: the base, the middle and both tier parents of the live
route have been made rigorous here, and the assembly above them is quarantined.
\texttt{op-classical} is \textbf{open}.
