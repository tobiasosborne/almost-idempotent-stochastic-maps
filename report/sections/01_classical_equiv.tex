\section{The signed-stochastic bridge}\label{sec:classical-equiv}

\begin{lemma}[Registry contract for \texttt{lem-classical-equiv}]
\label{lem:classical-equiv}
\contractquote{The signed-idempotent and stochastic-idempotent formulations of classical stability are equivalent up to universal constants: Q row-stochastic with ||Q^2-Q|| <= eta gives P=theta(2Q-1) signed affine retraction with ||P-Q|| <= C eta and neg mass delta <= C eta, and conversely row-normalising p_i^+ gives Q with ||P-Q|| <= 2 delta, ||Q^2-Q|| <= 6 delta+4 delta^2.}
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Status.} \texttt{proved}; \texttt{af: validated}.
This is one of the rigorous in-repo results.

\noindent\textbf{Proof tree.} The registry shard records a 29-node
adversarial tree with root \texttt{validated} and clean taint.  The
human-readable export is
\texttt{proofs/lem-classical-equiv/export.md}; the export also records
the earlier missing-definition challenge and its repair by importing
\texttt{def-negative-mass}.

\noindent\textbf{Role in the chain.} This bridge is the authorized way
to move between \texttt{op-classical}'s stochastic formulation and the
signed exact-idempotent registry.  It is a dependency for the inherited
simplex, rank-one, and approximate-simplex reductions, but those
downstream reductions remain \texttt{proved-mod-audit} unless and until
they clear an L0 gate.
