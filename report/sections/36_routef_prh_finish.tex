\section{The Route F finish edge}\label{sec:routef-prh-finish}

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-routef-prh-finish}]\label{lem:routef-prh-finish}
Let $A:\linfd{k}\to\linfd{n}$ and $M:\linfd{n}\to\linfd{k}$ be positive unital maps and let
$Q:\linfd{n}\to\linfd{n}$ be row-stochastic. If $K\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rpf-window}
  0\;\le\;\defect\;\le\;\min\bigl\{(24K)^{-1},\,1\bigr\},
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rpf-hyp}
  \opnorm{Q-AM}\;\le\;K\defect,
  \qquad
  \opnorm{MA-I}\;\le\;\frac{3K\defect}{1-3K\defect},
\end{equation}
then there is a stochastic idempotent $E$ (\texttt{def-stochastic}) with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rpf-target}
  \opnorm{Q-E}\;\le\;\bigl(K+4\sqrt{2K}\bigr)\sqrt{\defect}.
\end{equation}
\emph{Transcription note.} Both displays in \eqref{eq:rpf-hyp} are \emph{hypotheses}: the
pair $(A,M)$ --- the datum of \texttt{def-positive-approximate-retract} --- is given, and
nothing in this statement, or anywhere in this document, manufactures it from $Q$. The
denominator in \eqref{eq:rpf-hyp} is positive precisely because of the window
\eqref{eq:rpf-window}. The constant in \eqref{eq:rpf-target} is inherited from the
hypothesis constant $K$ and is universal only to the extent that $K$ is; no result
reproduced here fixes a value of $K$.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Route F PRH finish: let A:l-infinity(k)->l-infinity(n) and M:l-infinity(n)->l-infinity(k) be positive unital maps and let Q be row-stochastic; if K >= 1, 0 <= eta <= min{(24*K)^(-1),1}, ||Q-AM||_{infinity->infinity} <= K*eta, and ||MA-I||_{infinity->infinity} <= 3*K*eta/(1-3*K*eta), then there is a stochastic idempotent E with ||Q-E||_{infinity->infinity} <= (K+4*sqrt(2*K))*sqrt(eta).}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The tree has three steps --- a scalar preparation, one import of \texttt{lem-prh}
(\S\ref{sec:prh}) guarded by a dimension split, and a triangle-inequality finish --- and a
synthesis node that assembles them.

\emph{Scalar preparation.} Put $x=3K\defect$ and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rpf-eps}
  \varepsilon:=\frac{3K\defect}{1-3K\defect}=\frac{x}{1-x}.
\end{equation}
The window \eqref{eq:rpf-window} with $K\ge1$ gives $0\le x\le\tfrac18$, hence
$1-x\ge\tfrac78>0$, so \eqref{eq:rpf-eps} is well defined and
\[
  0\;\le\;\varepsilon\;\le\;\tfrac87x\;\le\;\tfrac17\;<\;\tfrac12,
  \qquad
  \varepsilon\;\le\;\tfrac{24}{7}K\defect\;\le\;4K\defect .
\]
Separately, $0\le\defect\le1$ gives $\defect^{2}\le\defect$, i.e.\
$\defect\le\sqrt{\defect}$, and multiplying by $K\ge1$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rpf-root}
  K\defect\;\le\;K\sqrt{\defect}.
\end{equation}
The two bounds on $\varepsilon$ are the whole point of the preparation: the first puts
$\varepsilon$ inside the PRH window $[0,\tfrac12)$, the second is what converts a
$\sqrt{\varepsilon}$ rate into a $\sqrt{\defect}$ rate.

\emph{The PRH import, with an exhaustive dimension split.} By \eqref{eq:rpf-eps} the second
hypothesis of \eqref{eq:rpf-hyp} reads exactly $\opnorm{MA-I_k}\le\varepsilon$ with
$0\le\varepsilon<\tfrac12$, which is the hypothesis of \texttt{lem-prh}. That lemma,
however, is stated for $k,n\ge1$, and the contract above assumes no dimension bound; the
tree therefore splits, and the split is exhaustive. If $k,n\ge1$, then
\texttt{def-positive-approximate-retract} identifies $A$ and $M$ as positive unital maps
with probability-vector rows, every premise of \texttt{lem-prh} is met, and it returns a
stochastic idempotent $E:\linfd{n}\to\linfd{n}$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rpf-prh}
  \opnorm{AM-E}\;\le\;2\sqrt{2\varepsilon}.
\end{equation}
The mixed degenerate cases cannot occur: if $k=0<n$ then each of the $n$ rows of $A$ is the
empty vector, of coordinate sum $0$ rather than $1$, contradicting the probability-row
requirement, and the same argument applied to $M$ excludes $n=0<k$. There remains $k=n=0$,
where $\linfd{0}$ is the zero space, its unique endomorphism is the empty $0\times0$
matrix, which is entrywise nonnegative, fixes the (empty) all-ones vector and is
idempotent --- a stochastic idempotent by \texttt{def-stochastic} --- and satisfies
$AM=E$, so \eqref{eq:rpf-prh} holds with error $0$. In every admissible dimension, then,
such an $E$ exists, and \texttt{lem-prh} is never invoked outside its stated domain.

\emph{The finish.} Writing $Q-E=(Q-AM)+(AM-E)$, the triangle inequality for
$\opnorm{\cdot}$ with the first hypothesis of \eqref{eq:rpf-hyp} and \eqref{eq:rpf-prh}
gives $\opnorm{Q-E}\le K\defect+2\sqrt{2\varepsilon}$. Monotonicity and multiplicativity of
the square root on the nonnegative reals turn $\varepsilon\le4K\defect$ into
$2\sqrt{2\varepsilon}\le2\sqrt{8K\defect}=4\sqrt{2K}\sqrt{\defect}$, and with
\eqref{eq:rpf-root},
\[
  \opnorm{Q-E}\;\le\;K\sqrt{\defect}+4\sqrt{2K}\sqrt{\defect}
  \;=\;\bigl(K+4\sqrt{2K}\bigr)\sqrt{\defect},
\]
which is \eqref{eq:rpf-target}.

challenges were the same defect, raised three times at three heights of the tree: the
import node asserted a direct application of \texttt{lem-prh} while neither the root

was, as written, outside the external's stated domain --- and the $k=n=0$ branch is
admissible under the root's hypotheses. The repair was not to weaken anything but to make
the dichotomy explicit and to discharge the degenerate branch by hand, which is the form
reproduced above. The degenerate case is a formality; the protocol nonetheless refused the
import until it was discharged, which is exactly the guard one wants on a
hypothesis-carrying external. The registry contract was not amended.

\noindent\textbf{What this closes, and what it does not.} This is the validated conditional PRH finish edge within Route F
and it is now \texttt{af}-validated, but it is an \emph{implication}. Its antecedent --- a
positive unital pair $(A,M)$ with $\opnorm{Q-AM}\le K\defect$ and
$\opnorm{MA-I}\le3K\defect/(1-3K\defect)$ for a dimension-free $K$ --- is supplied by no
result reproduced in this document. The registry rows that would supply it are
\texttt{lem-routef-f2-positive-unital-compression} (the positive unital pair),
\texttt{lem-routef-f3-retract-defect} (the retract-defect bound, whose conclusion is
literally the second hypothesis here), \texttt{lem-routef-k-ledger} (the dimension-free
$K$), together with \texttt{lem-routef-ai-defect-linearization} and
\texttt{lem-routef-functional-calculus-closeness} upstream of those, and the assembly
carrier \texttt{lem-thmainext-conditional}; every one of them is \texttt{proved-mod-audit}
with \texttt{af: none} --- \textbf{open} at this document's rigour bar and \textbf{not}
reproduced here (\S\ref{sec:status-outlook}). Consequently nothing here yields an
unconditional $\sqrt{\defect}$ bound for a row-stochastic $Q$, and \texttt{op-classical} is
\textbf{open}. The shape of \eqref{eq:rpf-target} is the shape the target asks for; the
antecedent is the whole remaining problem on this route.
