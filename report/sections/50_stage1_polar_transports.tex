\section{Parameterized transports of the Stage-1 polar layer}\label{sec:stage1-polar-transports}

This section and the next (\S\ref{sec:stage1-polar-transports-ii}) reproduce six
\texttt{af}-validated \emph{transport} rows, which carry six fixed-constant Stage-1 polar
lemmas of \S\ref{sec:stage1-polar-charts}--\S\ref{sec:stage1-smooth-polar} to the
\emph{parameterized} form of Definition~\ref{def:stage1-polar-witness-data}: throughout, $W$
is a Stage-1 polar witness datum with coefficient fields
$C_{\mathrm{rect}},C_{\mathrm{ch}},C_{\mathrm{pol}},C_{\mathrm{grp}},C_{\mathrm{path}},C_{\mathrm{der}}$
and margin fields $e_{\mathrm{rect}},\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}},\kappa_{\mathrm{pol}},\kappa_{\mathrm{der}}$
(the four derived scales of $W$ play no role in these six rows). Each row asserts universal
\emph{thresholds} --- coefficient floors $C^{0}_{\ast}$ and margin caps $\kappa^{0}_{\ast}$
--- such that \emph{every} tuple $W$ whose coefficients dominate the floors and whose
margins are dominated by the caps witnesses the parent conclusion at its own fields. All
ambient vocabulary ($\Herm$, $\aHerm$, $\Unit$, $\Unitc{\cdot}$, $\Unito{\cdot}$,
$\Lmul{V}$, $\phi_V$) is that of Definition~\ref{def:approximate-unitary-space}, never
restated.

\paragraph{The common transport skeleton.} Every tree in the family shares one skeleton,
stated once. (i) Instantiate the universal witnesses of the fixed-constant parent lemma as
the thresholds. (ii) Derive $\ecs_r\ge0$ from the product-norm axiom of
Definition~\ref{def:epsilon-cstar-algebra} at $x=y=J$: exact unitality and $\nrm{J}=1$ give
$1=\nrm{J\bdot J}\le(1+\ecs_r)$. Hence every guard combination in play
($\ecs_r+\delta$, $\ecs_r\delta+\delta^{2}$, $q+\ecs_rq+q^{2}$, $\ecs_r+r$) is nonnegative.
(iii) Transport each scalar guard by monotonicity: a coefficient only \emph{shrinks} from
the field of $W$ down to the threshold and a margin only \emph{grows}, so, the multiplied
quantities being nonnegative, the assumed field guard implies the parent's witness guard
(e.g.\ $\hat C(\ecs_r+\delta)\le C_{\mathrm{ch}}(\ecs_r+\delta)\le\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}\le\hat\kappa$).
(iv) Apply the parent lemma. (v) Weaken every conclusion coefficient from the witness back
up to the field, again by nonnegativity, using also that the defect sets are monotone in the
radius: for $r\le s$, $\Unitc{r}\subseteq\Unitc{s}$ and $\Unito{r}\subseteq\Unito{s}$ (the
right-inverse clause is unchanged and the defect bound only relaxes). Existence,
uniqueness, regularity, coverage and equivariance clauses carry over verbatim.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-stage1-rectified-cstar-transport}]\label{lem:stage1-rectified-cstar-transport}
There exist $C^{0}_{\mathrm{rect}}\ge1$ and $e^{0}_{\mathrm{rect}}\in(0,1/C^{0}_{\mathrm{rect}}]$
such that for every witness datum $W$ with $C_{\mathrm{rect}}\ge C^{0}_{\mathrm{rect}}$ and
$0<e_{\mathrm{rect}}\le\min\{e^{0}_{\mathrm{rect}},1/C_{\mathrm{rect}}\}$, every
finite-dimensional $\ecs_X$-$C^*$-algebra with $0\le\ecs_X\le e_{\mathrm{rect}}$ admits, on
the same involutive normed space, a bilinear product $\bdot$ and an element $J=J\dg$ for
which $(\calX,J,\bdot,\dagger)$ satisfies every exact-unit $\ecs_r$-$C^*$-algebra axiom of
Definition~\ref{def:epsilon-cstar-algebra}, including $\nrm{J}=1$, where
$\ecs_r=C_{\mathrm{rect}}\ecs_X$, and for every $x,y\in\calX$,
$\nrm{J-I_X}\le C_{\mathrm{rect}}\ecs_X$ and
$\nrm{x\bdot y-xy}\le C_{\mathrm{rect}}\ecs_X\nrm{x}\nrm{y}$.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Parameterized rectification transport: there exist C_rect^0 >= 1 and e_rect^0 in (0, 1/C_rect^0] such that, for every def-stage1-polar-witness-data tuple W with C_rect >= C_rect^0 and 0 < e_rect <= min{e_rect^0, 1/C_rect}, for every finite-dimensional epsilon_X-C*-algebra (calX, I_X, ., dagger) with 0 <= epsilon_X <= e_rect, there are on the same involutive normed space a bilinear product bold-dot and an element J = J^dagger for which (calX, J, bold-dot, dagger) satisfies every exact-unit epsilon_r-C*-algebra axiom of def-epsilon-cstar-algebra, including ||J|| = 1, where epsilon_r = C_rect*epsilon_X, and for every x, y in calX, ||J - I_X|| <= C_rect*epsilon_X and ||x bold-dot y - xy|| <= C_rect*epsilon_X*||x||*||y||.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The skeleton, seeded by Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1-rectified-cstar-control} with witnesses
$(c,e)$ and thresholds $C^{0}_{\mathrm{rect}}=c$, $e^{0}_{\mathrm{rect}}=e$, plus one extra
node: \emph{parameter monotonicity of the axiom list itself}. If $0\le\alpha\le\beta$ and
$(\calX,J,\bdot,\dagger)$ satisfies every exact-unit $\alpha$-$C^*$-algebra axiom, then it
satisfies every $\beta$-axiom: $\nrm{x\bdot y}\le(1+\alpha)\nrm{x}\nrm{y}\le(1+\beta)\nrm{x}\nrm{y}$,
the associator bound $\alpha\nrm{x}\nrm{y}\nrm{z}$ is at most $\beta\nrm{x}\nrm{y}\nrm{z}$,
and $\nrm{x\dg\bdot x}\ge(1-\alpha)\nrm{x}^{2}\ge(1-\beta)\nrm{x}^{2}$; every remaining
axiom is parameter-free. Since $\ecs_X\le e_{\mathrm{rect}}\le e$, the parent applies and
produces the axioms at $\alpha=c\,\ecs_X$; monotonicity passes to
$\beta=C_{\mathrm{rect}}\ecs_X\ge\alpha$, and $c\le C_{\mathrm{rect}}$ weakens the two
closeness estimates.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-stage1-unitary-graph-transport}]\label{lem:stage1-unitary-graph-transport}
There exist $C^{0}_{\mathrm{ch}}\ge1$ and $\kappa^{0}_{\mathrm{ch}}\in(0,\tfrac12]$ such
that for every witness datum $W$ with $C_{\mathrm{ch}}\ge C^{0}_{\mathrm{ch}}$ and
$0<\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}\le\kappa^{0}_{\mathrm{ch}}$, for every finite-dimensional
exact-unit $\ecs_r$-$C^*$-algebra, every $\delta>0$ with
$C_{\mathrm{ch}}(\ecs_r+\delta)\le\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$, every $V\in\Unitc{\delta}$ and
every $A^{\parallel}\in B^{\aHerm}_{2\delta}(0)$, there is a unique
$g_V(A^{\parallel})\in B^{\Herm}_{2\delta}(0)$ with
$f_V(A^{\parallel}+g_V(A^{\parallel}))=0$ --- $f_V$ exactly as in \eqref{eq:s1gc-f} ---
the element $V\bdot(J+A^{\parallel}+g_V(A^{\parallel}))$ lies in $\Unit$, the two graph
estimates \eqref{eq:s1gc-bounds} and the strict normal-derivative bound
\eqref{eq:s1gc-normal} hold with the field $C_{\mathrm{ch}}$ of $W$ in place of the
universal constant, and the resulting $C^{1}$ graph charts cover $\Unit$.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Parameterized unitary-graph transport: there exist C_ch^0 >= 1 and kappa_ch^0 in (0, 1/2] such that, for every def-stage1-polar-witness-data tuple W with C_ch >= C_ch^0 and 0 < kappa_ch <= kappa_ch^0, for every finite-dimensional exact-unit epsilon_r-C*-algebra (calX, J, bold-dot, dagger), every delta > 0 with C_ch*(epsilon_r + delta) <= kappa_ch, every V in calUbar_delta, and every A^par in B^{icalH}_{2delta}(0), there is a unique g_V(A^par) in B^{calH}_{2delta}(0) such that f_V(A^par + g_V(A^par)) = 0, where f_V(A) = (1/2)*(((J + A^dagger) bold-dot V^dagger) bold-dot (V bold-dot (J + A)) - J), the element V bold-dot (J + A^par + g_V(A^par)) lies in calU, ||g_V(A^par) + (1/2)*(V^dagger bold-dot V - J)|| <= C_ch*(epsilon_r*delta + delta^2), ||Dg_V(A^par)|| <= C_ch*(epsilon_r + delta), and ||D_{A^perp} f_V(A^par + g_V(A^par)) - I_{calH}|| <= C_ch*(epsilon_r + delta) < 1, and these C^1 graph charts cover calU.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The skeleton verbatim, seeded by Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1-unitary-graph-control} with
witnesses $(\hat C,\hat\kappa)$. With $s=\ecs_r+\delta$ and $q=\ecs_r\delta+\delta^{2}=\delta s$,
step (ii) gives $s,q>0$; step (iii) is
$\hat Cs\le C_{\mathrm{ch}}s\le\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}\le\hat\kappa$; the parent supplies the
unique $g_V$, membership in $\Unit$, cover, and the three bounds at $\hat C$; step (v)
weakens them to $C_{\mathrm{ch}}q$, $C_{\mathrm{ch}}s$, $C_{\mathrm{ch}}s$, and the strict
clause is
$C_{\mathrm{ch}}s\le\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}\le\kappa^{0}_{\mathrm{ch}}\le\tfrac12<1$.

\begin{conjecture}[\texttt{lem-stage1-maurer-cartan-transport} --- proof retracted 2026-07-28]\label{lem:stage1-maurer-cartan-transport}
There exist $C^{0}_{\mathrm{ch}}\ge1$ and $\kappa^{0}_{\mathrm{ch}}\in(0,\tfrac12]$ such
that for every witness datum $W$ with $C_{\mathrm{ch}}\ge C^{0}_{\mathrm{ch}}$ and
$0<\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}\le\kappa^{0}_{\mathrm{ch}}$, for every finite-dimensional
exact-unit $\ecs_r$-$C^*$-algebra, every $\delta>0$ with
$C_{\mathrm{ch}}(\ecs_r+\delta)\le\kappa_{\mathrm{ch}}$, and every family
$g=(g_U)_{U\in\Unit}$ of $C^{1}$ maps $g_U:B^{\aHerm}_{2\delta}(0)\to B^{\Herm}_{2\delta}(0)$
such that, for every $U$ and $A^{\parallel}$, $g_U(A^{\parallel})$ is the \emph{unique}
element of $B^{\Herm}_{2\delta}(0)$ with $f_U(A^{\parallel}+g_U(A^{\parallel}))=0$: every
tangent space $T_U\Unit$ is the image of $\Lmul{U}(I+Dg_U(0)):\aHerm\to\calX$, and
$\omega_U(Z)=(\Lmul{U}^{-1}Z)^{\parallel}$ is a global $C^{1}$ bundle trivialization with
distortion at most $1+C_{\mathrm{ch}}\ecs_r$, satisfying $\omega_{cU}(cZ)=\omega_U(Z)$ and
$\omega_U(iU)=iJ$ for every $c\in U(1)$.
\emph{Transcription note.} Unlike Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1-maurer-cartan-trivialization},
whose graph maps are the distinguished family supplied by
Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1-unitary-graph-control}, this row quantifies over \emph{any} family
with the displayed uniqueness property.

\end{conjecture}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The skeleton seeded by Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1-maurer-cartan-trivialization} with witnesses
$(\bar C,\bar\kappa)$, plus a genuine extra obligation: the arbitrary family $g$ cannot
simply be \emph{identified} with the parent's distinguished family, because the registered
external does not export that family's domain/codomain typing (the tree records this as a
conditional bridge only). Instead, the tangent formula is re-derived for $g$ itself. From
the guard, $\ecs_r<\tfrac12$; if $X\dg\bdot X=J$ then the $C^*$-lower bound, approximate
associativity and exact unitality give
$\nrm{\Lmul{X\dg}\Lmul{X}Y-Y}\le\tfrac{\ecs_r}{1-\ecs_r}\nrm{Y}$ with coefficient $<1$, so
$\Lmul{X}$ is injective, hence bijective in finite dimension, and $X$ has a right inverse:
\emph{every exact zero of the displayed $f_U$-equation lies in $\Unit$}. Consequently, for
$B\in\aHerm$ the curve $t\mapsto U\bdot(J+tB+g_U(tB))$ stays in $\Unit$ (using $g_U(0)=0$,
forced by uniqueness at $f_U(0)=0$) and differentiates at $0$ to $\Lmul{U}(B+Dg_U(0)B)$,
so $\operatorname{im}\Lmul{U}(I+Dg_U(0))\subseteq T_U\Unit$. The parent's fibrewise
isomorphism $\omega_U$ gives $\dim T_U\Unit=\dim\aHerm$ and
$\omega_U(\Lmul{U}(B+Dg_U(0)B))=B$ (the Hermitian part projects away), so the inclusion is
an equality of finite-dimensional spaces. The $\omega$, distortion and equivariance
conclusions of the parent mention no graph family and transfer unchanged; step (v) weakens
the distortion from $1+\bar C\ecs_r$ to $1+C_{\mathrm{ch}}\ecs_r$.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-stage1-polar-retraction-transport}]\label{lem:stage1-polar-retraction-transport}
There exist $C^{0}_{\mathrm{pol}}\ge1$ and $\kappa^{0}_{\mathrm{pol}}\in(0,\tfrac12]$ such
that for every witness datum $W$ with $C_{\mathrm{pol}}\ge C^{0}_{\mathrm{pol}}$ and
$0<\kappa_{\mathrm{pol}}\le\kappa^{0}_{\mathrm{pol}}$, for every finite-dimensional
exact-unit $\ecs_r$-$C^*$-algebra and every $\delta>0$ with
$C_{\mathrm{pol}}(\ecs_r+\delta)\le\kappa_{\mathrm{pol}}$, the map
$\Pi_\delta(U,H)=U\bdot H$ is a $C^{1}$ diffeomorphism from $\Unit\times B^{\Herm}_{\delta}(J)$
onto the open set $S_\delta:=\Pi_\delta(\Unit\times B^{\Herm}_{\delta}(J))$, its inverse
$(u_\delta,h_\delta)$ obeys the normalisation \eqref{eq:s1pr-inverse}, and the sandwich
\eqref{eq:s1pr-sandwich} holds with the field $C_{\mathrm{pol}}$ of $W$.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Parameterized polar-retraction transport: there exist C_pol^0 >= 1 and kappa_pol^0 in (0, 1/2] such that, for every def-stage1-polar-witness-data tuple W with C_pol >= C_pol^0 and 0 < kappa_pol <= kappa_pol^0, for every finite-dimensional exact-unit epsilon_r-C*-algebra and every delta > 0 with C_pol*(epsilon_r + delta) <= kappa_pol, the map Pi_delta: calU x B^{calH}_delta(J) -> calX, Pi_delta(U, H) = U bold-dot H, is a C^1 diffeomorphism onto the open set S_delta := Pi_delta(calU x B^{calH}_delta(J)), its inverse (u_delta, h_delta): S_delta -> calU x B^{calH}_delta(J) satisfies X = u_delta(X) bold-dot h_delta(X), u_delta(U) = U, and h_delta(U) = J for every X in S_delta and U in calU, and calU_{delta - C_pol*(epsilon_r*delta + delta^2)} subseteq S_delta subseteq calU_{delta + C_pol*(epsilon_r*delta + delta^2)}.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The skeleton verbatim (the shortest tree of the family), seeded by
Lemma~\ref{lem:stage1-polar-retraction} with witnesses $(C_*,\kappa_*)$. The
diffeomorphism, openness and normalisation clauses are coefficient-free and transfer
unchanged; only the sandwich is weakened: with $A=\ecs_r\delta+\delta^{2}\ge0$ and
$C_*\le C_{\mathrm{pol}}$, one has $\delta-C_{\mathrm{pol}}A\le\delta-C_*A$ and
$\delta+C_*A\le\delta+C_{\mathrm{pol}}A$, so radius monotonicity of $\Unito{\cdot}$ gives
$\Unito{\delta-C_{\mathrm{pol}}A}\subseteq\Unito{\delta-C_*A}\subseteq S_\delta
\subseteq\Unito{\delta+C_*A}\subseteq\Unito{\delta+C_{\mathrm{pol}}A}$.
