\section{The discharge of \texttt{op-classical}: the root theorem at the \texttt{af}-validated rung}\label{sec:op-classical-discharge}

This section records the discharged root of the classical upper-bound campaign.  Its
validated tree is deliberately short: the quantitative work has already been completed by
the Route-F assembly, and the root imports that conclusion and closes the remaining
vocabulary interface.  The statement is the upper stability bound only; it contains no
claim of square-root sharpness.

\begin{theorem}[\texttt{op-classical}]\label{op:classical}
There are universal constants $\eta_{0},C>0$, independent of the dimension $n$, such that
every row-stochastic matrix $Q$ satisfying
\begin{equation}
  \opnorm{Q^{2}-Q}\le \eta\le \eta_{0}
\end{equation}
admits a stochastic idempotent $E$ for which
\begin{equation}
  \opnorm{Q-E}\le C\sqrt{\eta}.
\end{equation}
This is the commutative case of \texttt{op-npps}; the general \texttt{op-npps} problem is out of scope and is not a T0 result in this repo.
\end{theorem}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Classical projection stability: there are universal eta_0,C>0 (n-free) such that every row-stochastic Q with ||Q^2-Q||_{inf->inf} <= eta <= eta_0 admits a stochastic idempotent E with ||Q-E||_{inf->inf} <= C sqrt(eta) (the commutative case of op-npps).}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The tree has one substantive import and one vocabulary branch, with five live nodes in
all.  It constructs no replacement constants at the root.  Instead, node~1.1 invokes
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-f0-assembly}, which supplies universal constants
$\eta_{0},C>0$ independent of $n$ for every row-stochastic map
$Q:\ell^{\infty}_{n}\to\ell^{\infty}_{n}$ satisfying
$\nrm{Q^{2}-Q}_{\infty\to\infty}\le\eta\le\eta_{0}$.  That external conclusion
produces a stochastic idempotent $E$ with
$\nrm{Q-E}_{\infty\to\infty}\le C\sqrt{\eta}$.  Thus the witness pair
$(\eta_{0},C)$ and the final estimate are taken over unchanged; there is no threshold
shrinkage, constant loss, or auxiliary approximation in this root tree.

Node~1.2 checks that this imported conclusion is literally the conclusion requested here.
Its first child uses Definition~\ref{def:stochastic} and
Definition~\ref{def:almost-idempotent} to identify a row-stochastic matrix with the
corresponding operator on $\ell^{\infty}_{n}$, and to identify the two notations
$\nrm{\cdot}_{\mathrm{inf}\to\mathrm{inf}}$ and
$\nrm{\cdot}_{\infty\to\infty}$ for the same induced norm.  Under that identification,
``independent of $n$'' is exactly ``$n$-free'', while the imported output is exactly a
stochastic idempotent.  The second child uses
Definition~\ref{def:near-positive-projection} only to read the parenthetical description
``the commutative case of \texttt{op-npps}'': it adds neither a hypothesis nor a further
conclusion.  Combining the imported Route-F assertion with these two vocabulary matches
gives the registry contract verbatim.
