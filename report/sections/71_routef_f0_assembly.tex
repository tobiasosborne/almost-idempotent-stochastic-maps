\section{The F0 assembly and the conditional main-extension theorem}\label{sec:routef-f0-assembly}

This section records two terminal interfaces of the validated assembly.  The first
specializes the strengthened Route-F ledger to the stochastic conclusion, with no
repetition of the factorization below it.  The second hides the MAIN-CB witness ledger
behind two universal constants.  The relevant vocabulary is that of
Definition~\ref{def:stochastic}, Definition~\ref{def:almost-idempotent},
Definition~\ref{def:extended-epsilon-cstar-algebra}, and
Definition~\ref{def:extended-delta-inclusion}; none is restated here.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-routef-f0-assembly}]\label{lem:routef-f0-assembly}
There are universal constants $\eta_{0},C>0$, independent of $n$, such that the
following holds.  Let $n\ge1$, let
$Q:\ell_{\infty}^{n}\to\ell_{\infty}^{n}$ be row-stochastic, and suppose that
$0\le\eta\le\eta_{0}$ and
\[
  \nrm{Q^{2}-Q}_{\infty\to\infty}\le\eta .
\]
Let $D:M_{n}\to\mathbb C^{n}$ be diagonal extraction onto the complex diagonal
algebra $\mathbb C^{n}=\ell_{\infty}^{n}(\mathbb C)$, let
$J:\mathbb C^{n}\to M_{n}$ be the diagonal inclusion, let
$Q_{\mathbb C}:\mathbb C^{n}\to\mathbb C^{n}$ be the canonical complex-linear
extension of $Q$, and put $\Phi=JQ_{\mathbb C}D$.  Then the same $Q$ admits a
stochastic idempotent $E$ satisfying
\[
  \nrm{Q-E}_{\infty\to\infty}\le C\sqrt\eta .
\]
For the universal $K$ and $\eta_{K}$ supplied by
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-k-ledger}, one may take
\[
  \eta_{0}=\eta_{K},
  \qquad
  C=K+4\sqrt{2K}.
\]
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Route F F0 assembly: there are universal eta_0,C > 0, independent of n, such that for every n >= 1, every row-stochastic Q: l_inf^n -> l_inf^n, and every 0 <= eta <= eta_0 with ||Q^2-Q||_{infinity->infinity} <= eta, let D: M_n -> C^n be diagonal extraction onto the complex diagonal algebra C^n = l_inf^n(C), let J: C^n -> M_n be the diagonal inclusion, let Q_C: C^n -> C^n be the canonical complex-linear extension of Q, and put Phi := J Q_C D; then the same Q admits a stochastic idempotent E satisfying ||Q-E||_{infinity->infinity} <= C*sqrt(eta); for the universal K and eta_K supplied by lem-routef-k-ledger, one may take eta_0 := eta_K and C := K+4*sqrt(2*K).}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The tree first invokes Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-k-ledger} and fixes its universal
scalars $K$ and $\eta_{K}$.  That imported row gives $K\ge1$ and
$\eta_{K}>0$, with both scalars independent of $n$, amplification level,
simple-block count, and block dimensions.  Define
$\eta_{0}:=\eta_{K}$ and $C:=K+4\sqrt{2K}$.  Positivity of $\eta_{0}$ is
immediate.  Since $K\ge1$, the principal square root $\sqrt{2K}$ is
nonnegative, and therefore $C\ge K\ge1>0$.  Both new constants are fixed
expressions in the imported universal scalars, so they retain the required
dimension independence.

Now fix arbitrary $n,Q,\eta$ satisfying the hypotheses of the statement, and
form $D,J,Q_{\mathbb C}$ and $\Phi$ exactly as displayed there.  The identity
$\eta_{0}=\eta_{K}$ changes the bound $0\le\eta\le\eta_{0}$ into the
smallness hypothesis required by the imported ledger; every other hypothesis,
including the canonical complex-linear typing of $Q_{\mathbb C}$, is literally
the parent row's input.  Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-k-ledger} therefore returns, for
the same $Q$, a stochastic idempotent $E$ with
\[
  \nrm{Q-E}_{\infty\to\infty}
  \le \bigl(K+4\sqrt{2K}\bigr)\sqrt\eta
  =C\sqrt\eta .
\]
This substitution is the whole assembly: the factor maps, their estimates, and
the stochastic finish have already been discharged inside the single imported
ledger and are not counted again at this interface.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-thmainext-conditional}]\label{lem:thmainext-conditional}
There are universal constants $C_{E}<\infty$ and $\varepsilon_{E}>0$ such that
every finite-dimensional extended $\varepsilon$-$C^{*}$-algebra $\mathcal A$,
for $0\le\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_{E}$, is carried by one extended
$C_{E}\varepsilon$-isomorphism
\[
  v:\mathcal B\longrightarrow\mathcal A
\]
from a finite-dimensional $C^{*}$-algebra $\mathcal B$.  The constants are
independent of dimension, amplification level, and block data.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Extended th_main_ext assembly: there are universal C_E < infinity and epsilon_E > 0 such that every finite-dimensional extended epsilon-C*-algebra A, for 0 <= epsilon <= epsilon_E, is carried by one extended C_E*epsilon-isomorphism v:B->A from a finite-dimensional C*-algebra, with constants independent of dimension, amplification level, and block data.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
Invoke Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-structural-assembly} and fix its universal MAIN-CB
witness-ledger datum $W$.  The tree chooses the two witnesses
\[
  C_{E}:=W.c0_{\mathrm{cb}}W.K_{\mathrm{call}},
  \qquad
  \varepsilon_{E}:=W.\varepsilon_{\mathrm{MAIN}}.
\]
The final clause of the imported assembly states that these quantities are
finite, positive, and universal, and that they are independent of dimension,
amplification level, block count, and block dimensions.  Thus they have exactly
the scalar properties claimed at the root.

Let $\mathcal A$ be an arbitrary finite-dimensional extended
$\varepsilon$-$C^{*}$-algebra with
$0\le\varepsilon\le\varepsilon_{E}$.  Since
$\varepsilon_{E}=W.\varepsilon_{\mathrm{MAIN}}$, the structural assembly
supplies
\[
  \mathcal B=\bigoplus_{\mathcal C}M_{|\mathcal C|}
\]
and a single extended
$W.c0_{\mathrm{cb}}W.K_{\mathrm{call}}\varepsilon$-isomorphism
$v:\mathcal B\to\mathcal A$.  It also supplies a unit estimate, but that extra
conclusion is not used by this interface.  Substitution of the definition of
$C_{E}$ turns the displayed map into the required extended
$C_{E}\varepsilon$-isomorphism.

Finally, Definition~\ref{def:extended-delta-inclusion} says that this is one
bijective linear map whose every amplification is a
$C_{E}\varepsilon$-inclusion.  Hence the same $v$ works at all amplification
levels with the same universal constant.  Together with the independence
already carried by $W$, and the arbitrariness of $\mathcal A$ and
$\varepsilon$, this proves the claimed interface.
