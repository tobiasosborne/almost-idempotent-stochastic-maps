\section{Zero-sum fan payment}\label{sec:fan-payment}

\begin{lemma}[Registry contract for \texttt{lem-fan-payment}]
\label{lem:fan-payment}
\contractquote{Zero-sum fan payment: let (w_1, p_1), ..., (w_m, p_m) be a finite family with vectors w_i in R^d and weights p_i > 0 satisfying sum_i p_i = 1, such that every w_i has coordinate sum zero (sum_l w_i(l) = 0) and the weighted barycenter is zero (sum_i p_i w_i = 0); write n(w) = sum_l max(-w(l), 0); then min over i* in {1, ..., m} of sum_i p_i n(w_i - w_{i*}) <= 2 * sum_i p_i n(w_i).}
\end{lemma}

This is one of the rigorous in-repo results.

\noindent\textbf{Role in the chain.} The all-mass fan payment lemma is
the discrete mechanism behind the certified plateau-2 constant on the
reduced fan families of the arm-A campaign: in a reduced edge-fan
chart the anchor-pivot score is exactly the weighted average
$\sum_i p_i\, n(w_i - w_*)$, so this lemma bounds the fan-template
$\Phi/\delta$ by $2$.  The registry shard body additionally records an
exact refutation certificate for the over-broad variant (arbitrary
$w_0$, no zero-sum hypothesis, ratio $5$); that certificate is wave
material and is not elevated here.
