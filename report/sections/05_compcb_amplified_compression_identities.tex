\section{Idempotence and adjoint symmetry of amplified compressions}\label{sec:compcb-amplified-compression-identities}

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities}]\label{lem:compcb-amplified-compression-identities}
There is a universal $\comb_{\mathrm{cmp}}>0$ such that, whenever $\mathcal A$ is an
extended $\ecs$-$C^*$-algebra, $\comb=\dproj+\ecs\le \comb_{\mathrm{cmp}}$, $P,Q$ are
$\dproj$-projections in $\mathcal A$, $n\ge1$, and $X\in\Amp{n}{\mathcal A}$, one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:aci-both}
  \Co{P_n}{Q_n}^{\,2}=\Co{P_n}{Q_n},
  \qquad
  \Co{P_n}{Q_n}(X)\dg=\Co{Q_n}{P_n}\bigl(X\dg\bigr),
\end{equation}
where $P_n=\ampel{n}{P}$ and $Q_n=\ampel{n}{Q}$.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Amplified compression identities: there is a universal e_cmp > 0 such that, whenever A is an extended epsilon-C*-algebra, e=delta+epsilon <= e_cmp, P,Q are delta-projections in A, n >= 1, and X is in M_n tensor A, one has Co_{P_n,Q_n}^2=Co_{P_n,Q_n} and Co_{P_n,Q_n}(X)^dagger=Co_{Q_n,P_n}(X^dagger), where P_n=I_n tensor P and Q_n=I_n tensor Q.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The argument is short because the amplification lemma has already done the structural
work; what remains is to check that two \emph{exact} level-one identities survive the
passage to matrices.

\emph{Identification.} Take $\comb_{\mathrm{cmp}}$ to be the universal constant supplied
by \texttt{lem-compcb-amplified-compression}. For $\comb\le \comb_{\mathrm{cmp}}$ that
lemma gives $\Co{P_n}{Q_n}=\ampl{n}\Co{P}{Q}$, and --- applied a second time to the
\emph{ordered} pair $(Q,P)$, which is a distinct instance --- also
$\Co{Q_n}{P_n}=\ampl{n}\Co{Q}{P}$. Both amplified compressions are therefore canonical
amplifications of level-one maps.

\emph{The level-one identities.} Writing $T=\Co{P}{Q}$ and $U=\Co{Q}{P}$,
\texttt{def-compressed-corner} states exactly $T^2=T$ and $T(x)\dg=U(x\dg)$ for all
$x\in\mathcal A$. These are equalities, not estimates; no smallness is consumed by them,
and the threshold above is needed only for the identification step.

\emph{Entrywise transfer.} Let $T,U$ be any linear maps on a self-adjoint operator space
satisfying those two identities, and let $T_n=\ampl{n}T$, $U_n=\ampl{n}U$. For
$X=[x_{ij}]$, idempotence is checked slotwise,
\[
  (T_n^2X)_{ij}=T\bigl(T(x_{ij})\bigr)=(T^2)(x_{ij})=T(x_{ij})=(T_nX)_{ij},
\]
so $T_n^2=T_n$. Adjoint symmetry combines the matrix involution with the slotwise action:
the $(i,j)$ entry of $T_n(X)\dg$ is $T(x_{ji})\dg=U\bigl(x_{ji}\dg\bigr)$, while the
$(i,j)$ entry of $U_n(X\dg)$ is $U\bigl((X\dg)_{ij}\bigr)=U\bigl(x_{ji}\dg\bigr)$. The
transposition of indices is what makes the \emph{swap} $\Co{P_n}{Q_n}\mapsto\Co{Q_n}{P_n}$
in \eqref{eq:aci-both} the right statement. Combining the identification with the transfer
gives the lemma.

\emph{A recorded contract amendment.} The export's final node is a contract-reconciliation
step, and it documents why the statement is shaped as it is. The originally codified
contract left the ambient algebra unbound: a challenge observed that the proof used, but
the contract never asserted, that $\mathcal A$ is an extended $\ecs$-$C^*$-algebra with
$P,Q$ in $\mathcal A$. The contract was amended to the prover's corrected root text ---
the version reproduced above --- as a mechanical, verdict-driven typing repair. The added
clauses are the intended and already-used hypotheses of the source argument, so nothing
was strengthened or weakened.
