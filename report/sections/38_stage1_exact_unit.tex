\section{Dimension-free exact-unit rectification}\label{sec:stage1-exact-unit}

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-stage1-exact-unit-rectification}]\label{lem:stage1-exact-unit-rectification}
There are universal constants $C_{\mathrm{unit}}<\infty$ and $\ecs_{\mathrm{unit}}>0$ such
that every finite-dimensional $\ecs_X$-$C^*$-algebra $\mathcal A$
(\texttt{def-epsilon-cstar-algebra}) with $\ecs_X\le \ecs_{\mathrm{unit}}$ admits, on the
same involutive normed space, an exact unit $J$ and a bilinear product $\bdot$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:eur-main}
  \nrm{J-I_X}\;\le\;C_{\mathrm{unit}}\,\ecs_X,
  \qquad
  \nrm{x\bdot y-xy}\;\le\;C_{\mathrm{unit}}\,\ecs_X\nrm{x}\nrm{y}
  \quad(x,y\in\mathcal A).
\end{equation}
\emph{Transcription note.} ``On the same involutive normed space'' is a real restriction and
not a formality: the underlying vector space, its norm and its involution are untouched, and
only the multiplication and the distinguished unit are replaced. ``Exact unit'' has exactly the
meaning of Definition~\ref{def:epsilon-cstar-algebra}; no alternative definition is
introduced here. $I_X$ denotes
the given approximate unit, and the threshold written $\ecs_{\mathrm{unit}}$ here is the
contract's \texttt{e\_unit} --- a ceiling on $\ecs_X$ alone, not on a combined defect. What
the statement asserts about $\bdot$ is exactly
\eqref{eq:eur-main} together with the exactness of $J$: \emph{no} clause claims that
$(\mathcal A,\bdot,J)$ is again an $\ecs'$-$C^*$-algebra, and the validated tree estimates
neither the associativity defect nor the product-norm constant of $\bdot$.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Dimension-free exact-unit rectification: there are universal C_unit < infinity and e_unit > 0 such that every finite-dimensional epsilon_X-C*-algebra with epsilon_X <= e_unit admits on the same involutive normed space an exact unit J and product bold-dot with ||J-I_X|| <= C_unit*epsilon_X and ||x bold-dot y-xy|| <= C_unit*epsilon_X*||x||||y||.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The construction is explicit and the constants are exhibited: the tree closes with the
universal witnesses
\begin{equation}\label{eq:eur-constants}
  \ecs_{\mathrm{unit}}=\tfrac12,\qquad C_{\mathrm{unit}}=8 .
\end{equation}
It proceeds in four steps --- rescale the unit, produce a Hermitian norming functional,
correct the product by three terms of rank one in each argument, and estimate.

\emph{A norm-one self-adjoint unit.} Write $I=I_X$, $a=\nrm{I}$ and $J=I/a$. The
approximate-unit clause of \texttt{def-epsilon-cstar-algebra} gives $\lvert a-1\rvert\le\ecs_X$,
so $\ecs_X\le\tfrac12$ forces $a\ge\tfrac12>0$ and $J$ is defined. Then $\nrm{J}=1$;
$J\dg=J$ because $I\dg=I$ and $a$ is a positive real; and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:eur-J}
  \nrm{J-I}=\lvert 1-a\rvert\le\ecs_X .
\end{equation}
This is the only place the self-adjointness of $J$ is produced, and the next step consumes
it.

\emph{A Hermitian norming functional at $J$.} On the one-dimensional subspace $\mathbb CJ$
define $g(\alpha J)=\alpha$; since $\nrm{\alpha J}=\lvert\alpha\rvert$ this has norm one, and
complex Hahn--Banach extends it to a complex-linear $\psi:\mathcal A\to\mathbb C$ with
$\nrm{\psi}=1$ and $\psi(J)=1$. Put $\psi^{\sharp}(x)=\overline{\psi(x\dg)}$. Because the
involution is conjugate-linear, isometric and bijective, $\psi^{\sharp}$ is again
complex-linear of norm one, and $J\dg=J$ gives
$\psi^{\sharp}(J)=\overline{\psi(J\dg)}=\overline{\psi(J)}=1$. Hence
$\varphi=\tfrac12(\psi+\psi^{\sharp})$ is complex-linear with $\nrm{\varphi}\le1$,
$\varphi(J)=1$ --- so in fact $\nrm{\varphi}=1$, since $1=\lvert\varphi(J)\rvert\le\nrm{\varphi}\nrm{J}$ ---
and is Hermitian:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:eur-phi}
  \varphi(x\dg)=\overline{\varphi(x)},\qquad \varphi(J)=1,\qquad \nrm{\varphi}=1 .
\end{equation}

\emph{The corrected product.} Define, bilinearly,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:eur-product}
  x\bdot y\;:=\;xy+\varphi(x)\bigl(y-Jy\bigr)+\varphi(y)\bigl(x-xJ\bigr)
              -\varphi(x)\varphi(y)\bigl(J-JJ\bigr).
\end{equation}
Substituting $x=J$ and using $\varphi(J)=1$, the first correction term cancels $Jy$ against
$y$ and the last two terms cancel each other, giving $J\bdot y=y$; symmetrically
$x\bdot J=x$. With $\nrm{J}=1$ and $J\dg=J$ this is exactly the exact-unit condition of
\texttt{def-epsilon-cstar-algebra}. The involution is unchanged and remains compatible:
Hermiticity \eqref{eq:eur-phi}, the axiom $(xy)\dg=y\dg x\dg$, $J\dg=J$ and
$(JJ)\dg=JJ$ give $(x\bdot y)\dg=y\dg\bdot x\dg$ term by term, so the construction really
does live on the same involutive normed space.

\emph{The estimate.} For any $z$, the product-norm axiom, \eqref{eq:eur-J} and the
approximate-unit axiom give
\[
  \nrm{Jz-z}\;\le\;\nrm{(J-I)z}+\nrm{Iz-z}
  \;\le\;\bigl((1+\ecs_X)\ecs_X+\ecs_X\bigr)\nrm{z}=(2+\ecs_X)\ecs_X\nrm{z},
\]
and likewise $\nrm{zJ-z}\le(2+\ecs_X)\ecs_X\nrm{z}$; taking $z=J$ and using $\nrm{J}=1$ also
gives $\nrm{J-JJ}\le(2+\ecs_X)\ecs_X$. Since $\nrm{\varphi}=1$, the three correction terms of
\eqref{eq:eur-product} are each bounded by $(2+\ecs_X)\ecs_X\nrm{x}\nrm{y}$, so
\[
  \nrm{x\bdot y-xy}\;\le\;3(2+\ecs_X)\ecs_X\nrm{x}\nrm{y}
  \;\le\;\tfrac{15}{2}\,\ecs_X\nrm{x}\nrm{y}\;\le\;8\,\ecs_X\nrm{x}\nrm{y}
  \qquad\text{for }\ecs_X\le\tfrac12 ,
\]
while \eqref{eq:eur-J} gives $\nrm{J-I_X}\le\ecs_X\le8\ecs_X$. That is \eqref{eq:eur-main}
with the witnesses \eqref{eq:eur-constants}, and it is the root.

\noindent\textbf{Correction of record (proof-level, not contract-level).} The Hahn--Banach
step drew a \emph{gap} challenge with a counterexample. As first written, the node asserted
only that $J$ has norm one and then symmetrised $\psi$; but $\psi^{\sharp}(J)=1$ needs
$J\dg=J$, and without it the symmetrisation can destroy the value at $J$. The verifier's
example: $\mathcal A=\mathbb C$ with $z\dg=\overline z$, $J=i$ and $\psi(z)=-iz$ --- a
complex-linear functional of norm one with $\psi(J)=1$ --- gives $\psi^{\sharp}(z)=iz$,
hence $\psi^{\sharp}(J)=-1$ and $\varphi=0$, so $\varphi(J)=0$; indeed no Hermitian
complex-linear functional takes the value $1$ at $i$. The repair was a child node deriving
$J\dg=J$ from the rescaling step and using it explicitly, which is why the account above
records self-adjointness where it does. The registry contract was not amended.
