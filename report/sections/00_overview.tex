\section{Roadmap and rigour status}\label{sec:overview}

This lab-book tracks the report-facing slice of the registry in
\texttt{argument/}.  The north star is \texttt{op-classical}; its
status is \emph{open}, and the status-ledger anchor is
\ref{op:classical}.  The definitions used by the registry are the
canonical shards \texttt{def-stochastic}, \texttt{def-almost-idempotent},
\texttt{def-near-positive-projection}, \texttt{def-signed-idempotent},
\texttt{def-negative-mass}, \texttt{def-height},
\texttt{def-visible-set}, \texttt{def-invisible-mass}, and
\texttt{def-exposed}.  This report references those identifiers; it
does not introduce parallel definitions.

The rigorous line is deliberately narrow.  A result may be called
rigorous here only when it is byte-matched to a local theorem under
\texttt{refs/}, validated in an \texttt{af} workspace, or Lean-proved.
At the time of this shard, the only registry results reproduced as
rigorous are the seven \texttt{af}-validated entries
\ref{lem:classical-equiv}, \ref{obs:height-collapse},
\ref{lem:mass-split}, \ref{lem:residual-lower},
\ref{lem:residual-upper}, \ref{conj:halo-collapse}, and
\ref{lem:factorization}.  Every other
registry result remains anchored
only in the status ledger, with its lower-rung status left intact.

The working headline is also status-sensitive.  The inherited campaign
distinguishes the realizable-family linear law from the worst-case
sqrt-envelope: the linear relation is the mechanism seen in the
reduction record, while the sqrt scale is the stability target.  The
sharp-exponent obstruction is \texttt{ex-hume}, anchored at
\ref{ex:hume}, but it is still \texttt{proved-mod-audit} here rather
than one of the rigorous results.  The bridge between the signed and
stochastic pictures is the validated result
\ref{lem:classical-equiv}; crossing between those pictures should cite
that registry id rather than re-normalising notation locally.
