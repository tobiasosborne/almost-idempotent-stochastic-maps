\section{Orientation and rigour boundary}\label{sec:overview}

\subsection*{The north star, and its status}

The target of this project is the classical (stochastic) stability statement
\texttt{op-classical}: there are universal constants $\eta_0,C>0$, independent of the
dimension, such that every row-stochastic $Q$ with
$\opnorm{Q^2-Q}\le\defect\le\eta_0$ admits a stochastic idempotent $E$
(\texttt{def-stochastic}) with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ov-target}
  \opnorm{Q-E}\;\le\;C\sqrt{\defect},
\end{equation}
the exponent $\tfrac12$ being sharp.

\textbf{This is OPEN.} Nothing in this document proves it, and nothing in this
document should be read as evidence that it is close to proved. What follows is a
rigorous \emph{partial} record: the components of the currently live route that have
cleared this repository's top in-repo rigour rung.

\subsection*{Two pictures, one bridge}

Two equivalent formulations are in play throughout. In the \emph{stochastic picture}
the object is a genuinely positive row-stochastic $Q$ that is only approximately
idempotent, with defect $\defect$ measured in the $\infty\!\to\!\infty$ map norm
(\texttt{def-almost-idempotent}). In the \emph{signed picture} the object is an exact
signed affine retraction $P$ --- $P\mathbf1=\mathbf1$ and $P^2=P$ \emph{exactly}
(\texttt{def-signed-idempotent}) --- whose failure of positivity is quarantined in the
single scalar $\negmass$, the negative mass (\texttt{def-negative-mass}). The two are
interchangeable up to universal constants, and the licence to cross between them is
\texttt{lem-classical-equiv} (\S\ref{sec:classical-equiv}); a bound must always name
the picture it lives in and cite the bridge when it crosses.

\subsection*{What this report contains}

Four blocks, in dependency order.

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The bridge} (\S\ref{sec:classical-equiv}). \texttt{lem-classical-equiv},
  the signed$\leftrightarrow$stochastic equivalence, with explicit constants in both
  directions.

\item \textbf{Positive-retract hardening} (\S\ref{sec:prh}). \texttt{lem-prh} converts a
  \emph{positive approximate retract} --- a pair of positive unital maps $A,M$ between
  finite commutative $C^*$-algebras whose composite $MA$ is $\varepsilon$-close to the
  identity (\texttt{def-positive-approximate-retract}) --- into an exact stochastic
  idempotent at distance $2\sqrt{2\varepsilon}$. It is the step that turns approximate
  retraction data into the exact object \eqref{eq:ov-target} asks for, at the sharp
  square-root rate.

\item \textbf{The COMP tier} (\S\S\ref{sec:compcb-amplification-naturality}--\ref{sec:compcb-single-compression-transfer},
  interleaved). This tier builds, over an \emph{extended} $\ecs$-$C^*$-algebra
  (\texttt{def-extended-epsilon-cstar-algebra}), the approximate corner calculus of
  $\dproj$-projections: the compressed corners $\Cnr{P}{Q}$, the compression maps
  $\Co{P}{Q}$ and the compressed product (\texttt{def-compressed-corner}). Its content
  is that all of this is \emph{matrix-uniform}: every estimate holds at every
  amplification level $n$ with one constant. The keystones are the amplification
  identity $\ampl{n}\Co{P}{Q}=\Co{\ampel{n}{P}}{\ampel{n}{Q}}$, the uniform
  rectangular-product estimate, and the statement that a nonvanishing corner $\Cnrs{P}$
  is itself an \emph{extended} $O(\comb)$-$C^*$-algebra --- the object the next tier
  needs.

\item \textbf{The H-CB tier} (\S\S\ref{sec:hcb2-amplified-adjointness}--\ref{sec:hcb4-canonical-inverse},
  interleaved). Over a closed H-CB datum (\texttt{def-hcb-datum}) this tier establishes
  the amplified estimates for the map $\Ha{Q}{P}{R}$ of \texttt{def-ha-map}: the
  column-Hilbert comparison, the variational identity that defines it, its column
  action, exact adjointness, its multiplicative defect, the diagonal unit and
  upper-norm estimates, and --- under explicit level-one hypotheses --- the diagonal
  lower modulus and the diagonal inverse bound, all uniform in $n$.

\item \textbf{The H-CB parent contract and EXT tier} (\S\ref{sec:hcb} and
  \S\S\ref{sec:extcb-one-dimensional-corners}--\ref{sec:extcb}). This tier develops
  corner arithmetic through a one-dimensional index, the dimension selection $(r,1)$
  of an EXT-CB datum, the four-corner merge, and the EXT parent that adds one dimension
  to a level-one datum.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{The honesty boundary}

Three rules govern what may appear here.

\emph{Only af-validated results are stated as lemmas.} Every numbered lemma below is a
registry result whose adversarial proof tree has root node \texttt{validated} with clean
taint, produced by fresh \texttt{codex} provers and \emph{separate} fresh per-node
verifiers; the orchestrator never judged a proof. Each shard records the node count and
names its workspace. Nothing else in the registry --- the inherited
\texttt{proved-mod-audit} transcriptions, the \texttt{conjecture} rows, the
\texttt{numerical} evidence, the \texttt{heuristic} arguments --- is restated here as a
theorem; those keep their tags in \texttt{argument/} and are surveyed, without promotion,
in \S\ref{sec:status-outlook}.

\emph{Every lemma is exactly as strong as its registry contract.} Each statement is a
typeset transcription of the one-line \texttt{contract} of its \texttt{argument/} shard,
reproduced byte-verbatim beneath it. Where a contract is deliberately conditional or
one-sided --- the two-sided compressed-unit bound only for \emph{nonvanishing}
projections, the level-one hypotheses of the inverse tier --- that shape is load-bearing
and is preserved. In particular a level-one conclusion is never glossed as a
matrix-uniform one.

\emph{No definition is restated.} Terms resolve to their canonical
\texttt{definitions/} shards, cited by \texttt{def-} identifier. Where a proof tree
consumed a byte-matched auxiliary registered inside its own \texttt{af} workspace ---
the $\ecs$-$C^*$ norm axioms, the compressed-product display, the one-dimensional
nonvanishing bridge --- it is named as such, and identified as a workspace external
rather than a registry definition.

\subsection*{What is deliberately absent}

No claim, direct or implied, that \texttt{op-classical} follows. The H-CB tier feeds the
parent contract \texttt{conj-hcb} (\S\ref{sec:hcb}), and that in turn feeds the EXT
parent \texttt{conj-extcb} (\S\ref{sec:extcb}); both are \texttt{proved} with
\texttt{af: validated}. What is open beyond them is the assembly, whose registry carrier
\texttt{lem-thmainext-conditional} is \texttt{proved-mod-audit} with \texttt{af: none},
together with the quarantined assembly interface. The route is a route, not a proof.
Also absent: any numerical statement (those live in
\texttt{runs/}, and are never rigorous), and the earlier signed-geometry material, which
is off the live route and inventoried in \S\ref{sec:status-outlook}.
