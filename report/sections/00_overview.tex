\section{Roadmap and rigour status}\label{sec:overview}

This lab-book is the \emph{paper-track} of the project: it reproduces the af-validated (T0) results of
the registry in \texttt{argument/} together with a thin spine of open conjectures that orients them. It
is deliberately \emph{not} a mirror of the whole registry --- the non-rigorous results (inherited
\texttt{proved-mod-audit}, \texttt{conjecture}, \texttt{numerical}, \texttt{heuristic}) are anchored
once, honestly, in the status ledger of \S\ref{sec:status-ledger} and are never restated as theorems
here. The north star is \texttt{op-classical}; its status is \emph{open}, and its status-ledger anchor
is \ref{op:classical}. The definitions used below are the canonical shards \texttt{def-stochastic},
\texttt{def-almost-idempotent}, \texttt{def-near-positive-projection}, \texttt{def-signed-idempotent},
\texttt{def-negative-mass}, \texttt{def-height}, \texttt{def-visible-set}, \texttt{def-invisible-mass},
and \texttt{def-exposed}; this report references those identifiers and introduces no parallel
definitions.

The target itself is the classical (stochastic) stability statement: there are universal constants
$\eta_0,C>0$ such that every row-stochastic $Q$ with $\opnorm{Q^2-Q}\le\eta\le\eta_0$ admits a
stochastic idempotent $E$ with
\begin{equation}\label{eq:ov-target}
  \opnorm{Q-E}\;\le\;C\sqrt{\eta},
\end{equation}
with the exponent $\tfrac12$ sharp. This remains \textbf{open} in this repo.

The rigorous line is narrow by design. A result may be called rigorous here only when it is byte-matched
to a local theorem under \texttt{refs/}, validated in an \texttt{af} workspace, or Lean-proved. At the
time of this shard the registry records \textbf{29 af-validated results}. Those reproduced as full
sections of this report are the signed--stochastic bridge \ref{lem:classical-equiv}; the halo-collapse
family \ref{obs:height-collapse}, \ref{lem:mass-split}, \ref{lem:residual-lower},
\ref{lem:residual-upper}, \ref{conj:halo-collapse}, \ref{lem:parametric-halo-collapse},
\ref{lem:depth-d-halo-collapse}, \ref{lem:genuine-disintegration}, \ref{lem:top-concentration}; the
$(EX)$/factorization and fan-payment chain \ref{lem:factorization}, \ref{lem:zerosum-triangle},
\ref{lem:weighted-min}, \ref{lem:fan-payment}, \ref{lem:negpart-subadditive},
\ref{lem:fan-payment-restricted}; the argmin engine \ref{lem:pivot-removing-move}; the hiddenness
witnesses and their consequences \ref{lem:hiddenness-dual-witness}, \ref{lem:always-tight-dual-support},
\ref{lem:hiddenness-depth-markov}, \ref{lem:row-far-dual-certificate}, \ref{lem:top-slab-companion}; the
slab/capacity primitives \ref{lem:cs-low-slab-pincer}, \ref{lem:row-zero-capacity},
\ref{lem:harmonic-affine-bridge}; and the starvation obstruction
\ref{lem:starvation-completion-obstruction}. Every other registry result remains anchored only in the
status ledger, with its lower-rung status left intact.

The working headline is itself status-sensitive. The inherited campaign distinguishes the
realizable-family \emph{linear law} from the worst-case \emph{$\sqrt{}$-envelope}: along the realizable
family the tight relation between negative mass $\negmass$ and height $\height$ is
\[
  \negmass=\tfrac12\,\height\qquad\text{(linear)},
\]
while the quadratic form $\negmass\gtrsim\height^2$ is only the worst-case envelope, binding because
$\height\le O(\tsq)=O(\sqrt{\negmass})$. The $\sqrt{\eta}$ exponent of \eqref{eq:ov-target} is
nonetheless sharp, certified by the family \texttt{ex-hume} (anchored at \ref{ex:hume}), which is still
\texttt{proved-mod-audit} here rather than one of the rigorous results. Crossing between the signed and
stochastic pictures should cite the validated bridge \ref{lem:classical-equiv} rather than
re-normalising notation locally.
