\section{The compressed-unit norm estimate}\label{sec:compcb-compressed-unit-norm}

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-compcb-compressed-unit-norm}]\label{lem:compcb-compressed-unit-norm}
There are universal $C_{\mathrm{co}}<\infty$ and $\comb_{\mathrm{co}}>0$ such that, for
$\comb=\dproj+\ecs\le \comb_{\mathrm{co}}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cun-upper}
  \nrm{\cunit{T}}\;\le\;1+C_{\mathrm{co}}\,\comb
  \qquad\text{for every $\dproj$-projection }T,
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cun-two-sided}
  \bigl\lvert\,\nrm{\cunit{T}}-1\,\bigr\rvert\;\le\;C_{\mathrm{co}}\,\comb
  \qquad\text{for every \emph{nonvanishing} $T$.}
\end{equation}
\emph{Transcription note.} $\cunit{T}=\Cos{T}(T)$ is the compressed unit of
\texttt{def-compressed-corner}; ``nonvanishing'' is the second branch of the alternative
in \texttt{def-delta-projection}. The asymmetry is deliberate and load-bearing: the
two-sided bound is \emph{false} in general, since a $\dproj$-projection may fall in the
small branch $\nrm{T}=O(\dproj)$, in which case $\cunit{T}$ is small rather than close to
a unit.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Compressed-unit norm estimate: there are universal C_co < infinity and e_co > 0 such that, for e=delta+epsilon <= e_co, every delta-projection T satisfies ||u_T|| <= 1+C_co*e, and every nonvanishing T satisfies abs(||u_T||-1) <= C_co*e.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
This shard's tree is instructive twice over, because its first attempt was audited and
found unsound, and the audit --- not the estimate --- is what is validated in the early
nodes.

\emph{The blocked first attempt.} The originally registered inputs
(\texttt{def-extended-epsilon-cstar-algebra} together with
\texttt{def-compressed-corner}) yield only the $\dproj$-projection dichotomy, the identity
$\cunit{T}=\Cos{T}(T)$, and the comparison
$\nrm{\Cos{T}-L_TR_T}\le k\comb$. They do \emph{not} yield the quantitative
multiplication bound $\nrm{XY}\le(1+\ecs)\nrm{X}\nrm{Y}$ --- the extended definition says
only that each $\Amp{n}{\mathcal A}$ \emph{is} an $\ecs$-$C^*$-algebra, without supplying
that term's quantitative axioms --- nor any unit-norm axiom that would give
\eqref{eq:cun-two-sided} from ``$\Cnrs{T}$ is an $O(\comb)$-$C^*$-algebra with unit
$\cunit{T}$''. A closure audit node records this verdict explicitly and refuses the root:
without those axioms there is no sound bridge from the permitted premises to the
contract.

\emph{The repair.} The missing quantitative content was then registered in the workspace
as byte-matched externals --- the $\ecs$-$C^*$ norm axioms
(\texttt{epsilon-banach-cstar-norm-axioms}) and the nonvanishing alternative --- and each
$O(\cdot)$ in them unpacked into a named universal constant after shrinking the
threshold. That gives universal $a,b,k<\infty$ and $\comb_0>0$ such that for
$\comb\le \comb_0$: (i) every $\dproj$-projection $T$ satisfies $\nrm{T}\le a\dproj$ or is
nonvanishing; (ii) $\nrm{XY}\le(1+\ecs)\nrm{X}\nrm{Y}$; (iii) $\cunit{T}=\Cos{T}(T)$ with
$\nrm{\Cos{T}-L_TR_T}\le k\comb$; and (iv) nonvanishing $T$ obeys
$\lvert\nrm{T}-1\rvert\le b\comb$.

\emph{Small branch.} Suppose $\nrm{T}\le a\dproj$ and $\comb\le\min(\comb_0,1)$. Since
$(L_TR_T)(T)=T(T^2)$, two applications of (ii) give
$\nrm{(L_TR_T)(T)}\le(1+\ecs)^2\nrm{T}^3\le4a^3\comb^3\le4a^3\comb$, while (iii) gives
$\nrm{\cunit{T}-(L_TR_T)(T)}\le k\comb\nrm{T}\le ka\comb^2\le ka\comb$. Hence
$\nrm{\cunit{T}}\le D\comb$ with $D=4a^3+ka$, and in particular
$\nrm{\cunit{T}}\le1+D\comb$. Note that here the compressed unit is \emph{small}, which is
why no two-sided statement can hold.

\emph{Nonvanishing branch.} Shrink $\comb_0$ so that $\comb_0\le1$ and $b\comb_0\le1$;
then (iv) gives $\nrm{T}\le2$. The compressed unit is compared directly with $T$ rather
than through any unit axiom: bilinearity gives
$T(T^2)-T=T(T^2-T)+(T^2-T)$, so by (iii), (ii) and $\nrm{T^2-T}\le\dproj$,
\[
  \nrm{\cunit{T}-T}
  \le\nrm{\cunit{T}-(L_TR_T)(T)}+\nrm{T(T^2)-T}
  \le k\comb\nrm{T}+(1+\ecs)\nrm{T}\dproj+\dproj
  \le(2k+5)\comb .
\]
The reverse triangle inequality together with (iv) then gives
$\bigl\lvert\nrm{\cunit{T}}-1\bigr\rvert\le(2k+5+b)\comb$, hence also the upper bound.

\emph{Assembly.} With $\comb_{\mathrm{co}}$ the shrunken threshold and
$C_{\mathrm{co}}=\max\bigl(4a^3+ka,\;2k+5+b\bigr)$, the dichotomy routes every
$\dproj$-projection to one of the two branches: the first gives \eqref{eq:cun-upper}, the
second gives \eqref{eq:cun-upper} and \eqref{eq:cun-two-sided}. The final assembly node
states expressly that it uses neither the superseded audit node nor any compressed-unit
norm axiom.
