\section{MAIN-CB reset states and ledgers: invariant preservation, the constant and structural-domain ledgers, and reset output typing}\label{sec:maincb-reset-ledgers}

The four rows below isolate the bookkeeping around a MAIN-CB reset.  They first keep one
literal improvement iterate and its typing, then choose a single universal ledger for all
four raw-call forms, and finally place every call scale inside the structural domains.
The reset states, raw calls, and witness tuple are those of
Definitions~\ref{def:maincb-reset-state}, \ref{def:maincb-raw-call}, and
\ref{def:maincb-witness-ledger}; no existence or estimate is read into those data definitions.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-maincb-reset-invariant-preservation}]\label{lem:maincb-reset-invariant-preservation}
Fix the universal witnesses $e_{\rm it},K_{\rm disp},K_{\rm floor}$ of
Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-improvement-iteration} and
$\epsilon^{\rm cb}_{\max},\delta^{\rm cb}_{\max},c_0^0$ of
Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-error-improvement}.  There are universal
$0<C_{\rm unit}<\infty$ and $\epsilon_{\rm unit},\delta_{\rm unit},a_{\rm unit}>0$,
furnished by the third clause of \texttt{prop\_delta\_hominc} and its implicit quantifier
convention, with the following property.  If $D\ge1$, $W$ is a MAIN-CB witness ledger,
\[
 c_0^{\rm cb}\ge\max\{c_0^0,K_{\rm floor},C_{\rm unit}(K_{\rm floor}+1)\},
 \quad r_{\rm reset}\le\min\!\left\{\epsilon^{\rm cb}_{\max},\frac{\delta^{\rm cb}_{\max}}D,
 \frac{e_{\rm it}}{D+1},\epsilon_{\rm unit},\frac{\delta_{\rm unit}}{\max\{1,K_{\rm floor}\}},
 \frac{a_{\rm unit}}{(1+K_{\rm disp})D},\frac1{2(1+K_{\rm disp})D}\right\},
\]
and an explicit raw call at $0\le t\le r_{\rm reset}$ has target an extended
$\epsilon_R$-$C^*$-corner $A_R$, literal extended $Dt$-inclusion
$u_R:B_R\to A_R$, $\nrm{u_R(I_{B_R})-u_{A_R}}\le Dt$, and $\epsilon_R\le t$, then it
admits a map $v_R:B_R\to A_R$ with
\[
 d_R\le c_0^{\rm cb}\epsilon_R,
 \qquad \nrm{v_R(I_{B_R})-u_{A_R}}\le c_0^{\rm cb}\epsilon_R.
\]
The map $v_R$ is bijective whenever $u_R$ is, and the source, target corner, and
amplification form are unchanged.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{After first fixing the universal e_it,K_disp,K_floor witnesses of lem-maincb-improvement-iteration and epsilon_max^cb,delta_max^cb,c0^0 witnesses of lem-maincb-error-improvement, there are universal 0 < C_unit < infinity and epsilon_unit,delta_unit,a_unit > 0 furnished by the third clause of prop_delta_hominc and its implicit quantifier convention such that, for every D >= 1 and every def-maincb-witness-ledger datum W with W.c0_cb >= max{c0^0,K_floor,C_unit*(K_floor+1)} and W.r_reset <= min{epsilon_max^cb,delta_max^cb/D,e_it/(D+1),epsilon_unit,delta_unit/max{1,K_floor},a_unit/((1+K_disp)*D),[2*(1+K_disp)*D]^{-1}}, every explicit raw call into an extended epsilon_R-C*-corner A_R at scale 0 <= t <= W.r_reset whose literal map u_R:B_R->A_R is an extended D*t-inclusion and satisfies ||u_R(I_{B_R})-u_{A_R}|| <= D*t and epsilon_R <= t admits an error-improved map v_R:B_R->A_R satisfying d_R <= W.c0_cb*epsilon_R and ||v_R(I_{B_R})-u_{A_R}|| <= W.c0_cb*epsilon_R, preserving bijectivity when u_R is bijective and leaving the source, target corner, and amplification form unchanged.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
Choose $C_{\rm unit},\epsilon_{\rm unit},\delta_{\rm unit},a_{\rm unit}$ from the
registered near-unit clause, with $a_{\rm unit}$ strictly below its positive threshold.
For a raw call put $d=Dt$.  The bounds $\epsilon_R\le t$ and
$t\le e_{\rm it}/(D+1)$ give $d+\epsilon_R\le e_{\rm it}$, so
Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-improvement-iteration} produces one fixed map $v_R$ with
$(v_R)_n=I_n\otimes v_R$, displacement at most $K_{\rm disp}Dt$, and extended-inclusion
defect $K_{\rm floor}\epsilon_R$.  At level one the triangle inequality gives
\[
 \nrm{v_R(I_{B_R})-u_{A_R}}\le(1+K_{\rm disp})Dt\le a_{\rm unit}.
\]
The other radius bounds give $\epsilon_R\le\epsilon_{\rm unit}$ and
$K_{\rm floor}\epsilon_R\le\delta_{\rm unit}$.  Applying the near-unit clause to this
same map yields the unit error
$C_{\rm unit}(K_{\rm floor}+1)\epsilon_R\le c_0^{\rm cb}\epsilon_R$, while its recorded
defect is at most $K_{\rm floor}\epsilon_R\le c_0^{\rm cb}\epsilon_R$.
If $u_R$ is bijective, then
$\nrm{v_R(x)}\ge[1-(1+K_{\rm disp})Dt]\nrm{x}\ge\nrm{x}/2$; thus $v_R$ is injective,
and equality of the finite dimensions of $B_R$ and $A_R$ makes it surjective.
The proof deliberately retains the iteration output rather than substituting the separate
existential map of Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-error-improvement}; hence its displayed signature
and amplification identity preserve the source, target, and amplification form.
The quoted contract is the user-ratified two-defect repair, superseding its 2026-07-30 form.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-maincb-reset-constant-ledger}]\label{lem:maincb-reset-constant-ledger}
Fix, in the order stated in the registry contract below, the witnesses exported by the
iteration, error-improvement, reset-invariance, corner-envelope, corner-identification,
corner-equivalence, and four raw-call producer rows, and put
$D_*:=\max\{1,D_0,D_1,D_2,D_3\}$.  Then Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-witness-arithmetic}
supplies one universal MAIN-CB witness ledger $W$ such that
\[
\begin{gathered}
 c_0^{\rm cb}=c_0,\quad L\ge L^0,\quad K_1\ge K_1^0,\quad
 K_2\ge\max\{K_2^0,1,L,c_0^{\rm cb}L\},\quad K_3\ge\max\{K_3^0,1,L,c_0^{\rm cb}L\},\\
 e_{\rm env}\le e_{\rm env}^0,\quad e_1\le e_1^{\rm provider},\quad
 e_{s2}\le\min\{e_{s2}^0,e_2\},\quad e_{\rm cross}\le\min\{e_{\rm cross}^0,e_3\}.
\end{gathered}
\]
Under the respective producer hypotheses at base scale $0\le t\le W.r_{\rm reset}$ and target ambient defect at most $t$, let $u_0,u_1,u_2,u_3$ be the literal maps furnished respectively by \texttt{lem-maincb-initial-raw-inclusion}, \texttt{lem-maincb-stage1-call-envelope} with \texttt{lem-maincb-stage1-raw-refinement}, \texttt{lem-maincb-stage2-call-envelope} with \texttt{lem-maincb-stage2-raw-extension}, and \texttt{lem-maincb-stage3-call-envelope} with \texttt{lem-maincb-stage3-raw-merge}. Then $u_0:\mathbb C\to A$ and $u_1:\mathbb C^{m+1}\to A$ are extended $D_*t$-inclusions, while $u_2:M_{r+1}\to A_R$ and $u_3:B_U\oplus B_V\to A_R$ are extended $D_*t$-isomorphisms.  Their respective unit
errors are at most $D_*t$, so all four are eligible for
Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-reset-invariant-preservation}.  Every selected witness is universal
and independent of dimension, amplification, block data, class count, and stage index.
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The tree chooses $L=L^0$, $K_1=K_1^0$ and
$K_i=\max\{K_i^0,1,L^0,c_0L^0\}$ for $i=2,3$, together with
$e_{\rm env}=e_{\rm env}^0$, $e_{s2}=\min\{e_{s2}^0,e_2\}$ and
$e_{\rm cross}=\min\{e_{\rm cross}^0,e_3\}$.  Applying
Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-witness-arithmetic} records these exact choices in one $W$ and makes
$r_{\rm reset}$ no larger than every provider threshold and every radius required by
Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-reset-invariant-preservation} with $D=D_*$.  The initial scalar map
$u_0(\lambda)=\lambda I_A$ has zero unit error.  Lemmas~\ref{lem:maincb-stage1-call-envelope} and \ref{lem:maincb-stage1-raw-refinement} give $u_1$ with defect and unit error $D_1t$.  Monotonicity
(Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-extended-inclusion-monotone}) weakens both map bounds to $D_*t$.
For Stage 2 the scale is $t=K_2\epsilon$: Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-stage2-call-envelope} provides the closed EXT-CB
datum with $\epsilon_R=L\epsilon\le t$, and Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-stage2-raw-extension} supplies a chosen isomorphism
$v_+$; the composite record defines $u_2:=v_+$ rather than identifying opaque outputs.
Stage 3 similarly takes $t=K_3\epsilon$, obtains the amplified four-corner datum, applies Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-stage3-raw-merge} to the datum supplied by Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-stage3-call-envelope}, and defines its output to be $u_3$.  A verifier challenged this last invocation;
the repaired live tree unpacks ``the respective producer hypotheses'' to retain the same
fixed cross witnesses and the four required bijective level-one corner maps, without
inferring bijectivity from the call envelope.  Weakening $D_i t$ to $D_*t$ preserves the
two isomorphism tags and unit bounds.  All four calls now meet the common reset radius and
near-unit hypotheses, so Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-reset-invariant-preservation} applies.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-maincb-structural-domain-ledger}]\label{lem:maincb-structural-domain-ledger}
Fix particular universal $e_{\rm sim},e_{\rm full}>0$ furnished by
Lemmas~\ref{lem:maincb-corner-equivalence} and
\ref{lem:maincb-full-corner-identification}, and fix a witness ledger $W$ furnished by
Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-reset-constant-ledger} using those same witnesses.  If
$0\le\epsilon\le\epsilon_{\rm MAIN}$, then
\[
 \epsilon\le e_{\rm env},\qquad \epsilon\le\frac{e_1}{K_1},\qquad
 \epsilon\le\frac{e_{s2}}{K_2},\qquad \epsilon\le\frac{e_{\rm cross}}{K_3}.
\]
The scales $\epsilon$, $K_{\rm call}\epsilon$, $K_1\epsilon$, $K_2\epsilon$, and
$K_3\epsilon$ are all at most $K_{\rm call}\epsilon$, and
\[
 K_{\rm call}\epsilon\le r_{\rm reset},e_{\rm sim},e_{\rm full},
 \qquad L\epsilon\le K_{\rm call}\epsilon,
 \qquad c_0^{\rm cb}K_{\rm call}\epsilon\le\tfrac12.
\]
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{After first fixing a particular universal e_sim>0 witness furnished by lem-maincb-corner-equivalence and a particular universal e_full>0 witness furnished by lem-maincb-full-corner-identification, fix one def-maincb-witness-ledger datum W whose existence is furnished by lem-maincb-reset-constant-ledger instantiated with those same e_sim,e_full witnesses; then 0 <= epsilon <= W.epsilon_MAIN implies epsilon <= W.e_env, epsilon <= W.e1/W.K1, epsilon <= W.e_s2/W.K2, and epsilon <= W.e_cross/W.K3, while the global scalar scale epsilon, atomic scalar scale W.K_call*epsilon, and Stage-1, Stage-2, and Stage-3 scales W.K1*epsilon,W.K2*epsilon,W.K3*epsilon are all at most W.K_call*epsilon <= W.r_reset,e_sim,e_full; moreover W.L*epsilon <= W.K_call*epsilon and W.c0_cb*W.K_call*epsilon <= 1/2.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
For this same fixed $W,e_{\rm sim},e_{\rm full}$, the phrase ``supplied by
Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-witness-arithmetic}'' in the constant-ledger conclusion yields
positivity of every field and the exact formulas
\[
 K_{\rm call}=\max\{1,L+1,c_0^{\rm cb},K_1,K_2,K_3\},
\]
and $\epsilon_{\rm MAIN}$ as the minimum of
$e_{\rm env},e_1/K_1,e_{s2}/K_2,e_{\rm cross}/K_3,r_{\rm reset}/K_{\rm call},
e_{\rm sim}/K_{\rm call},e_{\rm full}/K_{\rm call}$ and
$[2\max\{1,c_0^{\rm cb}K_{\rm call}\}]^{-1}$.  The first four entries give the four
displayed domain bounds.  The maximum formula gives
$K_{\rm call}\ge1,K_1,K_2,K_3$, so multiplication by $\epsilon\ge0$ compares the global,
atomic, and stage scales.  The next three minimum entries, multiplied by positive
$K_{\rm call}$, place their common upper bound inside
$r_{\rm reset},e_{\rm sim},e_{\rm full}$.  Also $K_{\rm call}\ge L+1\ge L$.
Finally put $x=c_0^{\rm cb}K_{\rm call}$ and $M=\max\{1,x\}$; then
$\epsilon\le1/(2M)$ and $x\le M$ give $x\epsilon\le1/2$.
The live tree records that the root already fixes $W$ with the complete provider provenance;
it does not derive omitted witnesses from an abbreviated dependency list.  Challenges about
that scope and about using a pending aggregate node were repaired by the explicit scope
correction and by making the two scalar branches depend directly on the validated formula node.
The amended contract also carries the recorded-field repair; its suspension and re-flip likewise followed the Stage-3 dependency status, with the workspace preserved.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-maincb-reset-output-typing}]\label{lem:maincb-reset-output-typing}
Under the same fixed witnesses and radius hypotheses as
Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-reset-invariant-preservation}, every indicated raw call admits one
error-improved map $v_R:B_R\to A_R$ satisfying
\[
 d_R\le c_0^{\rm cb}\epsilon_R,\qquad
 \nrm{v_R(I_{B_R})-u_{A_R}}\le c_0^{\rm cb}\epsilon_R,
\]
which is an extended $c_0^{\rm cb}\epsilon_R$-inclusion, is an extended
$c_0^{\rm cb}\epsilon_R$-isomorphism when $u_R$ is bijective, and leaves the source,
target corner, and amplification form unchanged.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{After first fixing the universal e_it,K_disp,K_floor witnesses of lem-maincb-improvement-iteration and epsilon_max^cb,delta_max^cb,c0^0 witnesses of lem-maincb-error-improvement, there are universal 0 < C_unit < infinity and epsilon_unit,delta_unit,a_unit > 0 furnished by the third clause of prop_delta_hominc and its implicit quantifier convention such that, for every D >= 1 and every def-maincb-witness-ledger datum W with W.c0_cb >= max{c0^0,K_floor,C_unit*(K_floor+1)} and W.r_reset <= min{epsilon_max^cb,delta_max^cb/D,e_it/(D+1),epsilon_unit,delta_unit/max{1,K_floor},a_unit/((1+K_disp)*D),[2*(1+K_disp)*D]^{-1}}, every explicit raw call into an extended epsilon_R-C*-corner A_R at scale 0 <= t <= W.r_reset whose literal map u_R:B_R->A_R is an extended D*t-inclusion and satisfies ||u_R(I_{B_R})-u_{A_R}|| <= D*t and epsilon_R <= t admits an error-improved map v_R:B_R->A_R that satisfies d_R <= W.c0_cb*epsilon_R and ||v_R(I_{B_R})-u_{A_R}|| <= W.c0_cb*epsilon_R, is an extended W.c0_cb*epsilon_R-inclusion, is an extended W.c0_cb*epsilon_R-isomorphism when u_R is bijective, and leaves the source, target corner, and amplification form unchanged.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
Unpack the same near-unit clause and fix its four universal constants independently of all
algebras, maps, $D,W,t$.  For one raw call set $q=(1+K_{\rm disp})Dt$.  The ledger radius
gives every threshold needed by the iteration and near-unit steps, in particular
$q\le\min\{a_{\rm unit},1/2\}$.  Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-improvement-iteration} produces a
single dagger-preserving $v_R$ with amplifications $I_n\otimes v_R$, displacement at most
$K_{\rm disp}Dt$, and extended-inclusion defect $K_{\rm floor}\epsilon_R$; this same map is
retained also at $\epsilon_R=0$, where the provider specifies its operator-norm limit.
At the unit, the displacement plus the raw-call unit error is at most $q$, so the near-unit
clause gives the sharper bound
$C_{\rm unit}(K_{\rm floor}+1)\epsilon_R\le c_0^{\rm cb}\epsilon_R$.
Record $d_R=K_{\rm floor}\epsilon_R$.  Since
$K_{\rm floor}\epsilon_R\le c_0^{\rm cb}\epsilon_R$, monotonicity
(Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-extended-inclusion-monotone}) types this very map as the asserted
extended inclusion.  If $u_R$ is bijective, the lower estimate
$\nrm{v_R(x)}\ge(1-q)\nrm{x}\ge\nrm{x}/2$ and finite-dimensionality make $v_R$ bijective;
monotonicity preserves the resulting extended-isomorphism tag.  No separate existential
output of Lemma~\ref{lem:maincb-error-improvement} is substituted, so the literal source,
target corner, and amplification family remain unchanged.
This is the user-ratified standalone strengthened reset-output row supplied by the consumer repair.
