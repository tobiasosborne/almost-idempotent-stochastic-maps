\section{Exact adjointness of the amplified Ha map}\label{sec:hcb2-amplified-adjointness}

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-hcb2-amplified-adjointness}]\label{lem:hcb2-amplified-adjointness}
There is a universal $\comb_{\mathrm{adj}}>0$ such that every H-CB datum with
$\comb\le \comb_{\mathrm{adj}}$, every $n\ge1$ and every $Z\in\Amp{n}{\Cnr{P}{R}}$
satisfy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:adj-main}
  \Han{Q}{P}{R}{n}(Z)\dg\;=\;\Han{Q}{R}{P}{n}\bigl(Z\dg\bigr).
\end{equation}
\emph{Transcription note.} The datum is that of \texttt{def-hcb-datum} and
$\Ha{Q}{P}{R}$ is the map of \texttt{def-ha-map}; $\comb=\dproj+\ecs$. The two daggers in
\eqref{eq:adj-main} are different operations: on the left it is the Hilbert-space adjoint
of the operator $\Han{Q}{P}{R}{n}(Z)$ between amplified column spaces, on the right it is
the involution of the ambient algebra applied to $Z$. The identity is \emph{exact} --- no
$O(\comb)$ error --- and the threshold is needed only so that the sesquilinear forms
involved are genuine inner products.
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
\emph{Threshold.} Set $\comb_{\mathrm{adj}}=\min(\comb_{\mathrm{cmp}},\comb_{\mathrm{ip}})$,
where $\comb_{\mathrm{cmp}}$ comes from
\texttt{lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities} and $\comb_{\mathrm{ip}}$ is a
universal smallness threshold below which the Euclidean sesquilinear forms of
\texttt{def-ha-map} and of the column-Hilbert displays are genuine inner products --- such
a threshold exists because those forms agree with the norms up to a $1\pm O(\comb)$
factor. Fix an arbitrary datum, $n$ and $Z$.

\emph{Corner symmetries.} Applying
\texttt{lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities} at $n=1$: if
$a\in\Cnr{A}{B}=\Img\Co{A}{B}$ then $a=\Co{A}{B}(a)$, so
$a\dg=\Co{B}{A}(a\dg)$ and $a\dg\in\Cnr{B}{A}$; and since $Q\dg=Q$, the compressed unit
$\widetilde Q=\Cos{Q}(Q)$ is Hermitian.

\emph{Exact product reversal.} The pivotal algebraic fact is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:adj-reversal}
  (a\cpr b)\dg=b\dg\cpr a\dg
  \qquad (a\in\Cnr{A}{B},\ b\in\Cnr{B}{C}),
\end{equation}
and it is \emph{exact}. The proof is three substitutions: the compressed-product display
gives $a\cpr b=\Co{A}{C}(ab)$; the imported identities at $n=1$ give
$\Co{A}{C}(ab)\dg=\Co{C}{A}\bigl((ab)\dg\bigr)$; the involution axiom gives
$(ab)\dg=b\dg a\dg$; and a second use of the display, legitimate because the reversed
corner memberships were just established, gives
$\Co{C}{A}(b\dg a\dg)=b\dg\cpr a\dg$. No associativity estimate and no approximation
enter. The tree records a correction here worth one sentence: an earlier audit node
concluded that \eqref{eq:adj-reversal} was \emph{not} derivable, because the formula
$a\cpr b=\Co{A}{C}(ab)$ had not been registered as an allowed input in the workspace;
after the compressed-product display was registered, that negative conclusion --- and the
associated demand for further definition provisioning --- was explicitly withdrawn.

\emph{Level one.} Fix $z\in\Cnr{P}{R}$ and put $T=\Ha{Q}{P}{R}(z)$. The defining relation
of \texttt{def-ha-map} reads, for all $x\in\Cnr{R}{Q}$ and $y\in\Cnr{P}{Q}$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:adj-defining}
  (y\dg\cpr z)\cpr x+y\dg\cpr(z\cpr x)=2\,\ipo{y}{Tx}\,\widetilde Q .
\end{equation}
Apply $\dg$ to \eqref{eq:adj-defining}. On the left, \eqref{eq:adj-reversal} reverses each
compressed product, turning the two summands into
$x\dg\cpr(z\dg\cpr y)$ and $(x\dg\cpr z\dg)\cpr y$. On the right, $\widetilde Q$ is
Hermitian and the scalar is conjugated, and conjugate symmetry together with the
definition of the Hilbert-space adjoint gives
$\overline{\ipo{y}{Tx}}=\ipo{Tx}{y}=\ipo{x}{T\dg y}$. Thus for all $x,y$,
\[
  (x\dg\cpr z\dg)\cpr y+x\dg\cpr(z\dg\cpr y)=2\,\ipo{x}{T\dg y}\,\widetilde Q .
\]
This is precisely the defining relation of \texttt{def-ha-map} for the pair $(R,P)$, the
symbol $z\dg$, the input $y$ and the test vector $x$; and that relation determines its
solution uniquely. Hence $\Ha{Q}{R}{P}(z\dg)y=T\dg y$ for every $y$, i.e.
$\Ha{Q}{R}{P}(z\dg)=\Ha{Q}{P}{R}(z)\dg$.

\emph{Amplification.} Write $Z=[Z_{ij}]$. By the amplification convention of the
contract, $\Han{Q}{P}{R}{n}(Z)$ is the block operator $[\Ha{Q}{P}{R}(Z_{ij})]$ from the
$n$-fold Hilbert direct sum of $\Cnr{R}{Q}$ to that of $\Cnr{P}{Q}$; its Hilbert-space
adjoint therefore has $(i,j)$ block $\Ha{Q}{P}{R}(Z_{ji})\dg$. On the other side,
$(Z\dg)_{ij}=Z_{ji}\dg$, which lies in $\Cnr{R}{P}$ by the corner symmetry above, so the
$(i,j)$ block of $\Han{Q}{R}{P}{n}(Z\dg)$ is $\Ha{Q}{R}{P}(Z_{ji}\dg)$. The level-one
identity equates the two blockwise, and equality of all blocks is \eqref{eq:adj-main}.
Since $n$ and $Z$ were arbitrary, the lemma follows.
