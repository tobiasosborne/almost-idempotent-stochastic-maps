\section{The uniform rectangular compressed-product estimate}\label{sec:compcb-rectangular-product}

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-compcb-rectangular-product}]\label{lem:compcb-rectangular-product}
There are universal $C_{\mathrm{co}}<\infty$ and $\comb_{\mathrm{co}}>0$ such that, for
$\comb=\dproj+\ecs\le \comb_{\mathrm{co}}$, every compatible amplified rectangular pair
$A,B$ satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rp-main}
  \nrm{A\cpr B-AB}\;\le\;C_{\mathrm{co}}\,\comb\,\nrm{A}\,\nrm{B}.
\end{equation}
\emph{Transcription note.} The tree fixes ``compatible amplified rectangular pair'' as an
explicit abbreviation, and the quantifier in \eqref{eq:rp-main} ranges over exactly these
data and no others: an extended $\ecs$-$C^*$-algebra $\mathcal A$; an integer $n\ge1$;
$\dproj$-projections $P,Q,R$ in $\mathcal A$; the amplifications
$P_n=\ampel{n}{P}$, $Q_n=\ampel{n}{Q}$, $R_n=\ampel{n}{R}$; elements
$A\in\Cnr{P_n}{Q_n}$ and $B\in\Cnr{Q_n}{R_n}$; and the compressed product
$A\cpr B=\Co{P_n}{R_n}(AB)$ formed as in \texttt{def-compressed-corner} inside
$\Amp{n}{\mathcal A}$. In particular both factors live in one \emph{square}
amplification $\Amp{n}{\mathcal A}$ --- a restriction that later forces a separate
row/column statement (\S\ref{sec:compcb-row-column-product}).
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Uniform rectangular compressed-product estimate: there are universal C_co < infinity and e_co > 0 such that, for e=delta+epsilon <= e_co, every compatible amplified rectangular pair satisfies ||A dot B-AB|| <= C_co*e*||A||||B||.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The shape of the argument is: unpack a level-one universal estimate into named constants,
verify that the amplified data satisfy its hypotheses, and apply it inside
$\Amp{n}{\mathcal A}$. No new analysis is done at level $n$; the whole point is that none
is needed.

\emph{Unpacking the level-one estimate.} \texttt{def-compressed-corner} states, for any
triple $U,V,W$ of $\dproj$-projections in an $\ecs$-$C^*$-algebra, that
$X\cpr Y=\Co{U}{W}(XY)$ on $\Cnr{U}{V}\times\Cnr{V}{W}$ and that
$\nrm{X\cpr Y-XY}\le O(\dproj+\ecs)\nrm{X}\nrm{Y}$. The tree makes the big-O explicit:
there are constants $C_{\mathrm{corner}}<\infty$ and $\comb_{\mathrm{corner}}>0$,
independent of the algebra, of the projections and of the elements, such that the bound
reads $C_{\mathrm{corner}}\comb\nrm{X}\nrm{Y}$ whenever $\comb\le \comb_{\mathrm{corner}}$.
This independence is the reason the final constant is uniform in $n$: the level-one
estimate is a statement about \emph{every} $\ecs$-$C^*$-algebra, and $\Amp{n}{\mathcal A}$
is one of them.

\emph{The amplified data are admissible.} Because $\mathcal A$ is extended,
$\Amp{n}{\mathcal A}$ is an $\ecs$-$C^*$-algebra for every $n$; and $P_n$ is a
$\dproj$-projection whenever $P$ is, since $P_n\dg=P_n$,
$P_n^2-P_n=\ampel{n}{(P^2-P)}$ and the block-diagonal norm satisfies
$\nrm{\ampel{n}{Z}}=\nrm{Z}$ --- likewise for $Q_n,R_n$. Applying
\texttt{lem-compcb-amplified-compression} to the three pairs $(P,Q)$, $(Q,R)$, $(P,R)$
identifies the amplified corners with $\Amp{n}{\Cnr{P}{Q}}$, $\Amp{n}{\Cnr{Q}{R}}$,
$\Amp{n}{\Cnr{P}{R}}$ and the amplified compressions with the entrywise amplifications;
\texttt{lem-compcb-amplified-compression-identities} supplies the idempotence and dagger
identities for those maps. Hence a compatible amplified rectangular pair really is an
ordinary rectangular pair in $\Amp{n}{\mathcal A}$, and its amplified compressed product
is literally the ordinary compressed product there, $A\cpr B=\Co{P_n}{R_n}(AB)$, which
lands in $\Cnr{P_n}{R_n}$.

\emph{Assembly.} Take
\[
  C_{\mathrm{co}}=C_{\mathrm{corner}},\qquad
  \comb_{\mathrm{co}}=\min\bigl(\comb_{\mathrm{corner}},\comb_{\mathrm{cmp}}\bigr),
\]
with $\comb_{\mathrm{cmp}}$ the threshold of the amplification dependencies; both are
universal, finite and positive. For $\comb\le \comb_{\mathrm{co}}$ the level-one estimate
applies inside $\Amp{n}{\mathcal A}$ with $U=P_n$, $V=Q_n$, $W=R_n$, $X=A$, $Y=B$, and
gives \eqref{eq:rp-main} with constants independent of $n$ and of all the data.

restriction, not a formality: \S\ref{sec:compcb-row-column-product} exists precisely
because a row/column pair fails it.
