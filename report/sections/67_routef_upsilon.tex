\section{Route-F Upsilon rows: component construction, the left inverse, and the two closeness estimates}\label{sec:routef-upsilon}

This section reproduces the four \texttt{af}-validated rows that build the
$\Upsilon'$ branch from the raw-factor setting, prove its approximate left-inverse
property, compare it with $\widetilde\Upsilon$, and normalize it to a UCP map
$\Upsilon$.  The common ambient datum is that of
Definition~\ref{def:routef-raw-factor-setting}; UCP is used in the sense of
Definition~\ref{def:ucp-map}.
\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-routef-upsilon-prime-component-construction}]\label{lem:routef-upsilon-prime-component-construction}
Fix first one global package $W_{\mathrm{RF}}$ supplied by Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-raw-factor-setting-formation},
and for every admissible $(H,\Phi,\defect)$ fix over it a raw-factor datum $S$, writing
its fields without qualification.  For every $\Delta'$ supplied by Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-delta-prime-closeness}
and every $\Delta$ supplied from it by Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-delta-normalization-closeness},
with $C_N,C_R$ from (1.3), put
\[
 \rho_{\Upsilon'}=\min\{\rho_T,\rho_{\mathrm{id}},\rho_\Delta,
 \rho_2,\rho_3,(2C_R)^{-1}\}.
\]
If $0\le\defect\le\rho_{\Upsilon'}$, then there are $m\ge1$, nonzero finite-dimensional
$L_j,E_j$ $(1\le j\le m)$, a finite-dimensional $F$, operators
$W_j:H\to L_j\otimes E_j$, and an isometry $V:H\to H\otimes F$, such that
\[
 B=\bigoplus_{j=1}^{m}B(L_j),\quad \sum_jW_j^*W_j=I_H,\quad
 \Delta(X)=\sum_jW_j^*(X_j\otimes I_{E_j})W_j,\quad
 \Phi(T)=V^*(T\otimes I_F)V.
\]
For each $j$ there are a nonempty finite $\Sigma_j$, unitaries $U_{js}$, weights
$p_{js}\ge0$ summing to one, a positive contraction $C_j$ on $E_j$, and a unit
vector $\xi_j\in E_j$, with
\[
 \sum_s p_{js}(U_{js}^*\otimes I)Z(U_{js}\otimes I)
 =I_{L_j}\otimes\frac{\operatorname{Tr}_{L_j}(Z)}{\dim L_j},
 \qquad
 R_j:=\sum_s p_{js}(U_{js}^*\otimes I)W_jW_j^*(U_{js}\otimes I)
 =I_{L_j}\otimes C_j,
\]
where the partial trace is unnormalized, and $0\le C_j\le I_{E_j}$,
$\nrm{C_j}\ge1-C_R\defect$, and
$1-\langle\xi_j,C_j^*C_j\xi_j\rangle\le2C_R\defect$.
Writing $\widehat U_{js}$ for $U_{js}$ in block $j$ and zero elsewhere and
$\iota_{js}(z)=U_{js}z\otimes\xi_j$, the operators and maps
\[
 \Lambda_j:=\sum_s p_{js}(\Delta(\widehat U_{js}^*)\otimes I_F)VW_j^*\iota_{js},
 \quad \Upsilon'_j(T):=\Lambda_j^*(\Phi(T)\otimes I_F)\Lambda_j,
 \quad \Upsilon'(T):=(\Upsilon'_j(T))_{j=1}^{m}
\]
satisfy: every $\Lambda_j$ is a contraction, every $\Upsilon'_j$ and $\Upsilon'$ is CP,
and $\nrm{\Upsilon'}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le1$.
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The tree first applies finite-dimensional $C^*$-algebra structure and canonical Stinespring
dilation.  It writes $B$ as the displayed direct sum, decomposes the representation as
$L_j\otimes E_j$, and obtains the $W_j$ formula; the same matrix-unit construction for
$B(H)$ gives $F,V$ and the formula for $\Phi$.  For $d_j=\dim L_j$, it chooses the explicit
Weyl family $\Sigma_j=\{0,\ldots,d_j-1\}^2$, equal weights $d_j^{-2}$, and
$U_j(a,b)e_k=e^{2\pi iak/d_j}e_{k+b}$.  Summing in $a$ removes off-diagonal matrix units,
and summing in $b$ averages the diagonal ones, yielding the partial-trace twirl.  Applied
to $W_jW_j^*$, this gives $R_j=I_{L_j}\otimes C_j$ with $0\le C_j\le I$.

For the lower bound the tree identifies
$W_j^*R_jW_j=\sum_s p_{js}\Delta(\widehat U_{js}^*)\Delta(\widehat U_{js})$.  The raw-factor
norm and $\Delta$-normalization bounds give $\nrm{\Delta(e_j)}\ge1-C_N\defect$; degree two
and contractivity of $\Phi$ give $\nrm{C_j}\ge1-(C_N+C_2)\defect=1-C_R\defect$.
The cutoff $(2C_R)^{-1}$ makes this at least $1/2$, proving $E_j\ne0$ before choosing
$\xi_j$.  Norm attainment gives $\nrm{C_j\xi_j}=\nrm{C_j}$ and hence
$1-\nrm{C_j}^2\le2C_R\defect$.  Finally the summands defining $\Lambda_j$ are typed and
contractive, so $\nrm{\Lambda_j}\le1$.  Tensoring, compression, and direct sums preserve
CP; the amplified direct-sum norm is a maximum, so $\nrm{\Upsilon'}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le1$
without an $m$- or amplification-factor.  This is the construction factor introduced
after the original monolithic row exceeded the registry's brittleness cap.
\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-routef-upsilon-prime-left-inverse}]\label{lem:routef-upsilon-prime-left-inverse}
In the setting of Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-upsilon-prime-component-construction}, put
$C_L=C_2+C_3+2C_R$ and use the same $\rho_{\Upsilon'}$.  For every $q\ge1$,
$Y=(Y_1,\ldots,Y_m)\in M_q(B)$, and $j$,
\[
 \nrm{(\Upsilon'_j)_q(\Delta_q(Y))-Y_j}\le C_L\defect\nrm{Y}.
\]
Consequently $\nrm{(\Upsilon'\Delta-I_B)_q(Y)}\le C_L\defect\nrm{Y}$ and
$\nrm{\Upsilon'\Delta-I_B}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le C_L\defect$.
\end{lemma}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
Fix $q,Y,j$ and expand both occurrences of $\Lambda_j$ in $(\Upsilon'_j)_q(\Delta_q(Y))$.
The resulting double probability average contains
\[
 A_{st}=\Phi_q\!\left(\Delta_q(\widehat U_{js})
 \Phi_q(\Delta_q(Y))\Delta_q(\widehat U_{jt}^{*})\right).
\]
Degree two with an identity input replaces the middle $\Phi_q(\Delta_q(Y))$ by
$\Delta_q(Y)$ at cost $C_2\defect\nrm{Y}$.  Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-degree-three-estimate}
then replaces the three-factor expression by $\Delta_q(\widehat U_{js}Y\widehat U_{jt}^{*})$
at cost $C_3\defect\nrm{Y}$.  Contractive sandwiches and probability weights preserve
the sum of these errors.  Call the resulting double average $P_{jq}(Y)$.
Substituting the block formula for $\Delta_q$ and factoring the sums places the amplified
twirl $I_q\otimes R_j$ on both sides of $Y_j\otimes I_{E_j}$.  Since
$R_j=I_{L_j}\otimes C_j$, compression by $z\mapsto z\otimes\xi_j$ gives exactly
$P_{jq}(Y)=\alpha_jY_j$, $\alpha_j=\langle\xi_j,C_j^*C_j\xi_j\rangle$.
Thus $0\le1-\alpha_j\le2C_R\defect$ and
$\nrm{P_{jq}(Y)-Y_j}\le2C_R\defect\nrm{Y}$; the triangle inequality gives
$C_2+C_3+2C_R=C_L$.  Taking the maximum component norm in $M_q(B)$ and then the
supremum over $q,Y\ne0$ proves the cb bound without multiplicity loss.  The ledger records
two repaired scope challenges: $A_{st}$ was rebound locally rather than imported from a
pending sibling, and $P_{jq}(Y)$ was defined again in its own branch before fresh validation.
\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-routef-upsilon-prime-closeness}]\label{lem:routef-upsilon-prime-closeness}
With the same globally fixed $W_{\mathrm{RF}}$, admissible datum $S$, and maps
$\Delta',\Delta$, let $C_N,C_R,C_L,C_{\Upsilon'}$ be as in (1.3) and let
$\rho_{\Upsilon'}$ be as above.  If $0\le\defect\le\rho_{\Upsilon'}$, every Choi
multiplicity space in the component construction is nonzero, that construction
produces a completely positive $\Upsilon'$, and
\[
 \nrm{\Upsilon'-\widetilde\Upsilon}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le C_{\Upsilon'}\defect.
\]
\end{lemma}

\contractquote{After first fixing one global witness package W_RF supplied by lem-routef-raw-factor-setting-formation, for every input (H,Phi,eta) to which that formation result applies, fix one def-routef-raw-factor-setting datum S over that same W_RF supplied by the same result, for every Delta' supplied for (W_RF,S) by lem-routef-delta-prime-closeness, and every Delta supplied from that same Delta' by lem-routef-delta-normalization-closeness, writing the fields of (W_RF,S) as the unqualified symbols below: Upsilon-prime CP closeness: with C_N, C_R, C_L, C_Upsilon' from (1.3) and rho_Upsilon' := min{rho_T, rho_id, rho_Delta, rho_2, rho_3, (2*C_R)^(-1)}, for 0 <= eta <= rho_Upsilon', every Choi multiplicity space used below is nonzero and the componentwise construction produces CP Upsilon' with ||Upsilon' - tilde-Upsilon||_cb <= C_Upsilon'*eta.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-upsilon-prime-component-construction} supplies the nonzero multiplicity
spaces, CP $\Upsilon'$, and $\nrm{\Upsilon'}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le1$.  With
$K_j(T)=\Lambda_j^*(T\otimes I_F)\Lambda_j$ and $K=(K_j)_j$, the tree has
$\nrm{K}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le1$ and $\Upsilon'=K\Phi$.  Because
$\rho_{\Upsilon'}\le\rho_T\le1/8$, Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-functional-calculus-closeness}
applies, and
\[
 \Phi-\Phi\widetilde\Phi=(\Phi-\Phi^2)+\Phi(\Phi-\widetilde\Phi)
\]
gives $\nrm{\Upsilon'(I-\widetilde\Phi)}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le(1+C_\theta)\defect$.
The $\Delta$ estimate and Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-upsilon-prime-left-inverse} give
$\nrm{\Upsilon'\widetilde\Delta-I_B}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le(C_\Delta+C_L)\defect$,
while the raw norm bound gives $\nrm{\widetilde\Upsilon}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le2$.
The identity $\widetilde\Delta\widetilde\Upsilon=\widetilde\Phi$ yields
\[
 \Upsilon'-\widetilde\Upsilon
 =\Upsilon'(I-\widetilde\Phi)
 +(\Upsilon'\widetilde\Delta-I_B)\widetilde\Upsilon.
\]
The preceding estimates bound this by $[1+C_\theta+2C_\Delta+2C_L]\defect
=C_{\Upsilon'}\defect$, the ledger entry (1.3).  Factoring added the preceding two
dependencies while leaving this row's registry contract byte-unchanged.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-routef-upsilon-normalization-closeness}]\label{lem:routef-upsilon-normalization-closeness}
With the same fixed data and every $\Upsilon'$ supplied by
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-upsilon-prime-closeness}, put
\[
 C_\Upsilon=6C_T+7C_{\Upsilon'},\qquad
 \rho_\Upsilon=\min\{\rho_{\mathrm{unit}},\rho_{\Upsilon'},
 [2(C_T+C_{\Upsilon'})]^{-1}\}.
\]
For $0\le\defect\le\rho_\Upsilon$, $b=\Upsilon'(I)$ is invertible and, for every
$X\in B(H)$,
\[
 \Upsilon(X)=b^{-1/2}\Upsilon'(X)b^{-1/2}
\]
defines a UCP map satisfying
$\nrm{\Upsilon-\widetilde\Upsilon}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le C_\Upsilon\defect$.
\end{lemma}

\contractquote{After first fixing one global witness package W_RF supplied by lem-routef-raw-factor-setting-formation, for every input (H,Phi,eta) to which that formation result applies, fix one def-routef-raw-factor-setting datum S over that same W_RF supplied by the same result, for every Delta' supplied for (W_RF,S) by lem-routef-delta-prime-closeness, every Delta supplied from that same Delta' by lem-routef-delta-normalization-closeness, and every Upsilon' supplied from that same pair by lem-routef-upsilon-prime-closeness, and for every X in B(H), writing the fields of (W_RF,S) as the unqualified symbols below: Upsilon UCP normalization: with C_Upsilon := 6*C_T+7*C_Upsilon' and rho_Upsilon := min{rho_unit, rho_Upsilon', [2*(C_T+C_Upsilon')]^(-1)}, for 0 <= eta <= rho_Upsilon, b = Upsilon'(I) is invertible and Upsilon(X) = b^(-1/2)*Upsilon'(X)*b^(-1/2) is UCP with ||Upsilon - tilde-Upsilon||_cb <= C_Upsilon*eta.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
Set $d=(C_T+C_{\Upsilon'})\defect$.  The radius gives $0\le d\le1/2$, and
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-upsilon-prime-closeness} with the raw unit estimate gives
\[
 \nrm{b-I}\le\nrm{\Upsilon'(I)-\widetilde\Upsilon(I)}
 +\nrm{\widetilde\Upsilon(I)-I}\le d.
\]
Since $\Upsilon'$ is CP, $b$ is positive.  Its spectrum lies in
$[1-d,1+d]\subset[1/2,3/2]$, so $b$ is invertible and $c=b^{-1/2}$ satisfies
$\nrm{b},\nrm{c}\le3/2$.  On this interval,
$|t^{-1/2}-1|=|t-1|/[\sqrt t(1+\sqrt t)]$, so functional calculus gives
$\nrm{c-I}\le\nrm{b-I}\le d$.  Conjugation of $\Upsilon'$ by $c$ is CP at every
amplification, while $cbc=I$ makes $\Upsilon$ unital; hence it is UCP with cb norm one.
The rearrangement $\Upsilon'(Y)=b^{1/2}\Upsilon(Y)b^{1/2}$ gives
$\nrm{\Upsilon'}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le\nrm{b}\le3/2$.  Finally
\[
 \Upsilon-\Upsilon'=(c-I)\Upsilon'c+\Upsilon'(c-I)
\]
gives $\nrm{\Upsilon-\Upsilon'}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le(15/4)d\le6d$.  Adding the
$C_{\Upsilon'}\defect$ prime-closeness bound yields
$\nrm{\Upsilon-\widetilde\Upsilon}_{\mathrm{cb}}\le(6C_T+7C_{\Upsilon'})\defect$.
The ledger's sole challenge was a sibling-scope gap in the cb-norm-one step; the live
tree repairs it with a self-contained amplification argument before fresh validation.
The quoted contract already carries the earlier rescope amendment inserting the explicit
``for every $X\in B(H)$'' binder; its mathematical suffix was otherwise unchanged.
