\section{The F0 seam: a stochastic matrix as a unital completely positive map}\label{sec:routef-f0-seam}

Route F attacks \texttt{op-classical} with the approximate-$C^{*}$ machinery of the pinned
source, and that machinery speaks about unital completely positive maps of a matrix algebra,
not about stochastic matrices. The two lemmas of this section are the seam between the two
languages: the first says that the classical datum lifts to an admissible input for the
machinery, the second says that the lift costs nothing in the one quantity the machinery
consumes.

\paragraph{Notation for this section.} Fix $n\ge1$. Write $\Mat n$ for the complex
$n\times n$ matrices, $\mathbb C^{n}=\ell^{\infty}_{n}(\mathbb C)$ for the commutative
$C^{*}$-algebra of diagonals of $\Mat n$, $J:\mathbb C^{n}\to\Mat n$ for the diagonal
inclusion and $D:\Mat n\to\mathbb C^{n}$, $D([a_{ij}])=(a_{11},\dots,a_{nn})$, for diagonal
extraction. The real self-adjoint part of $\mathbb C^{n}$ is written $\linfd n$; a
row-stochastic $Q$ (\texttt{def-stochastic}, Definition~\ref{def:stochastic}) acts there,
and $Q_{\mathbb C}$ denotes its canonical complex-linear extension to $\mathbb C^{n}$.
``UCP'' is \texttt{def-ucp-map} (Definition~\ref{def:ucp-map}) throughout, and
$\nrm{\cdot}_{\mathrm{cb}}$ is the completely bounded norm, the supremum over matrix levels
$r\ge1$ of the norms of the amplifications $T_{r}=\mathrm{id}_{\Mat r}\otimes T$.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-routef-f0-ucp-lift}]\label{lem:routef-f0-ucp-lift}
With the notation above, let $Q$ be row-stochastic. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f0-phi}
  \Phi\;:=\;J\,Q_{\mathbb C}\,D\;:\;\Mat n\longrightarrow\Mat n
\end{equation}
is a unital completely positive map.
\emph{Transcription note.} The typing is the content: $D$ lands in the \emph{complex}
diagonal algebra $\mathbb C^{n}$, and it is $Q_{\mathbb C}$, not $Q$, that acts there. No
smallness hypothesis on $Q$ is assumed, and nothing is claimed about $\Phi$ beyond
unitality and complete positivity.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Route F F0 UCP lift: let n >= 1, let D: M_n -> C^n be diagonal extraction onto the complex diagonal algebra C^n = l_inf^n(C) and J: C^n -> M_n the diagonal inclusion, let Q: l_inf^n -> l_inf^n be row-stochastic, and let Q_C: C^n -> C^n be the canonical complex-linear extension of Q; then Phi := J Q_C D: M_n -> M_n is a unital completely positive map.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
The tree factors the claim through the three composed maps and then closes by composition.

\emph{The middle map.} By \texttt{def-stochastic} the matrix $Q$ has nonnegative entries
$q_{ij}$ and satisfies $Q\mathbf 1=\mathbf 1$. If $x=(x_{j})$ is positive in
$\mathbb C^{n}$ then every $x_{j}$ is a nonnegative real, so
$(Q_{\mathbb C}x)_{i}=\sum_{j}q_{ij}x_{j}\ge0$; and $Q_{\mathbb C}(\mathbf 1)=\mathbf 1$.
Thus $Q_{\mathbb C}$ is positive and unital, and since its domain $\mathbb C^{n}$ is a
commutative $C^{*}$-algebra it is automatically completely positive
(\texttt{def-ucp-map}); hence UCP.

\emph{The two diagonal maps.} $D$ is unital, and for $r\ge1$ and a positive block matrix
$X=[X_{ab}]\in\Mat r(\Mat n)$ the amplification $D_{r}$ returns the $n$-tuple of principal
compressions $\bigl[(X_{ab})_{ii}\bigr]_{a,b=1}^{r}$, $i=1,\dots,n$, each of which is
positive in $\Mat r$; so $D$ is CP, hence UCP. Dually $J$ is unital, and for $r\ge1$ a
positive element $(A_{1},\dots,A_{n})$ of $\Mat r(\mathbb C^{n})=\bigoplus_{i=1}^{n}\Mat r$
is sent by $J_{r}$, after the canonical permutation of tensor factors, to the positive
block-diagonal matrix $\mathrm{diag}(A_{1},\dots,A_{n})\in\Mat{rn}$; so $J$ is CP, hence
UCP.

\emph{Composition.} Compositions of UCP maps are UCP (\texttt{def-ucp-map}), and
\eqref{eq:f0-phi} is such a composition.

$\linfd n$, while the diagonal of a complex matrix algebra lies in $\mathbb C^{n}$. The
repair was an amendment of the contract to the text quoted above --- the diagonal algebra
made complex and $Q$ replaced by $Q_{\mathbb C}$ --- after which the tree was
verifier-accepted. The same defect family is repaired in
Lemma~\ref{lem:routef-f2-positive-unital-compression} below, where it was the cause of a
first, abandoned elevation run.

\begin{lemma}[\texttt{lem-routef-f0-defect-identity}]\label{lem:routef-f0-defect-identity}
With the notation above and $\Phi=J\,Q_{\mathbb C}\,D$ as in \eqref{eq:f0-phi},
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f0-defect}
  \nrm{\Phi^{2}-\Phi}_{\mathrm{cb}}\;=\;\opnorm{Q^{2}-Q}.
\end{equation}
\emph{Transcription note.} This is an \emph{equality}, not a two-sided estimate with
constants: the seam neither loses nor gains defect, and no $\defect\mapsto\ecs$ conversion
happens here. The left side is a completely bounded norm on $\Mat n$, the right side the
$\ell^{\infty}$ map norm of \texttt{def-almost-idempotent}
(Definition~\ref{def:almost-idempotent}); the identity is what licenses reading one as the
other.
\end{lemma}

\noindent\textbf{Registry contract (verbatim).}
\contractquote{Route F F0 defect identity: let n >= 1, let D: M_n -> C^n be diagonal extraction onto the complex diagonal algebra C^n = l_inf^n(C) and J: C^n -> M_n the diagonal inclusion, let Q: l_inf^n -> l_inf^n be row-stochastic with canonical complex-linear extension Q_C: C^n -> C^n, and put Phi := J Q_C D; then ||Phi^2 - Phi||_cb = ||Q^2 - Q||_{infinity->infinity}.}

\paragraph{Proof (prose account of the af-validated tree).}
Put $L:=Q_{\mathbb C}^{2}-Q_{\mathbb C}$. The proof is an algebraic reduction, then two
matched inequalities at every matrix level, then a single scalar computation.

\emph{Reduction.} For $x\in\mathbb C^{n}$ the matrix $J(x)$ is diagonal with diagonal $x$,
and $D$ extracts that diagonal, so $DJ=I_{\mathbb C^{n}}$. Hence
\begin{equation}\label{eq:f0-JLD}
  \Phi^{2}-\Phi\;=\;J\,Q_{\mathbb C}(DJ)Q_{\mathbb C}\,D-J\,Q_{\mathbb C}\,D
  \;=\;J\bigl(Q_{\mathbb C}^{2}-Q_{\mathbb C}\bigr)D\;=\;JLD .
\end{equation}

\emph{The matrix levels.} Fix $r\ge1$ and identify
$\Mat r(\mathbb C^{n})=\bigoplus_{j=1}^{n}\Mat r$ with the norm $\max_{j}\nrm{X_{j}}$.
After the canonical permutation of tensor factors $J_{r}(X)=\mathrm{diag}(X_{1},\dots,X_{n})$,
so $\nrm{J_{r}X}=\max_{j}\nrm{X_{j}}$ and $J_{r}$ is isometric; $D_{r}$ returns the tuple
of diagonal $n\times n$ blocks, each a compression, so $D_{r}$ is contractive; and
$D_{r}J_{r}=I$. Submultiplicativity therefore gives
$\nrm{J_{r}L_{r}D_{r}}\le\nrm{L_{r}}$, while testing on inputs of the form $J_{r}X$ gives
$\nrm{J_{r}L_{r}D_{r}(J_{r}X)}=\nrm{J_{r}L_{r}X}=\nrm{L_{r}X}$ and hence
$\nrm{J_{r}L_{r}D_{r}}\ge\nrm{L_{r}}$. So $\nrm{(\Phi^{2}-\Phi)_{r}}=\nrm{L_{r}}$ for
every $r$, by \eqref{eq:f0-JLD}.

\emph{The scalar computation.} Let $(l_{ij})$ be the coefficient matrix of $L$, which is
the real matrix of $Q^{2}-Q$. For $X=(X_{j})\in\Mat r(\mathbb C^{n})$ one has
$(L_{r}X)_{i}=\sum_{j}l_{ij}X_{j}$, so
$\nrm{L_{r}X}\le\bigl(\max_{i}\sum_{j}\lvert l_{ij}\rvert\bigr)\max_{j}\nrm{X_{j}}$.
Conversely pick $i_{0}$ attaining the maximal row sum and take
$X_{j}=\sgn(l_{i_{0}j})I_{r}$ (with $\sgn 0=0$): then $\max_{j}\nrm{X_{j}}\le1$ and the
$i_{0}$-component of $L_{r}X$ is $\bigl(\sum_{j}\lvert l_{i_{0}j}\rvert\bigr)I_{r}$. Hence
$\nrm{L_{r}}=\max_{i}\sum_{j}\lvert l_{ij}\rvert$ for \emph{every} $r$, and that row
maximum is $\opnorm{Q^{2}-Q}$. Taking the supremum over $r$, which is the definition of the
cb norm, gives \eqref{eq:f0-defect}.
